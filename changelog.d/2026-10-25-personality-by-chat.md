- **You can now make a personality by talking to Jarvis (2026-10-25).** Settings -> "How Jarvis talks" already let you type a name, one line and one example; what was missing was the conversation. Say **"make me a personality like a grumpy but caring mechanic"** (typed or spoken, desktop or phone) and the PC answers in chat: it asks the LOCAL model once to draft a name, how it should sound, one example exchange and which of the nine built-in presets it starts from, then shows the whole draft and says `"save it"` keeps it. Refine it in your own words - "make it shorter", "call it Old Timer", "base it on the butler" - and say `"save it"`; the row is saved through the SAME `jarvis_manner.save_user_personality()` path the Settings form uses (name 60, line 240, each example half 240, 20 saved at most), so `"switch to <name>"` finds it straight away. `"forget it"` drops a draft; a temporary chat is refused because it remembers nothing. Wording only: no new route, no approval card, no permission change, and the model is only ever the local one - the draft is fields the PC validates, never a finished prompt, so a personality cannot change what Jarvis may do.
