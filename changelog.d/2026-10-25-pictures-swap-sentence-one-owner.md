PR #818 stopped the desktop panel printing both, but the answer still carried two copies and the phone's own `Pictures.madeWords` joins them, so the phone printed the sentence twice too. The backend now sends it once: a drawn picture says it in `words`, a failed draw says it in `error`, and `card` keeps only the machine-readable times and has no `words` key; the desktop panel's `words || card.words` guard is unchanged for a PC that has not taken this update. The desktop, the phone and the Rust boundary all show the owner the sentence exactly once, with no Kotlin change, and the new tests count the sentence in the whole answer (a phrase, not the word "words") on a drawn picture, a failed draw and a chat that did not come back - so a second copy in any field fails them.
