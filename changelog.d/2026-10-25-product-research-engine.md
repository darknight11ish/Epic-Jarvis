- **The product check is built offline, in two halves (2026-10-26).** The engine (`backend/jarvis_product_research.py`) takes review records - rating, date, verified purchase, Vine label, title, body, helpful votes, variant, every field optional - and returns a reasons-based report: the "what Amazon chose to show, not all of the reviews" caveat; eight evidence-only signals with counts, examples and thresholds (verified share, Vine share, incentive wording, own shingle/MinHash near-duplicate wording with possible/shortlisted/compared pair counts, date burstiness whose flags actually use the stated listing-or-sample baseline, rating-histogram shape, stylometry, review-versus-product mismatch); dates calendar-validated so an impossible date is unknown instead of crashing the report; what buyers agree, complain and are mixed about from the local model's aspect reading, with every quote accepted only when it is at least 8 characters and a real substring of ONE review's title OR body (invented, short and boundary-crossing quotes dropped and counted); a "set aside, and why" list that removes nothing; body-only `with_text`; "not enough trustworthy data" as a first-class outcome; and what could not be checked. The read half (`backend/jarvis_product_read.py`) turns one page's supplied HTML into the listing facts and review records in that same schema, with honest warnings for a challenge/sign-in page, not-a-product page, missing listing fields, no reviews, a truncated page or review block and missing review fields, plus `assemble_report()` which returns the engine report with the records for debugging; `capture_with_owner_browser()` is the named seam and raises a documented `CaptureNeedsOwnerBrowser` instead of pretending to fetch. Deliberately no per-review fake/real label and no numeric trust score, no route/tool/card/Settings/app surface, no network or storage, standard library only, nothing imports either module yet. Tests: `backend/test_product_research.py` (118 checks) and `backend/test_product_read.py` (67 checks), synthetic fixtures only, network spies and AST source scans that would fail on a forbidden import or call. **Honest limits:** all evidence is synthetic - no real Amazon page or review sample has been parsed; the thresholds are readable rules, not a calibrated classifier; the capture seam is a placeholder until the owner's signed-in browser session is wired; the sample is Amazon's curated list, not all reviews.
