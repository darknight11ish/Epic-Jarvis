- **The offline engine for a reasons-based product check is built (2026-10-25).** It takes review records somebody extracted from a product page (rating, date, verified purchase, Vine label, title, text, helpful votes, variant - every field optional) and returns a report of what is actually observable: the sample caveat ("what Amazon chose to show, not all of the reviews"), eight evidence signals with counts, examples and thresholds (verified share, Vine share, incentive wording, own shingle/MinHash near-duplicate wording, date burstiness against the listing baseline, rating-histogram shape, stylometry, review-vs-product mismatch), what buyers agree and complain about from the local model's aspect reading with every quote validated as a real substring (invented ones dropped and counted), every review a rule set aside with its reasons, "not enough trustworthy data" as a first-class outcome, and what could not be checked. There is deliberately no per-review fake/real verdict and no numeric trust score. Nothing is wired to a route, tool, card or app surface, it is standard library only and it never opens a network connection or runs anything. Tests: `backend/test_product_research.py` (97 checks, synthetic fixtures only, a network spy, quote-validator bites and the caveat words). **Honest limits:** there is no page reading yet, so no real review sample has been run through it; the thresholds are conservative rules, not a calibrated classifier, and the sample is Amazon's curated list, not all reviews.
