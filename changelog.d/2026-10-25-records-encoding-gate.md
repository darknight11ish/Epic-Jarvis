- **The record files are now proven to be real UTF-8, not just bytes that happen to decode.** A new suite, `backend/test_records_encoding.py`, fails if `docs/CLAIMS.tsv`, `docs/DECISIONS.md`, `docs/defect-register.tsv`, `docs/DEFECT-REGISTER.md`, `docs/ABOUT-JARVIS.md` or `README.md` stops decoding as strict UTF-8 or carries a double-decoded character (a lone U+00C3, a lone U+00C2, U+00E2 U+20AC, or U+FFFD). That is the class that put a garbled section sign into one `docs/CLAIMS.tsv` row on 2026-10-25 (fixed by #816): mojibake is *valid* UTF-8, so every count checker stays green while the text is wrong, and only a reader noticed. The suite also proves its own detector against the incident's own bytes, so a pattern that silently stopped matching cannot pass as green. `changelog.d/` is deliberately not scanned: one fragment quotes the bad bytes on purpose, as the record of the bug.
