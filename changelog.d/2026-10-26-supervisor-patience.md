- **The desktop's watchdog now waits up to about three and a half minutes before calling the backend hung, and its "stop after 3 restarts" cap works again (2026-10-26).** It used to allow 45 seconds (3 failed checks, 15 seconds apart) and then restart the backend, and on 2026-10-26 that cut off a real answer: "tell me about the Galaxy" ran 37-38 seconds and was restarted mid-turn. Now 10 checks have to fail in a row, and one probe gets 5 seconds instead of 1.5 because a cold first probe costs about 3.0 seconds while `hello()` builds the voice sessions. The wait is not simply 10 x 15 s: a backend that is listening but silent makes each failed check wait out the whole probe, so the honest worst case is 10 x (15 s + 5 s) = 200 seconds - which is what the app's own settings screen and `docs/INSTALL.md` now say, instead of the "two and a half minutes" they said before. The restart window is 15 minutes, not 10, for the same reason: three slow hangs are noticed about 200 seconds apart, so a 10-minute window could never hold all three - the fourth attempt would arrive after the first had aged out, the cap would never trip, and the app would restart a wedged backend every three and a half minutes for ever without ever telling the owner. The 15-second check interval and the Windows service are unchanged. Two Rust tests pin the arithmetic and the window-versus-cycle relationship, so raising the patience again cannot silently break the cap.
