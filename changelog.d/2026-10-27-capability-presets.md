- **Nine focus presets, four of them new capabilities, and Fast moves to `qwen3.5:4b` (2026-10-27).** The Presets menu now offers nine local presets: the original five plus **Reflective Coach** (talking a difficult thought through), **Pictures & Screens** (a screenshot or picture), **Documents & Notes** (long reading and study) and **Browser & Forms** (long pages and multi-step form work). All four capability rows run `qwen3.5:9b` on the RTX 2060 12 GB - Reflective Coach, Documents & Notes and Browser & Forms at 196,608 tokens (the measured General setup: 44.9 tokens a second, 9.17 GB of video memory, no spill) and Pictures & Screens at 32,768 - while Fast runs the small `qwen3.5:4b` on the RTX 2080 SUPER at 16,384 tokens, measured at 77.1 tokens a second and 4.15 GB at a 65,536-token window. The split is deliberate: only the 12 GB card holds the 196,608-token window, and the 8 GB display card keeps its memory for Windows and the desktop while Fast runs there; Coding's 30B model uses both cards only while it is applied. An apply that needs a different model raises ONE approval card (if that model is already the one loaded, the choice is saved and nothing is approved), and nothing switches by itself - no per-feature automatic model switching is added. Reflective Coach is a **model choice only**: not a therapist, with no store, no route and no network, and the matching personality wording is not part of this change.
