- **A rushed approval card can no longer be swipe-approved because its reason was cut short (2026-10-27).** `jarvis_gate.check()` stored the rush latch's chip as `json.dumps(raised)[:1000]`, and that chip is a couple of kilobytes once `json.dumps` escapes non-English text: the real bounds are `quote[:80]`, `context[:200]` and `source[:120]` (`jarvis_content_risk.py:319`, `:217-219`), and every non-ASCII character becomes six characters of `\uXXXX`. A chip cut at 1,000 is no longer JSON, so `pending()`'s `json.loads` threw and its `except` served `raised: null` - and BOTH apps read that as "not raised" (the phone's `swipeable = risk.swipeOk && raised == null`, the desktop's `if (approval.raised) return false`), so the one card the latch had raised arrived looking ordinary with its swipe back on. `jarvis-backend/jarvis_gate.py:1803` now stores the chip whole (the column is `TEXT` and `chip_from()` already bounds every value, so there is nothing to trim); the separate 4,000-character `detail` cut is deliberately untouched, because that is a different fault (a cut-off request the phone has no guard against) and changing it here would alter a second behaviour. The published base's three companions are kept in step with it, because the repository's own suites reverse the patch stack out of that file: `backend/gate-touches.patch` (its added side now carries the same line, so it still reverses out and re-applies - `test_gate_touches`, `test_approval_guards`), the deploy block `tools/hud-deltas/gate-digest-insert-new.txt` (must be the base's own text, byte for byte - `test_reconcile_live_backend`), and `backend/patch-history/gate-touches/` with the previous patch text. Measured through the real `check()` with a real `jarvis_content_risk` latch: a 2,068-character chip was stored as 1,000 characters, `JSONDecodeError: Invalid \uXXXX escape`, `raised: None`, `risk.swipe_ok: True`, card swipeable; after the change the same card stores 2,106 characters, parses, and is not swipeable. Proved by `backend/test_gate_rush_chip.py` (19 checks, 12 of which fail on the old line), including a control that an unraised card of the same action keeps its swipe, and a check that `detail` is still cut at 4,000. **Honest limits:** `jarvis-backend/jarvis_gate.py` has no twin in `backend/`, so the two-file rule does not apply to it; `jarvis-backend/` is the published base and a running PC is not updated by committing this, so this fixes the repository's gate copy and does NOT claim the owner's live backend is already fixed - that needs a `tools/reconcile_live_backend.py` delta of this region (the existing digest deltas are already applied there and are skipped) or a base re-take, measured separately. Nothing on the phone was touched, and the phone's rule is applied here as `ApiModels.kt` declares it, not compiled.
