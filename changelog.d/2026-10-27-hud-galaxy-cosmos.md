- **The Galaxy opens on the Planetary Orrery, and all eight of its looks are drawn in 3D (2026-10-27).** The owner: *"none of the galaxy on the current jarvis hud look like the one that gemini made"* — he chose the exact 3D port and Planetary Orrery as the default look. The HUD's Galaxy now renders the Gemini page's look through one three.js scene: its eight coordinate systems at its own radii and wave amplitudes, its palettes and fog (density 0.0016, ACES exposure 1.25), its camera positions and look-ats, its guide bodies (three ecliptic rings, three hexagonal cortical plates with six apical trunks, twelve forked twigs and six basal roots, four nebula wells inside a 3,200-point cloud, a 72-step double helix with 37 base-pair rungs and a laser disk that rides the time axis, a radar floor with three zone rings, four crosshairs, a rotating sweep and a containment dome, four tree baselines, one zen halo), its 1,800-star sky and its five animation branches. It draws the **real** graph — never the page's sample facts — at the HUD's own 120/240-node and 18/36-label budgets, with every name still a real `<button>` and its arrow/Home/End walking projected from the 3D camera. The new module is `galaxy-cosmos.js` (`jarvis-desktop/src/`, served from `backend/static/` and carried by the published base like the other page modules); the HUD page itself changes only at the mount point, in its three byte-identical copies. The Galaxy opens on the Planetary Orrery, chosen once and remembered under the existing look key, and the other seven remain in the same Look control. Reduced motion, a browser without WebGL, and a page served without three.js all fall back to the **flat layout of the same look**, so no machine loses a look; the 3D engine stops drawing the moment the Galaxy leaves the screen. The three fields `/api/graph` does not send are derived rather than invented (nebula wells by group, helix order by any date in `meta`, radar rings by kind of thing), and the Radar dome's rings are not a claim about safety. three.js itself is vendored by its own pull request, with its licence in the notices.
