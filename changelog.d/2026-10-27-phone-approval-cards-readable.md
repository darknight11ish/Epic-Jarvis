- **Every approval card waiting on the PC is readable on the phone again (2026-10-27).** The PC sends `replaces_kind: null` on any card that replaces nothing (`jarvis_gate.pending()` sets it from `replaces_words_for(...)`, which returns nothing for an ordinary card), and the phone declared that field non-nullable while its JSON reader does not coerce nulls - so the explicit null made the whole row fail to decode and the phone showed only "N waiting approval could not be read on this phone" with an empty queue, for every ordinary card (picture mode's card was the one seen on 2026-10-27). The three replaces fields are now normalised before decoding, so ordinary cards read exactly as the HUD shows them; no server wire change, no new route, no write. A pin test decodes a real gate-shaped row with those nulls; the phone's own look at a real card remains a field-test row, not a pass.
