- **A card stays readable even when one of its fields arrives null (2026-10-27).** The phone now settles every declared field of a waiting card's `risk`, `notice`, `raised`, `options` and `replaces` before decoding it, because a single explicit null inside any of them would otherwise throw the whole row out of the queue exactly as `replaces_kind` did. Unreadable values settle toward caution, never convenience: an unreadable `replaces` keeps the two-press ask, an unknown reversibility/reach is read as the worse one, an unreadable weight makes the card heavy (slower Approve), Deny stays allowed, Approve never switches on by itself, and a raise that was sent still refuses every quick gesture. A null-bomb test pins a row where every one of those fields is null and asserts it is read with those values; no server change, no new route, no write.
