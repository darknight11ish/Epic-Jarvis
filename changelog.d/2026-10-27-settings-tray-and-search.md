- **The tray's "Update available" row opens the Updates card itself, and Settings no longer calls any of its settings "Everyday" or "Rare" (2026-10-27).** Right-clicking the tray icon and choosing the update row used to open Settings at the top, so the version, the release notes and Install were several screens down; it now opens Settings at its `#updates` card - a new window is built at `settings.html#updates`, an already-open one hears a `settings-place` event, and either way the card is scrolled to with its first control focused. App lock is still asked before any of it, and the tray still never checks, downloads or installs anything. The page's Everyday/Rare bands are gone, with their two jump-list labels and headings - every card, id, anchor and jump link is unchanged, and the read-only "What Jarvis does" group stays as the one heading. Settings search now orders what it finds by where the words matched: a card's own title or a name the menus catalogue already answers to for it (exact, then prefix, then a typo), then its labelled rows, then its detail text; ties go to the shorter title, then the title itself, never to the section or the page's order. Typing "update" now puts Updates first instead of fifth behind cards that merely mention updating, and "update" joined the Updates card's spoken names in `jarvis_menus.py`, so "open update" names it too. Limits: this is desktop only - the phone has its own Settings search and its own update row - and the scroll/focus proof is interaction evidence from a local headless Chromium, not a claim about a packaged build or about what the owner sees until he opens it himself.
