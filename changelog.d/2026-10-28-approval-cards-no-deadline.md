- **An approval card no longer has a deadline (2026-10-28).** The owner ruled: "if there doesn't need to be a time limit don't have one." A routine card used to refuse itself after fifteen minutes and vanish from every screen; it now stays, with its Approve and Deny and no countdown, until it is answered or the PC withdraws the work behind it. The request that asked still stops waiting on the shipped `approval_timeout_seconds`, and that moment is recorded on the row: an Approve after it is refused in one plain sentence ("…the request that asked has finished, so nothing can be approved from it. Nothing was changed…") and the card is taken off the list then - never silently expired, never approved into nothing. A Deny always works, including on an old card. The four ceremonies of the 2026-10-23 ruling (pairing a device, an approval key, a restore) keep waiting for as long as the owner needs. Nothing is auto-approved, no client clears anything, and the Windows Hello / signed-approval receipt is unchanged. **Honest limits:** a backend restart does not keep a card whose request died with it, and a phone renders a 409 with its own plain sentence rather than the PC's exact one (the desktop HUD shows the PC's). Proven by `backend/test_gate_no_deadline.py`; built in `backend/no-deadline-cards.patch` and `jarvis_owner_check.py`.
