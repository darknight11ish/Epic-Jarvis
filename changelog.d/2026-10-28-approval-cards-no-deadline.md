- **An approval card no longer shows a countdown, and a finished request's card leaves the queue (2026-10-28).** The owner ruled: "if there doesn't need to be a time limit don't have one." A routine card used to tick down fifteen minutes and refuse itself; it now shows Approve and Deny with no clock while its request is genuinely waiting. The request that asked still stops waiting on the shipped `approval_timeout_seconds`, and that moment is recorded on the row: once it plus a 60-second grace has passed, the next queue read takes the card off (a lapse, never a decision), so you are never shown an Approve that can only answer 409. Inside that grace a tap is refused in one plain sentence ("…the request that asked has finished, so nothing can be approved from it. Nothing was changed…") and the card is withdrawn. A Deny always works. The four ceremonies of the 2026-10-23 ruling (pairing a device, an approval key, a restore) have no such moment and are answerable for as long as you need. Nothing is auto-approved, no client clears anything, and the Windows Hello / signed-approval receipt is unchanged. **Honest limits:** a tap in the grace window is the race the 409 exists for, and a backend restart does not keep a card whose request died with it. Proven by `backend/test_gate_no_deadline.py`; built in `backend/no-deadline-cards.patch` and `jarvis_owner_check.py`.
