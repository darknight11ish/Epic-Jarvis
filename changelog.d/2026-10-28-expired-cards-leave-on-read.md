- **Approval cards that have timed out leave the phone's list by themselves (2026-10-28).** The owner asked "there is approval cards that are expired, how do I clear them easily from my android phone", and the phone was telling the truth: on his OnePlus (`f0a5b32b`, app 0.2.3086) four cards sat under "4 WAITING ON YOU" with "Nothing runs until you decide.", each one saying "Timed out — ask the desktop to raise this again." and drawing no Approve and no Deny, with nothing on the screen to remove them. The cause was one line: the gate's sweep that marks an overdue row `expired` ran inside `_init()`, which is **once per process** (`if _inited: return`), so a card whose waiting thread died with an earlier process — a backend restart, a killed backend, an abandoned request — was not old enough to be swept by the next start-up if it was raised inside the last timeout-plus-60-seconds window, and after that nothing ever swept it. It stayed `pending` for the life of that process, so `/api/pending` served it, every app drew it as an unanswerable "waiting on you" card, and a later identical request was told "the same request is already waiting for you" about a card nobody was waiting on. The sweep is now one helper, `jarvis_gate._expire_overdue()`, that `pending()` runs before it builds the list, so the queue a client is handed holds only rows still inside their own window. **No new control and no new route:** clearing an expired card would approve nothing, but it would also be a second mechanism for a fact the read already knows, and the owner should not have to press anything to get rid of a card he cannot answer. **It is a lapse, not a decision** — the state written is `expired`, never `denied`, and nothing is approved; the row still shows as `expired` in `history` and both apps' Activity lists. **It cannot take a live card:** the deadline is recomputed on the PC at the moment of the read and no client sends an id, so a stale phone page has nothing to ask with. The four cards that have no deadline (pairing, an approval key, a restore) are never touched, and the 60-second grace the start-up sweep always gave is kept. **The phone's only added half is one line** in `MainActivity.onResume` that re-reads `/api/pending` when the app comes to the front, because the list on screen is a copy and nothing told it to re-read — that is what makes "open Jarvis" the whole answer instead of only a cold start. Tests: `backend/test_pending_expiry_on_read.py` (8 tests / 30 checks — the once-per-process defect, an overdue card leaving while a live one stays, the grace, all four no-deadline cards, `decide()` refusing a lapsed card, the clean read, and the "already waiting" refusal going away); `backend/test_gate_no_expiry.py` 183 checks and `backend/test_installed_stand_in.py` 15 checks still pass. **Honest limits:** nothing is deployed here — the owner's live `jarvis_gate.py` needs `scripts/apply-patches.ps1`, and the phone's resume re-read needs a new APK before the list refreshes every time the app comes to the front (the backend half alone already clears it on any fresh open or reconnect). That Kotlin line is compiled by CI's `phone-unit-tests` job and by nothing at this desk, so it is CI-only verification. A card that expires while a screen is already open still sits there until that screen next reads the queue.
