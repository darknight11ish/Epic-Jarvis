- **One PC-wide voice: change it in either app and Jarvis sounds like it - in Live too (2026-10-28).** A voice picked in the Android app or the desktop app is now the voice you actually hear, on both, including Jarvis Live. The cause of "I change the voice and Live sounds the same": a chosen personality was quietly speaking in its own voice over the top of the one both apps were showing. A personality now *writes* its voice into that one setting instead of overriding it, so the screen, the event and the sound can no longer disagree. The phone's Live screen also says which voice it is speaking in, and says plainly when your PC has no voice engine and Live is using the phone's own voice - the one case a PC setting cannot change.
