- **Settings, Devices can pair the phone again, and the card no longer calls an unreachable PC an old one:** The owner reported that his Pixel 9's Jarvis app was not pairing and that the app's Settings had nothing for "pair"/"reconnect to the PC". Both halves were true, and the second one is why the first could not be fixed from the phone: the pairing flow itself was complete (`net/Pairing.kt`, `PairingScreen.kt`, the QR code and the typed code), but the pairing screen is drawn only while `!paired || repairing` (`MainActivity.kt:2150`), so a phone that still holds a token - exactly the state a rotated key, a reinstalled PC or a moved address leaves it in - never saw it again. Settings, Devices, the one card that talks about pairing, offered exactly one pairing-adjacent control: "Unpair & clear the token", which takes the pairing AWAY and then says "Pair again to use Jarvis" with no way to do it. The two non-destructive ways back existed but could not be found by name: the Home status line, which looks like text rather than a button, and Platform checks -> "Change desktop or token". Settings, Devices now draws "Pair with your PC" as a Primary action above "Unpair & clear the token", in BOTH branches of that card, and it runs the same two lines Platform checks already runs, so there is one definition of how re-pairing starts. The card also stopped making a claim it could not support: it drew "Your PC's Jarvis does not have a device list yet" whenever the phone had not read the PC's version, which is also the state of a PC that is simply off, asleep or logged out of Tailscale - so a paired phone was told its PC was too old when the truth was that it could not be reached. `Devices.notShown` now chooses between that sentence and "This phone cannot reach your PC right now, so its list of devices is not shown here." `PairFromSettingsTest` adds 6 JVM tests: the sentence choice both ways, the button drawn above Unpair in both branches, the handler really setting `repairing`, and the label staying plain words. **Not proven here:** anything on a phone - the Kotlin is CI-compiled only, and no APK from this branch was installed, so the field-test step is the CI-built APK, open Settings, Devices, and expect "Pair with your PC" above "Unpair & clear the token".
