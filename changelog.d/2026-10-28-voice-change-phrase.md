"Your voice" and "his voice" are now names of the one `voice_speaker` choice, and "use the Clara voice" / "switch to the Ashby voice" are one closed-world sentence that only fires on a voice the pack really offers, so the natural sentence reaches `jarvis_settings_registry.set_voice_speaker` - the same `jarvis_voices.set_speaker` the Settings picker and `POST /api/voice/voices/speaker` call. It is the built-in voice only and **no card is raised either way**, because this change is cosmetic and PC-wide, the same rule the speed and the manner already follow; it is one `voices/state.json` write and one `voices` event, so the desktop and the phone both follow. Ashby and Clara also answer to the short name the owner says, not just their picker label ("Ashby (made for Jarvis)") - the label alone never matched the sentence. A name this PC's voice pack does not offer is refused with the real voice names and changes nothing; "use the passive voice" and "change the volume" are untouched and still reach the model. A recorded voice is a different, carded feature (`custom_voice`, Voices screen) and no chat sentence can select one. Proved in `backend/test_settings_registry.py` and `backend/test_settings_tool.py`; **not field-tested on the owner's PC** - the v1.0 pack that offers Ashby is not the pack on this dev machine, and the test supplies that pack's own ids rather than typing a voice name.
