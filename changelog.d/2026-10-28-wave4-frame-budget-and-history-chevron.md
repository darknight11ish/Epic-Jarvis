- **Wave 4: the Galaxy gets its frame budget on a machine with no usable GPU, and an open History group's chevron points down instead of reading as collapsed (2026-10-28).** Two pieces of work were still sitting on two stale HUD pull requests (#910, #914) and had never reached `main`, so they are recovered here against `origin/main` rather than by merging either branch. **The frame budget** is the pair of rules `#910`'s `perf(hud)` commit was the second half of: a slow renderer first loses PIXELS - `nextRenderScale` steps the 3D canvas's device pixel ratio down towards `RENDER_SCALE_MIN` (0.25) while the 2D label canvas, the CSS size, the camera and the projection never move, so text stays crisp and the picture never shrinks on screen - and only a machine still slower than `SLOW_FRAME_MS` (a 34ms median frame, about 30 a second) after that draws every OTHER frame, advancing the clock and the animation on the cheap frame so the motion steps rather than the page freezing. Both halves are now in `galaxy-cosmos.js` and its two vendored copies, and `info()` reports `scale`, `ratio` and `halfRate` so a test can read the budget instead of trusting it. This is genuinely new to `main`: the pickaxe finds no commit on `main` that ever contained `nextRenderScale` (the branch that had it merged `main` *into itself* and the resolution rule arrived in that merge's conflict resolution, which is why squashing lost it). **The chevron** is `#914`'s smaller fix: the History panel's caret is one right-pointing chevron (`chevron: "M9 6l6 6-6 6"` in `hud-history.js`), so the CSS rotation is the whole reading - and the rule on `main` rotated the CLOSED case by -90 degrees, which left an OPEN group showing an unrotated right-pointing caret beside its own rows, i.e. looking collapsed. The rule is now keyed on `data-open="1"` and rotates 90 degrees, so open points down and collapsed points right. Tests: `tests/galaxy-cosmos.mjs` 35 -> 42 checks (7 new, pure Node, no GPU: the thresholds are ordered, a fast renderer never exceeds the display's ratio, a slow one steps down and stops at the floor, a very slow one jumps straight there, a cheap picture earns pixels back one step at a time, a steady frame holds, nonsense input is never a broken picture, and the rule is monotone - worse frames never give more pixels) and `tests/hud-history.mjs` 37 -> 38 (1 new: the open/collapsed caret rules in all three page copies). The browser suites `tests/hud-galaxy-looks.mjs` and `tests/hud.mjs` also pass, so the engine still draws all eight looks and the page still passes its own wiring checks. **What was deliberately NOT taken, and why:** `HALO_LIMIT` (drop the glow haloes past 120 names) is also absent from `main` and was left out - it changes what the picture looks like with no test and no measurement on `main`'s newer per-role node builder, so it is a follow-up with its own evidence rather than a tag-along.
