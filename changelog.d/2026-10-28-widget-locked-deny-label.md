- **The locked approval widget stops offering a "Deny" it cannot perform (2026-10-28).** The owner's field bug on the OnePlus, on APK v0.2.2979 (main after #908): with the phone locked (App lock / private view) the approval widget showed "Needs your OK / A decision is waiting / Deny / Review", and tapping **Deny** only opened the app - no denial was sent. The safety rule stays (the owner kept it: a locked phone must not deny a card it cannot show), so the label changed instead. While App lock or "Hide memory lists" is on, the card now draws ONE button, **"Unlock to review"**, which opens the app; the Deny + Review pair is drawn only when a deny can really happen (`ApprovalWidgetRules.denyActsDirectly`). The hidden card's words are untouched ("Needs your OK", "A decision is waiting", no second line), and the locked state no longer shows "Review" either - one button, saying what it does. The labels and what each tap does now live in `ApprovalWidgetRules.cardButtons`, so `WidgetStatesTest` and `ApprovalWidgetRulesTest` check the promise on the JVM instead of a reader believing the drawing code; the old `showsDeny(hidden, denyOk) = hidden || denyOk` - the rule that let a locked card promise a Deny - is gone. **Not field-tested:** no APK built from this change was installed, so the locked card has not been seen on a phone; the limits are stated in the PR.
