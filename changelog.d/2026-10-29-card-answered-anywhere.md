- **A card answered anywhere leaves every live surface within 30 seconds, a killed phone's widget within 30 minutes, and a decision now says which surface made it (2026-10-29; the widget bound corrected and the beat hardened after review, 2026-10-30).** The owner's report: *"when a decision/approval card has already been addressed (approved or denied) on either the Android app or the desktop program, make sure the Android widgets (and every other surface that shows a card/notification) update to reflect that - no stale card left showing, no dead Approve/Deny on a resolved card."* The queue was already one list - `GET /api/pending`, with a decided row gone from the next read and a second answer refused - so what was missing was the second half of "one source": the `approval` event is a **doorbell that can be missed** (the backend's pump publishes only on change and swallows its own exceptions, and a pump that is not running rings nothing at all), and the phone had no clock of its own to fall back on. The desktop's HUD page has had a 30-second safety-net read since long before, and its stream re-reads the queue on every connect; the phone re-read it only when an event arrived. Now both platforms ask the same question on the same 30 seconds while a card is waiting on a **connected** link - `QueueBeat.due` (phone, re-reading through `refreshPending`, the one assignment `pending` gets, so the home-screen widget, the notification drawer, Home and the Inbox plate all clear in one beat) and `queue_beat_due` (`stream.rs`, re-reading through `refresh_pending`, the one read the bar, the widget, the tray and the HUD follow). The rule is **two conditions on both sides**, and `stale` is deliberately not one of them: a failed read is what makes a link stale, and the next successful read is what clears it, so suppressing the beat while stale switches off the only retry that can recover - one transient `/api/pending` failure held Approve until the hourly reconnect while Deny still worked. Neither reads anything when the queue is empty, so this is not a poll of an idle PC, and neither can approve or deny anything. Two review rounds hardened the halves that could fail silently: the PC's beat can no longer move the stream's 70-second silence deadline (it is absolute - `last_chunk_at + SILENCE_TIMEOUT` - so `"Jarvis went quiet"` still fires on a dead socket), and a phone response is applied only if no newer queue read has started, so a beat's slow pre-decision GET cannot re-add a card the owner just answered. Two more halves: the approvals widget now carries `updatePeriodMillis="1800000"` (the board widget's own period), because a RemoteViews drawn by a killed process is otherwise held by the launcher for ever - a cold redraw says "Not checked yet" instead of a card it cannot vouch for - which is also why the bound is not one number for every surface: **30 seconds while the app is running and the link is live; up to 30 minutes for a killed phone's widget; and while the desktop's stream is down there is no beat at all, so its bound is the next event or reconnect** (a stale-but-connected link recovers on the next beat). And the phone finally says **who** answered - it sent only `{"id"}` on `/api/approve` and `/api/deny`, so the route's `"hud"` default recorded every phone decision as the browser HUD's (the owner's field evidence: a phone deny stored as `decided_by:"hud"`); it now sends `"by": "phone"` from one constant used by both bodies. Proven by `backend/test_decided_by_attribution.py` (13 checks: the route reads `by` and defaults to hud, a decision is stored with the word it was sent, the card leaves the next read, a second answer is refused), `QueueBeatTest` (6: the rule's cases, the stale matrix, and a source check that the runtime runs the beat and does not stack a second), `PendingRefreshOrderTest` (4: a slow pre-decision response cannot overwrite the post-decision empty list, and a superseded failure cannot mark a fresh success stale), `WidgetStatesTest` (the widget's period, read from the XML a launcher reads), `SignedApprovalTest` (both bodies carry the same word), `ApiContractTest`'s instrumentation check of the posted body, and `stream.rs`'s `the_queue_beat_runs_only_for_a_waiting_card_on_a_connected_link`, `silence_wins_at_the_absolute_deadline_while_the_beat_keeps_firing` (behavioural, plus a control that the old timeout-rebuild shape never reaches silence) and `a_failed_queue_beat_is_recovered_by_the_next_read`. **Not proven:** any of it on a phone - no APK from this branch has been installed, so the field step is a CI APK, a card raised, denied on the desktop, and the home-screen widget clearing within 30 seconds; the 30-minute launcher period was not observed on a device either.
