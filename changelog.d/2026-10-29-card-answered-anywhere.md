- **A card answered anywhere leaves the Android widget, the notification and every other surface within 30 seconds, and a decision now says which surface made it (2026-10-29).** The owner's report: *"when a decision/approval card has already been addressed (approved or denied) on either the Android app or the desktop program, make sure the Android widgets (and every other surface that shows a card/notification) update to reflect that - no stale card left showing, no dead Approve/Deny on a resolved card."* The queue was already one list - `GET /api/pending`, with a decided row gone from the next read and a second answer refused - so what was missing was the second half of "one source": the `approval` event is a **doorbell that can be missed** (the backend's pump publishes only on change and swallows its own exceptions, and a pump that is not running rings nothing at all), and the phone had no clock of its own to fall back on. The desktop's HUD page has had a 30-second safety-net read since long before, and its stream re-reads the queue on every connect; the phone re-read it only when an event arrived. Now both platforms ask the same question on the same 30 seconds while a card is waiting on a live link - `QueueBeat.due` (phone, re-reading through `refreshPending`, the one assignment `pending` gets, so the home-screen widget, the notification drawer, Home and the Inbox plate all clear in one beat) and `queue_beat_due` (`stream.rs`, re-reading through `refresh_pending`, the one read the bar, the widget, the tray and the HUD follow). Neither reads anything when the queue is empty, so this is not a poll of an idle PC, and neither can approve or deny anything. Two more halves: the approvals widget now carries `updatePeriodMillis="1800000"` (the board widget's own period), because a RemoteViews drawn by a killed process is otherwise held by the launcher for ever - a cold redraw says "Not checked yet" instead of a card it cannot vouch for; and the phone finally says **who** answered - it sent only `{"id"}` on `/api/approve` and `/api/deny`, so the route's `"hud"` default recorded every phone decision as the browser HUD's (the owner's field evidence: a phone deny stored as `decided_by:"hud"`). It now sends `"by": "phone"` from one constant used by both bodies. Proven by `backend/test_decided_by_attribution.py` (13 checks: the route reads `by` and defaults to hud, a decision is stored with the word it was sent, the card leaves the next read, a second answer is refused), `QueueBeatTest` (the rule's four cases plus a source check that the runtime runs it and does not stack a second), `WidgetStatesTest` (the widget's period, read from the XML a launcher reads), `SignedApprovalTest` (both bodies carry the same word), and `stream.rs`'s `the_queue_beat_runs_only_for_a_waiting_card_on_a_live_link` plus its control on the read-loop wiring. **Not proven:** any of it on a phone - no APK from this branch has been installed, so the field step is a CI APK, a card raised, denied on the desktop, and the home-screen widget clearing within 30 seconds; the 30-minute launcher period was not observed on a device either.
