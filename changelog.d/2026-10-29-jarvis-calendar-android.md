- **The phone gets Jarvis's own calendar: a month grid, a day list and a home-screen widget (2026-10-29).** The owner asked for a full calendar of Jarvis's own - local only, on by default, disableable - and this is the Android half of it. A new `CALENDAR` screen (last in `Screen`, so a saved back stack still restores by name) opens from a row beside "Coming up" on Brain and from the widget's tap: a 7-column month grid with today ringed, a dot on every day with an event, Previous / Today / Next, and the selected day's list underneath (all-day first, then the times from earliest). One event is added, changed or deleted at a time from a sheet - there is no delete-all and no bulk form, the same rule `/api/schedule/act` keeps - and every write is held while the link is down or catching up. Titles read "(hidden)" under App lock or "Hide memory lists and chat history", while the times stay. The sixth home-screen widget (`CalendarWidget.kt`, 4x2) shows today's date and the next three events, "Nothing today" when that is the truth, one sentence and no title while the phone is locked, "Not up to date" on a stale link, and - when the calendar is switched off - it KEEPS its place and says "Jarvis Calendar is off · tap to turn it on" with a tap into Settings, because a vanishing widget or an empty square both read as a fault. On by default: the PC's switch is the only one both apps read, and turning it off hides the calendar everywhere without deleting one event. Two things the PC does not send, the phone does not pretend to know: the read window starts at the PC's today and runs 90 days forward, so a day outside it says "Not read" instead of "Nothing on this day", and the Repeat choices are the PC's own four (no "Yearly", which the PC refuses). The Add sheet's "Remind me" box says under itself that nothing rings yet - the field is stored for the slice that makes it ring.
