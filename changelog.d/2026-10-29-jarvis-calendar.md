- **Jarvis has a calendar of its own (2026-10-29).** The owner asked for "a full fledged easy to use calendar built into Jarvis" that is "disabled but on by default", local-first and shown beside Google Calendar and iCal. S1 is the store and the API, with no screen yet: `backend/jarvis_local_calendar.py` (and its `jarvis-backend/` twin, a `SHIPPED` entry and a `plugins/ready/jarvis-calendar/` folder) keeps events in `calendar.db` beside `schedule.db`, opened with the shared closing connection, and answers `GET /api/calendar?days=7` (or `?id=`), `POST /api/calendar/add`, `POST /api/calendar/act` and `POST /api/calendar/settings` from its own `install()` - so there is no `jarvis_hud.py` patch, no gate entry and no network door. **The date and time are stored as they read on the clock, never as an epoch**, so a 09:00 event stays 09:00 across a daylight-saving change; the epoch is computed on read and follows the PC's own jump (a time that never happened goes at the jump, a time that happened twice takes the first). Recurrence v1 is `none`/`daily`/`weekly`/`monthly` with an optional count or until date, and a monthly 31st is clamped to the short months (28 February in a common year). The feature is **ON with no settings file**, OFF hides the list and refuses writes while keeping every event, and a damaged settings file is OFF with a reason. One event per write, no delete-all and no bulk form; the owner's 2026-10-29 ruling records the cardless decision and the ids-only audit in `decisions.d/` and `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`. `calendar.db` is on the backup list (a missing name there silently drops every event). **"Turn off the Jarvis calendar" works by typing from the day the store lands** - through `CHANGE_BY_ASKING` and the registry's `BoolSetting("jarvis_calendar", ...)`, which calls the same `handle_settings` the route calls, so the switch cannot disagree with itself. "calendar" alone is deliberately not one of its names: a bare calendar alias would also catch "turn off Google calendar" and flip the wrong switch, so "turn off Google calendar" still resolves to nothing. `py -3 jarvis_local_calendar.py check` proves the store, routes, settings, recurrence, DST and backup line in a throwaway folder (17 checks); `backend/test_local_calendar.py` adds 88 checks (all four routes through a stand-in Handler with a cross-origin refusal and a missing-token refusal on each, the owner's own phrase driven through the real chat grammar, damaged store, one-day window, no bulk). **Honest limits:** no HUD card, phone plate or widget yet - those are the next slices and the contract they build against is in `docs/JARVIS-API.md` §163; reminders are stored and never rung; Google and iCal stay exactly as they were and say `not_on_a_screen` until their read is wired to a screen.
