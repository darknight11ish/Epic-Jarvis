- **The phone's field-test bugs are fixed: true counts, readable cards, separate taps and honest words (2026-10-29).** The 2026-10-29 real-device test found eight phone bugs; this fixes six here and points the other two at the work already doing them. The Inbox header now counts the approval cards it lists ("1 card waiting", never "0 waiting" above a waiting card). An approval card whose PC sent no summary never draws raw JSON - it starts folded with a readable line and an "Explain more" control that opens plain words. Home's status words are their own button, so a tap near "Connected" can no longer turn automatic model restarting off. A device row says "last active", never the unsupported "last used" (the PC writes that number on any request, including the app's own 90-second poll). "What can you do?" no longer offers a phone a tray-icon right-click without saying it is on the PC. "Change your voice to Ashby" when Ashby is not made says so in the Voices screen's own words instead of listing 22 voices. The calendar answer names the PC's Settings, Accounts rather than sending a phone to a screen it does not have. Settings search relevance (B5) is PR #970's, and the made-Ashby case (part of B3) is #962's.
