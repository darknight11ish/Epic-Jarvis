- **A small Jarvis window can stay on screen while you use other apps (2026-10-30).** Settings -> This app -> Picture-in-picture is Android's own picture-in-picture: the window shows the face and the first line of the newest answer (the default), the last few messages, or Jarvis Live, and it switches to the Live view by itself while a Live session runs. It never approves anything, never shows hidden or locked content (it says "Hidden while Jarvis is locked." instead) and cannot change a setting; its only action is Expand, which brings Jarvis back. Floating Jarvis and this can both be on - the floating face hides while the window is up and returns when it ends. Phone only, saved on that phone, and not yet driven on real hardware.
