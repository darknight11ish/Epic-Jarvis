- **The Jarvis Calendar can now be switched from the desktop's own Settings, and the calendar's read fails closed (2026-10-30).** Two halves were still missing after the HUD card landed. The first the owner could see: the phone has had the calendar's ON/OFF row since its own slice, but the desktop's calendar lives in the HUD - a different window with no Settings page - so on his PC there was no control for it anywhere except asking out loud, and the feature's own row said so. Settings now has a "Jarvis Calendar" card whose one switch reaches the same value the phone's row and the typed sentence reach (`POST /api/calendar/settings`, one field), through a new `calendar_settings` command that drops every event, title, place and count in Rust - the Settings page draws one switch and cannot read his day. No card either way, and turning it OFF never deletes an event; the sentence under the switch says both. The second was a bug found while wiring the tests: the HUD's calendar read treated ANY object as a calendar unless it said `available: false`, so the single-event answer `{"ok": true, "event": {...}}`, a bare event and an empty body all became "an available calendar with no events" and were drawn as *"Nothing in the next 7 days"* - a reassuring empty day invented from an answer nobody understood. The read now requires a real boolean `available` and, when true, an `events` array, and refuses anything else in words; the two write handlers had the same shape of bug (`reply.ok === false` accepted an answer with no `ok` at all as "Event added.") and now require `ok: true` positively. All of it is pinned by two new checks in `tests/hud-calendar.mjs` (33 in all), and documented as `docs/JARVIS-API.md` section 166.
