- **The desktop's floating face (the picture-in-picture face) can be made bigger or smaller from the tray, opens on the screen you are looking at, and can no longer be left half off-screen (2026-10-30).** The owner asked to improve the PC version after the phone work, and to look at picture-in-picture behaviour on the desktop. Three changes, all in `jarvis-desktop/src-tauri/src/windows.rs` plus two tray rows: (1) **a remembered face size** — 160, 200, 240 or 300 logical pixels, stepped with the tray's new "Floating face: bigger" / "Floating face: smaller" rows under More…, applied live when the face is open, persisted in `floating.json`, and defaulting to the same 200 so nothing changes for anyone who never touches it (an older or hand-edited prefs file falls back to 200 rather than a 0-pixel window); (2) **opens on the monitor under the cursor** instead of always centred on the primary display, which is the wrong screen as soon as a second monitor is in use; (3) **a full on-screen clamp** — a saved position is pulled entirely inside the monitor it overlaps most (or the nearest monitor when that one is gone), so unplugging a monitor or moving a window between mixed-DPI screens cannot leave the face unreachable. The existing promises are untouched: always-on-top by default, never takes the keyboard, no text box and no button, clicks pass through where the animal is not drawn (the hit grid and its 32×32 measurement are size-independent because the page fills the window), no new command, route, permission or approval card, and the same two local-only Tauri commands (`get_floating` now also reports `size`; `set_floating` accepts optional `clickThrough`/`size`).
