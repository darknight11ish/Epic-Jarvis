- **Chats can be archived, and put back (2026-10-30).** The owner asked on 2026-10-29 that chats can be archived and deleted with a confirm step. Delete already existed and is unchanged; this adds the missing half. A chat can now be put away without being deleted: `POST /api/history/archive {"id": "<conversation id>", "archived": true}` archives one chat, and `archived: false` brings it back exactly where it was - reversible, metadata only, and no approval card or confirm step, because it stores no words, opens no chat and sends nothing (delete keeps its own confirm). `GET /api/history?archived=1` lists the archived chats, the ordinary History list never shows them, and every conversation row carries `archived: true|false`, so the desktop HUD/Brain and the phone draw the same two lists. The flag lives in the PC's own encrypted history file as a plain `archived_at` column, so a chat archived on the phone is archived on the PC and survives a restart. Tested by `backend/test_chat_log.py`.
