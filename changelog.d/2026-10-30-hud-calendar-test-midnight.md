- **The calendar HUD test no longer fails in the last 90 minutes of the day:** its `soon` event fixture was `now + 90 minutes`, so on a CI run at 22:00 UTC the event fell on tomorrow and the drawn card (rightly) listed nothing for today. The fixture is clamped to today with a minute to spare and the suite now proves that clamp over every hour of a fake day, so no PR inherits a red `frontend` at one particular time of the clock.
