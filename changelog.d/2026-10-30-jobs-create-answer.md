- **Long Fuse: start a background job and answer the one thing it is waiting for (2026-10-30).** The job queue had a list and a cancel and no way in or out: a job could only be created by code, and a job that needed a capability waited for ever. The Brain's Work tab now starts ONE job from your own words - it begins with no permissions and parks the moment it needs one - and answers a parked job's single grant with **Allow once** or **No**: one job, one use, no standing permission, and a No stops that job without remembering it. The phone's Work pane gets the same loop in its next slice.
