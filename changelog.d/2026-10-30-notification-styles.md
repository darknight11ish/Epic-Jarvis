- **The phone's notifications can be styled, and Jarvis builds the style from your words (2026-10-30).** Say "make my Jarvis notifications look like a warm orange pulse" and Jarvis makes a preview from a closed menu - a colour name, a figure, an animation (still, pulse, flip, spinner) and a speed - which you tap Add to apply to your approval cards and reminders. The animation is one Android itself performs inside the notification (`ViewFlipper` frames and an indeterminate spinner), so it keeps running when the app's process is dead and costs no wakeups. It changes only how the words are drawn: Deny is still the only button on an approval card, nothing new is shown, and while the phone is locked or App lock is on the plain notification is used instead. The desktop deliberately has none: a Windows toast is drawn from Windows' own template, so there is no layout to style (see `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` §8 and JARVIS-API §163). Not verified on a device: no emulator or phone at this desk, so what is proven is that the app compiles, the 155 backend checks and the JVM tests pass, and every View used is on `RemoteViews`' allow-list.
