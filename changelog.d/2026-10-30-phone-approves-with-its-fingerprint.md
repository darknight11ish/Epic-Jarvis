It now says **"Approve asks for your fingerprint or PIN."**, or "Approve asks for your fingerprint." under "Fingerprint only", on its own line under the buttons, from `SecurityRules.approveCheckWords` - which reads the same two things the check reads (`approvalNeedsCheck` and `Security.method`), so it cannot promise a check that will not run, and `ApproveAsksFingerprintTest` (in the required `phone-unit-tests` job) holds the sentence and the gate together over every shared risky case. No backend change: the signed-approval contract (JARVIS-API §91) already accepts a phone's signature and already refuses a risky card from a device with its own key that carries none, and `backend/test_approval_sign.py` already proves both. The buttons keep the shared "Deny"/"Approve" words on both apps, and Deny, a cancelled check and a failed one still send nothing. One difference is written down rather than half-built: an action with no written words is refused on the PC before any prompt (`owner_check: "unlabelled"`) and the phone cannot know that, because the row carries no field for it - JARVIS-API §3, ARCHITECTURE §8.
