- **Your town can be typed on the phone now, not only on the PC.** Appearance → "Sun, moon and weather" used to say "Your town is typed on the PC, in Settings, Animal options" and show nothing to type in. The PC now accepts a town from either app and says so in its own words, and the phone has a "Your town" field with a "Save town" action beside the town it already shows and "Forget my town". The PC looks the words up in the town list it already carries (about 16,000 towns, nothing fetched online) and keeps only the position rounded to about 11 km; typing a town sends those words only to your own PC over the pairing link, and never a name or position to any service. A town the PC cannot find is refused there, in its own words, and the field stays so you can correct it. Moving the town while Open-Meteo weather is on still switches Open-Meteo off, exactly as the PC's own field always did, because its ONE card named the old position. The desktop is unchanged; the Android compile is checked by CI, and the field itself still needs a run on the phone.
