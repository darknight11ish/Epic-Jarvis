- **A described widget can now be arranged four ways, and the phone stops offering two home-screen slots that could never draw (2026-10-30).** "Put my reminders at the top" or "make the count big" now picks one of four fixed arrangements - `stack` (the default, and what every existing widget already gets, so nothing on a home screen changes unless asked), `big` (the first number drawn large), `list` (the heading, then the lists, then the counts and bars) and `compact` (one heading, one thing and the buttons) - checked on the PC and drawn the same way by the desktop widget window and by "Jarvis widget" 1-3 on the phone. The list is CLOSED and always will be: Android fixes a widget's layout when the APK is built, so no server can add one, and the docs now say that plainly instead of promising a look Jarvis cannot draw. Nothing new is approved and nothing is sent anywhere new: same blocks, same sources, same five tile buttons, same App-lock hiding, same stale-link gate, no new route. Two real defects found while building it are fixed too: the phone's slot picker offered five slots while the app shipped three board receivers, so slots 4 and 5 could be chosen on that screen and never drawn (it offers three now, and a JVM test counts the manifest to keep the two equal), and `jarvis_widgets.py`'s own docstring named a `widgets.patch` that has never existed (the module ships whole and its routes are switched on by `jarvis_brain_reads.install()`). The arrangements are proved as rules by both apps' tests against the shared fixture; no widget has been seen drawn on a real launcher or an emulator yet.
