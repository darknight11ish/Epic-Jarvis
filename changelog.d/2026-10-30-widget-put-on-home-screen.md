- **A widget you built by chatting is one tap from your home screen (2026-10-30).** When a home-screen slot has a saved widget, Brain → Widgets now offers **Put it on my home screen**: the phone asks the launcher to place that slot's widget itself, instead of only telling you the touch-and-hold route. It always says which of three things happened - that widget is already there; the home screen was asked (a launcher may still show its own confirm or refuse); or this launcher cannot add widgets on request, in which case it gives the touch-and-hold route in words. It never claims the widget was placed when Android only accepted the request. Nothing is sent to the PC, no approval card is raised, and which widget a slot shows is still chosen in the slot picker. The app compiles and the JVM tests pin every sentence and all three receivers; the pin itself was not driven on a phone at this desk (none here), so that row stays NOT-TESTABLE until the field test.
