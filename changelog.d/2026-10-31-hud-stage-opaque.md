- **The Studio stage panels are opaque now, so nothing shows through the Telemetry panel (2026-10-31).** The owner's screenshot: with the Telemetry stage tab active, the words of the main-screen cards (Today, Coming up, Right now, Settings) read through the panel - "words on top of words". Cause: the dark theme's window surfaces are translucent by design (`theme.css`: `--bg-window: rgba(4,7,12,.86)`, `--surface-1: rgba(10,17,25,.9)`), the HUD aliases them to `--void`/`--plate`, and the split-stage right rail - the one panel that draws Telemetry, Approvals, Reminders and Memory - painted itself from those aliases plus one gradient, so it was ~10-14% transparent over whatever the stage had behind it. Fix: the panel now puts the theme's opaque surface token **`--surface-sunken`** (opaque in all three themes: #04070c dark, rgb(228,233,240) light, rgb(0,0,0) high contrast) under its own gradient, so it keeps the theme's look and is solid; no colour is hard-coded and the Galaxy stage is untouched. Tests: a new browser-free pin (`tests/hud-stage-opaque.mjs`) checks the rule, the opaque token in every theme and the three-copy rule, and the Playwright HUD suite now asserts the Telemetry panel's computed background is an opaque rgb and keeps its gradient. Nothing about the panels' content, the routes or the tab behaviour changed.
