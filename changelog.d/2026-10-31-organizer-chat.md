- **The vault organizer now has a real chat door, and a stale card can no longer block a new one (2026-10-31).** The organizer field test found two gaps. First, a turn-on card that timed out or whose window was dismissed could leave the in-memory marker saying a card was waiting while `/api/pending` showed none, so a later "turn on the vault organizer" answered "one card is already waiting" and never raised another; the switch's status and turn-on now reconcile that marker against the gate's own waiting cards (matched by the action and the card's setting), and the gate's own expiry bounds it, so a fresh turn-on raises a fresh card. Second, chat could only say the organizer was off/on: with it ON, "organize my notes" / "plan my vault" / "tidy my vault" runs the READ-ONLY plan and shows the module's own scheme words and counts, says nothing has changed yet, and names the one-card, never-deletes apply; "apply the plan" / "organize my vault now" / "tidy it for real" raises the writer's ONE approval card through its own `POST /api/vault/organize` path, returns the module's words unchanged, and a denial writes nothing. A scoped ask ("organize just my Welcome note", "reorganize a specific set of notes") says plainly that there is no per-note mode: the organizer works on the whole vault, one card for the whole batch. The tests are hermetic (temp vault, fake gate) and cover raise-stale-status-fresh-card, the read-only plan, deny/apply, and the scoped and off answers. No new tool, no model call; not deployed.
