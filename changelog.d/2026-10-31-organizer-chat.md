- **The vault organizer now has a real chat door, and a stale card can no longer block a new one (2026-10-31).** The organizer field test found two gaps. First, a turn-on card that timed out or whose window was dismissed could leave the in-memory marker saying a card was waiting while `/api/pending` showed none, so a later "turn on the vault organizer" answered "one card is already waiting" and never raised another; the switch's status and turn-on now reconcile that marker against the gate's own waiting cards (matched by the action and the card's setting), and the gate's own expiry bounds it, so a fresh turn-on raises a fresh card. Second, chat could only say the organizer was off/on: with it ON, "organize my notes" / "plan my vault" / "tidy my vault" runs the READ-ONLY plan and shows the module's own scheme words and counts, says nothing has changed yet, and names the one-card, never-deletes apply; "apply the plan" / "organize my vault now" / "tidy it for real" raises the writer's ONE approval card through its own `POST /api/vault/organize` path, returns the module's words unchanged, and a denial writes nothing. A pick-a-set ask ("organize just my Welcome note", "organize these notes: Welcome, Alpha", "reorganize a specific set of notes: ...") now gets a READ-ONLY scoped plan for exactly those notes - tags only, no moves, no Map-of-Content or Vault Home, no other note read for content, and while the hidden lists are on the words are counts only; "apply the plan" raises the writer's ONE card for the whole selected batch, never deletes, and one Undo restores exactly that batch. Empty, unknown, outside or hidden selections are refused in the module's own words and change nothing, and the default stays the whole vault. The tests are hermetic (temp vault, fake gate) and cover raise-stale-status-fresh-card, the read-only plan, the selection/refusal matrix, deny/apply, scoped Undo, the hidden-lists counts-only words, and the whole-vault regression. No new tool, no model call; not deployed.
