- **The price and flight radar is real, and now says so (2026-10-31).** Asked about checking flights, Jarvis said it could not check real-time flight prices and offered travel sites. Wrong: `backend/jarvis_price_radar.py` is a read-only flight engine and local price history (`jarvis_price_sources.py` supplies the item sources), reading Google Flights' public search page - unofficial and experimental, can change or break - with a `[price_radar] enabled` switch that ships OFF and is turned on by the ordinary `change_own_config` card. It has no booking, cart, seat-hold or checkout code path, no chat lookup route and no app screen yet. Chat now answers flight-search questions from that measured truth: read-only, off by default, how to turn it on, what it will and will not return, no invented prices; booking is refused plainly with the real reason; the flight radar's own system-prompt line stops general chat denying it. (The catalogue has no price-radar feature row in this tree; that gap is reported rather than invented.)
