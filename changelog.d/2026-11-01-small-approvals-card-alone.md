- **A small read-only approval is approved by its card alone - no fingerprint, no Windows Hello (2026-11-01).** The owner asked: *"for small approvals like asking jarvis to search the web (for things like weather or the news or anything small) i would like to only have to approve the card, not necessarily use my fingerprint on top of approving the card. make this setting adjustable in settings or through chatting to jarvis."* The reason it asked was the approval-check rule itself, not the owner's setting: `SecurityRules.riskyByToday` (and the PC's `jarvis_owner_check.is_risky`) calls anything `outbound` risky, and a web search is outbound, so pressing Approve on it asked for the phone's fingerprint or the PC's Windows Hello on top of the card's own yes. There is now a third answer to "which approvals ask" - **`small`**, next to `risky` (today's rule) and `every` - and it is the default. A card is small only when BOTH hold: its action is on an explicit read-only list (a web search, a public query, the news, the one page you named, public GitHub data, the model catalogue, and the calendar / mail / notes / home reads - `jarvis_owner_check.SMALL_ACTIONS`, mirrored in `SecurityRules.SMALL_ACTIONS`), and the gate's own risk row for it says it changes nothing (`reversible` "yes"), has classified it, and nothing rushed it. Everything else - unclassified, irreversible, rushed, or anything that writes, spends, sends, deletes or changes an account, a credential, a device, the network, an app or a permission - is not small and still asks. The backend answers it once, in `is_small`, and applies the same answer to both prompts: no Windows Hello for such a card approved on this PC, and a phone's approval of it needs no device signature (a signature can only be made after a fingerprint). The phone's Security screen draws all three choices, the desktop Settings note says what the rule is, and the PC's own answer is changeable by voice or chat through the existing `change_setting` tool and the new `small_approvals` setting ("ask me for my fingerprint on everything again" / "leave the small approvals alone") - the same function the approval itself reads, so the two prompts can never disagree. Nothing is auto-approved: the card is still raised and still needs its own yes, denying still asks nothing, and the rule is held to the PC's own answers by `contract/risky-approval-cases.json` (`tools/gen_risky_approval_cases.py`, now carrying `small` per row), with `SmallApprovalContractTest` on the phone and new checks in `backend/test_owner_check.py` and `backend/test_settings_registry.py`.
