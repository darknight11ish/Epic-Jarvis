- **Goals keep a progress log, and Jarvis can be told about them (2026-11-02).** The owner asked for "a goals section in the reminder area and a way for me to record progress there along with talking to jarvis". Goals already existed (`jarvis_goals.py`, Brain -> Goals on both apps), so this adds the half that was missing to that one store rather than building a second goals feature: every goal now carries an optional target and note and an **append-only progress log** (one line, an optional number and an optional date), can be **paused** temporarily and opened again, can be **marked done**, and can be **removed** with its log - the only delete, and one no model tool offers. Talking to Jarvis works through four new cardless tools: `goals_list`, `goal_add`, `goal_progress` and `goal_done`. They raise no approval card, exactly like adding or ticking off a to-do item, and they refuse to write in a turn shaped by a web page or an email. A progress line is never edited or replaced. `POST /api/goals/<id>/progress` and the done/pause/resume/remove routes answer under `GET /api/goals`; the phone's Reminders screen gets its own **Goals** choice in the follow-up PR, and the PC's existing Goals plate is unchanged. Proved by `backend/test_goals_progress.py` (90 checks) and `backend/test_goals.py` (142 checks, unchanged).
