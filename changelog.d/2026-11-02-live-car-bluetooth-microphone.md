- **Live hears you through a car's Bluetooth again (2026-11-02).** Jarvis Live now records through a car's (or headset's) hands-free microphone properly. A car's Bluetooth microphone only exists while the phone is in a call, so Live asks Android for the call configuration - the voice-communication recorder source, communication audio mode, and the car as the communication device - and puts the phone's audio mode and device back when it stops. Before this, Live made the car the communication device and recorded on the normal path, so the car showed a call and the microphone recorded silence (the owner's report, 2026-11-02: "it acts like it is in a call but jarvis cannot hear me"). With Bluetooth that has no microphone (media only) the Live screen now says so, and if Android refuses the car Live falls back to the phone's own microphone instead of silence. The route is decided by a pure function with JVM tests for no Bluetooth, A2DP only, hands-free Bluetooth, a wired headset, a device unplugged mid-session and restoration after stop. Only the owner's own car can prove it works there.
