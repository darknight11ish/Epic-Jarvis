One check per Live session had already shipped (#1069), but a session started BY VOICE threw away the check that had just passed: "Hey Jarvis, let's talk" is only answered after the utterance passed the owner check, yet `Live.start` began an unverified session, so the next 1.5-2.0 s clip was answered "Say a full sentence so I can re-check your voice" - the owner verifying twice at the beginning of one session.
