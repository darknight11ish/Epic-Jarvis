- **The phone gets its own editable Home HUD, and Home draws from it (2026-11-02).** The owner's words of 2026-11-01 - "add building the real editor to the queue" - are built: `MenuPrefs` already held a `HudLayout` (four ordered sections, five read-only built-in presets, one `apply` reducer, `normalize` for untrusted documents) and **nothing drew it**; now Home does and there is a real screen to arrange it. **Home** (`HomeScreen.kt`) draws the four sections from `MenuPrefs.hudLayout` - each card in its section and in its order, the body in one column below 600 dp and two at or above (`HudLayout.columnsAt`), the existing hide rules applied on top (`HudLayout.visible` plus the same `MenuView` the screen already reads), so a menu hidden by voice or in Settings still empties its Home card, while the three protected safety surfaces ignore hiding and the three indicators were never cards and cannot be moved. It sits as the conversation list's first row, capped at 260 dp with its own scroll, because a first-run layout holds every card and a fixed block that tall would push the chat off Home. **The editor** ("Edit HUD", `ui/screens/EditHudScreen.kt`) moves a card by drag, by click-only Up / Down / "Move to" buttons and by five TalkBack custom actions - all three build the same `HudLayout.Action` and land in one `HudEditSession.apply`, which calls `HudLayout.apply`; there is exactly one call to the model's reducer in the whole screen. Motions are never saved as they happen: the session keeps a draft and a session-only undo stack, and `Done` hands the finished document to `MenuPrefs.saveLayout` once. `Reset` returns the default arrangement as a draft, so it can be undone. Cards a preset left out are offered back ("Not on your Home screen") through a new `HudLayout.Action.Add`, and every card the layout holds is drawn even while it is hidden, so nothing can be edited out of reach. **Presets and JSON**: the five built-ins are read-only, "Duplicate to custom" makes an editable copy (it saves the draft first - "duplicate what I am looking at" has to mean the draft), a copy can be renamed or deleted, and a pasted or hand-edited layout reads through `HudLayout.fromJson` → `normalize` before it is drawn or stored, so a hostile document can never remove, duplicate or smuggle in a safety surface. **The door** is Settings' new "Home screen" row (`SettingsScreen.kt` plate, `SettingsJump.kt` entry, `SettingsSearch` row, screen `EDIT_HUD` in `Nav.kt`, wired in `MainActivity.kt`): no route, no approval card, no pairing gate - a display preference, the same class as the theme, saved on this phone only, with nothing sent to the PC. `tools/check_parity.py` is clean and the desktop's seven widescreen presets are deliberately untouched (the phone's sections are not the desktop's regions and it never reads `contract/hud-presets.json`). Proved by 18 new JVM tests over the pure model and the session (drag / button / TalkBack all reaching one reducer, every card movable by a click-only twin, protected-card and indicator invariants after every edit, preset, paste and Reset, a hostile import that cannot hide a safety surface, `Add`'s closed world, undo, and Done-not-per-frame), source pins on the shipped screens (one reducer call, two writers, `HomeHud` first in the list, `columnsAt` asked rather than 600 repeated, the Settings door end to end), and updated expectations in `FindAnythingTest` and `MenuVisibilityTest` for the new always-drawn Settings row.
