- **The phone's Reminders button now opens a Reminders screen of its own, with a Daily to-do list inside it (2026-11-02).** The owner asked twice in one day: first for a Reminders button where Appearance's had been, then - once he had used it - *"I want a custom reminders screen on the jarvis Android app. right now when I open it it just takes me to a subsection of the brain that has reminders. I also want another option within the reminders that includes a daily to-do list"*. So the button no longer jumps to Brain -> Coming up: it opens a new `Screen.REMINDERS` (`ui/screens/RemindersScreen.kt`, the pure half in `net/Reminders.kt`) whose top carries TWO choices. **Reminders** is the same list the Brain plate draws - "Just went off" first with Snooze and Delete, then the timers, alarms, reminders and repeating jobs - from the same one `GET /api/schedule` read, drawn by the SAME row composables (`ScheduleRow`, `WentOffRow`, now shared), so the two surfaces cannot say a different thing about one job or offer a different button for it. **Daily to-do list** is the scheduler's to-do list in the PC's own two ways of showing it (JARVIS-API section 179): daily rollover, where each older item carries the PC's age tag ("from yesterday", "from Friday (3d ago)"), and day by day, where the same items sit under the PC's own day headings; Add, Done and Delete are the three writes the API already had, and the two modes can be switched right there through the scheduler's existing `{"do": "todo_mode"}` - the same one value the Settings card and a spoken sentence reach, so the three cannot disagree. Nothing new is granted and nothing new is read: no route, no backend or contract change, no new permission, no card added or removed (a repeating job's card is still raised on the PC when it is set up by talking to Jarvis), one job per tap and no delete-all - the named lists ("shopping") with their one "Clear list" stay in Brain -> Coming up, and the new tab says where they are rather than growing a second copy. The Brain plate is untouched and still the way in to those lists, the standby schedule and "a file from your PC", and it can never be hidden, so nothing moved out of reach; the desktop HUD's own Reminders tab is unchanged and `tools/check_parity.py` has nothing to classify. "Hide memory lists and chat history" hides the words exactly as before (`Schedule.hide`, with a Show that asks for the fingerprint or PIN first) and every write is held on a stale link. Tests: 14 new JVM checks in `RemindersTest` (the two choices, which rows each draws, the PC's tags and day headings in both modes, the named lists staying out, the mode control offered only when the PC sent the modes, and the wiring and route pins), the full `jarvis-client` JVM suite green at 3,242 tests in 293 classes, `HomeNavRowTest` updated for the new destination. **What is not tested:** nothing was drawn on a phone or an emulator from this desk - the tap, the tab switch and the to-do rows on a real screen are the Lead's run, and CI's build is the only compiler that has seen the shipped APK.
