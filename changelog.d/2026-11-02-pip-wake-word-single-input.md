The window now shows "Listening", then "Thinking", then "Speaking" while that turn runs - the only word a voice turn may ever put there - and goes back to the face alone when it ends, so the resting window keeps the owner's 2026-10-31 "just the model, no wording" revision. The listener, the route, the permissions and the backend are unchanged; the phone still does no speech-to-text.
