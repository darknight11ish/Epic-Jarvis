- **A reminder can ask you a question, with up to three of your own buttons (2026-11-02).** A reminder may now carry `{"kind": "ask"}`: your own question, up to three buttons in your own words (each records the answer, marks it done, or snoozes it), and where an answer is written - its own history, today's Obsidian note, a goal's progress or a habit tick. Answering is a log line, cardless like ticking a to-do; the PC draws the question on its "Just went off" row and the phone on its notification, with an answer from the phone refused while it is locked. No new route (it rides `POST /api/schedule/act {"do": "answer"}`) and no new permission model. The schedule grammar grew every N days, every N weeks, several times in one day, a first occurrence and an optional end date, each said in one readable sentence; a monthly rule did not exist before and does now. Goal progress and habit ticks are clean seams that say "not set up yet" on a PC without those records, and a button that opens a file or a page is refused by name with its reason until a second opener exists in Rust. `docs/JARVIS-API.md` §21.14; `backend/test_schedule_ask.py` is 152 checks.
