- **Jarvis can explain which accounts are connected, what it may do with each, what can be changed, and how to disconnect or connect one (2026-11-03).** One read-only `GET /api/accounts` answers the owner's own question - "what accounts are actively connected to Jarvis, what permissions and abilities does it have for them, what's changeable, how do I disconnect, how do I connect a new one" - for twelve families: the private iCal link, CalDAV sign-in, Google Calendar, GitHub, email, Home Assistant, the search keys, the cloud-AI keys, the blind credential vault, the notes folder, and two honest non-entries (Notion is an export, not a sign-in; Spotify/Drive/Dropbox are not connectable and Jarvis says so rather than pretending). Every ability is resolved live from the permission table (`jarvis_framework.action_tier`) and the gate's own risk words (`jarvis_gate.risk_for`) - nothing is a copied tier, and when those modules cannot be read the row says so instead of guessing a "never". The default path opens no socket, writes nothing and never raises a card, so "connected" is used only when a verifier actually ran in that call; otherwise the honest words are "saved" or "not set up", and "when did I connect this" is answered "I can't know" because the credential store keeps no timestamp. No value is ever returned - not a secret, a mask, a prefix or a length; entry names only. The chat sentence and the desktop/phone Accounts plates that call this are the coordinated follow-up, not part of this change.
