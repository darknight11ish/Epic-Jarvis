- **Reminders can be routines: up to 12 parts, each with its own time and its own buttons (2026-11-03).** "Remind me at 8 to start the laundry, then at 8:30 move it to the dryer, then at 9 fold it" sets one reminder with timed parts; "morning routine at 7: 1. meds 2. keys 3. badge" sets a checklist whose parts come up one after another. Each part is one notification, each part has its own Done / record / snooze buttons and its own destination (history, today's Obsidian note, a goal, a habit), and an existing reminder's question or buttons can now be edited in place instead of deleting it and retyping it. Creating a routine in chat needs no card and leaves one Undo receipt, so Undo outlives the 120-second "cancel that" window; only a part that runs a task raises the usual schedule card. Honest limits: the PC is still the clock, so a part that goes off while the phone is away is not pushed later; a part more than 12 hours missed is skipped and counted rather than notified; the Windows toast names the part in words but carries no per-part buttons; and the phone answers parts and never creates or edits a routine, because a part's destination can be a folder on this PC.
