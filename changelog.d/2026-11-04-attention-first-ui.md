- **The Work pane puts what needs you first, and everything quiet on one line (2026-11-04).** The desktop's Work tab and the Android app's "Background jobs" now split the job list with one `waits_for_person` predicate: an approval, a blocked/`needs_input` question or an unacknowledged failure is drawn first and expanded under "Needs you", and when nothing needs you that section is not created at all, so it takes **zero pixels** - no heading, no empty box, no gap. Everything else is ONE collapsed "Quiet / healthy" line (`Quiet / healthy · 3 background jobs — 1 still running, 2 finished · last finished 5m ago`) that opens on a click or tap to the same rows. The PC's own optional per-job `waits_for_person` boolean is believed when a payload carries it, and both apps derive the same answer from `state` otherwise, so a backend without the field looks right rather than broken. The split is stable (the read's own order), adds no new approval, no new action and no polling, and leaves the existing one-shot grant answer and Cancel exactly where they were. Both apps: `jarvis-desktop/src/attention.js` + `jarvis-desktop/tests/attention-first.mjs`, and `jarvis-client`'s `net/JobAttention.kt` + `AttentionFirstTest.kt`.
