- **The phone can record a call when you say "record call", and your PC turns it into a dated note (2026-11-04).** Say "record call" to Jarvis (or press Arm in Settings, Voice, Call recording) and the next call on this phone is recorded: a notification stays up for the whole call with the clock, Stop and the reminder "Recording - tell the other person", and the audio goes to your own PC as it is made. The PC turns it into words, dates it, works out who was speaking and writes one note in Obsidian named with the date and time. The honest limit is in the notification and in the card: Android does not let a normal app record the call's own audio, so this records the phone's MICROPHONE with the call on speaker - both voices through the air - and if the phone refuses to switch the call to speaker the notification says "Turn the call on speaker so Jarvis can hear both sides" instead of claiming both sides were caught. A dropped connection loses nothing: every piece is written to the phone's own storage first, and a failed send is retried with the same number. A crash between the PC's own 200 and the phone's bookkeeping used to make that retry ask for a number the PC already had, which answered 409 for ever (2026-11-05's privacy audit, D3); the phone now reads the number the PC says it wants (`expected`), records every piece below it as sent and sends the real next one, so the recording recovers instead of being stuck. A recording the PC has not taken is kept and offered again - and, when the owner no longer wants it sent (a denied card, a PC that refuses it), the same card offers Delete behind a confirmation that says it is deleted from this phone only and cannot be undone (D7). While it is armed or recording, Jarvis Live, "hey Jarvis" and the talk button keep their microphones closed (they are paused, not stopped, and come back when you disarm), and nothing on the phone turns speech into words or writes to the vault. **NOT tested on a real call from this session** - the owner's own Pixel 9 is the field test, after this and the PC half land.
