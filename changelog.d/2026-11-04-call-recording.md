- **Say "record call" and your phone records the call; this PC transcribes it, labels who spoke and files a new Obsidian note named for the date and time (2026-11-04).** The owner's ask, in their own words: *"add the ability of jarvis to record a call if I tell it a keyword 'record call' where it will record all the audio, transcribe it, put a date and identify the speakers in the transcription, and store all of it in a new obsidian note labeled with the date and time"* - the call being taken through the Android Jarvis app. **The honest platform fact first:** Android does not let an ordinary app capture the call's own audio stream (`VOICE_CALL` is privileged; his primary phone is a Google Pixel 9), so the phone records **through its microphone with the call on speaker** and uploads that, and the far end is heard through the speaker - quieter and noisier than a system recorder would give. That is said on the card, in the docs and in the note itself, and importing a file from the phone's own call recorder is a later addition that is **not built**. This pull request is the **PC half**, one shipped module (`backend/jarvis_call_notes.py`) with five routes installed by its own `install()` through `plugins/ready/call-notes` - no patch: `POST /api/call/recording/start|chunk|finish`, `GET /api/call/recording` (+`?session=`) and the PC-only `POST /api/call/recording/delete`, 16 kHz mono s16le PCM, chunks in order from `seq` 1 at most 512 KiB each, a `receipt` that is the frozen contract's one-line STRING plus the structured `recording`, `finish` requiring `consent_notice: "on"` and starting the transcription on a daemon thread so the request never waits for the model. It reuses the existing local reader (`jarvis_media_read.read_media`, sherpa-onnx, English only, in 9-minute parts because its own ceiling is 10 minutes), the owner's enrolled voice print and the real speaker model (`jarvis_voice.find_profile` + `EcapaEmbedder`/`StrongEmbedder` (whichever the print was made with) + `cosine`, then clustering at 0.55 up to six, numbered in first-speech order; with no model or no trained print **every line says "Speaker"** and the note records why - never a guessed name), and the notes editor's own create path (`jarvis_notes_edit.handle_create`, so the ONE `edit_vault_note` card, File Guardian copy and undo receipt are unchanged - a call can therefore raise TWO cards, the start and the note). The note is a NEW `Calls/YYYY-MM-DD HHMM - Call.md` with `date`, `started_at`, `duration`, `source` ("phone call, speaker recording"), `speakers`, `audio`, `session`, `consent` ("Recorded locally; tell the other person") and `[HH:MM:SS] You: ...` lines in the time of day they were said; if the vault is missing or invalid the transcript is still written as `transcript.md` beside the audio and that path is returned with a plain sentence, so a recording is never lost. **It is off by default** (`[call_recording] enabled = false`, a hand edit - no in-app switch, the same shape `github_search` uses), and starting is ONE `record_call` approval card at tier `ask` because it captures *other people's* speech. The eight whole messages ("record call", "record this call", "record the call", "start recording the call", "start call recording", "stop recording", "stop the call recording", "stop call recording") are answered by `jarvis_quick.py` whole-message only, and the answer carries the new additive `X-Jarvis-Route` field `call_record: {"action": "arm"|"stop"}` that the Android app acts on; with the feature off the reply says so and the field is absent. Registered in the repo's own machinery: `features/features.json` (+ the two app copies and the regenerated catalogue), `contract/feature-controls.json` (one Command-Only row, 103/43/2/16/12), `jarvis_asks_first.py`'s page, `jarvis_card_words.py`, `jarvis_reach.py`, the framework's `[autonomy.tiers]` and `[call_recording]`, `_where.py` and `apply-patches.ps1`'s SHIPPED lists, and `plugins/registry.json` (all regenerated by their own tools). **Proved by** `backend/test_call_notes.py` - 211 checks: byte-exact chunk assembly behind a canonical WAV header, out-of-order/oversized/consent refusals, fake transcriber segments into the note body, fake embedders for the owner match, the clusters and both degrade cases, the note name and frontmatter, the vault-missing fallback, the phrase parser and whole-message rule, the directive, the feature-off refusal, the PC-only read route and the install wrapper. **Recorded, not hidden:** the gate's `_RISK` table now HAS a row for `record_call` (`("yes", "local", ...)`, added 2026-11-05 by this fix), so its card no longer prints the generic "unclassified action ... able to leave this PC" warning whose second half was wrong for this action; the phone's recorder is its own pull request against that contract and neither lane drove a real call on the Pixel 9, so the speaker path and its words are NOT-TESTABLE until the owner's field test; the delete route is PC-only and behind its own `delete_call_recording` card, and recordings are kept until the owner deletes them (no timer; the 400-recording cap refuses rather than deletes - `docs/CALL-RECORDING.md` section 7); a repeated chunk `seq` is now accepted idempotently (200, `already: true`) instead of 409, so a crash between the PC's 200 and the phone's own bookkeeping can no longer brick a recording; and **no real phone recording, transcription, speaker model, vault write or approval card was driven** - those rows are NOT-TESTABLE, never passed.
