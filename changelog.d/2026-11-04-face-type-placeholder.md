- **The phone's full-screen face bar fills its empty space with "Ask Jarvis" (2026-11-04).** The small typing box beside the microphone used to be a 48-96dp stamp with empty space after it. The owner asked on 2026-11-04 for "the blank box in this screenshot in the bottom right corner to take up rhe remaining exmptyspace around it with the wording \"Ask Jarvis\" present (but it disseapars affer i tap the box and the keyboard pops up, reappears if itss empty)", and that is what the bar does now. The box takes its whole cell of the bar (`Modifier.weight(1f)`, the same weight the two left labels take, so the 48dp microphone stays exactly in the middle, "Show chat"/"More" stay left and Stop still appears at the right edge while an answer streams). While it is empty and unfocused it draws the owner's own words, the same "Ask Jarvis" the chat's box shows above itself. The words go the moment the box takes focus - the same tap that opens the keyboard - and come back once it is empty again and focus has left it; that rule is one plain function, `FaceBarPlan.placeholderVisible`, which the JVM tests hold exactly, because a Compose screen cannot be driven from them here. TalkBack still calls the box "Type a message to Jarvis", unchanged: a screen reader's name for a control is not a placeholder, and the owner asked for the visible words. The 96dp cap that used to keep the box from becoming a second composer is gone with the empty space it left, and the box still clears its own 48dp touch target on the narrowest phone this app supports (320dp) even with Stop showing. What is NOT proved here: nothing was built onto or driven on a phone from this branch, so a real tap moving focus and taking the words away, and the fit at 360dp, are reasoned and unit-tested only. (The typos in the quoted ask are the owner's own words.)
