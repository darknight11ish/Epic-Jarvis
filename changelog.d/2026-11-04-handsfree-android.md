- **Hands-free mode on the phone: every approval card is read out, "confirm" or "deny" answers it by voice, and Jarvis can write a text message for the owner (2026-11-04).** A new Settings row (last on the screen, so it moves no other row), and the same switch from a typed chat sentence or said in Jarvis Live - one parser for both, answered by the phone rather than by the model. With it on, a card the PC raises arrives as a `handsfree_card` event (`card_id`, `action`, `say`, `expires_in_sec`): the phone fetches the PC's own WAV from `/api/voice/say` for `say` and plays it through its speaker (never during a phone call, and never in the phone's own voice if the PC has no voice engine - the card is then shown only, and no microphone opens), posts an ongoing notification naming the card and how long it has to answer with its own "Open the card" action (under App lock, "Hide memory lists and chat history", or a phone with no secure lock of its own, BOTH notifications carry one generic line instead - so neither a card's words nor a text message reaches the lock screen - and each is `VISIBILITY_PRIVATE` with a redacted public version, the same `SecurityRules.notificationsLocked` rule every other notifier uses), and then opens the PC's own ANSWER WINDOW - the event's `expires_in_sec`, a minute at most or the card's shorter countdown - marked by the same two-note chime the talk button uses. The window records at most the PC's 30 seconds of 16 kHz mono WAV (`handsfree/HandsfreeClip.kt`, because a longer clip is refused rather than truncated) and closes about a second after the owner stops speaking (the same `TurnEnd` rule the talk button uses, so a one-word answer does not wait out the minute), then uploads to `POST /api/handsfree/answer`; **the phone never transcribes anything** - the PC transcribes it, checks the owner's voice print and decides - and the phone SPEAKS and shows the PC's own `say` ("Approved. Carrying on.", "That was not your voice, so nothing was decided.", "Sorry - was that yes or no?"), so no refusal or decision is ever worded by the app. An unclear answer the PC asks once more is answered again in a second window inside the same deadline, and never a third. `POST /api/handsfree` is the ONE switch path (the GET on the same path is the read, and the contract has no `/enabled` route): OFF is immediate and cardless, ON goes through ONE ask-tier card, and the phone reads the PC's own `enabled` back before it says on. A refused answer keeps the PC's sentence; the app's own fallback ("Your PC did not accept that answer - nothing changed.") is used only when a PC sent none, and "the card had expired" is never said unless the card's own state says so. `phone_command kind=send_sms` is carried out on the phone - the name is resolved in its own address book by an exact, case-folded, unique match (two numbers for one name sends nothing and says why; the same number twice is one match; a number the owner said is used as it is), `SEND_SMS` and `READ_CONTACTS` are runtime permissions asked for when the switch goes on, and `/api/phone/result` gets an answer for every command, with Android's own `RESULT_OK` as the only thing that ever means "sent"; the PC's `phone_result` then replaces that notification's line with the PC's own sentence, quietly, so the two halves say the same thing. The microphone is arbitrated through one new ledger (`voice/MicOwner.kt`): the answer window HOLDS it and so does the talk button (`audio/Recorder.kt`, which takes it before it opens its own `AudioRecord` and gives it back in a `finally`), so a press and a window can no longer open two recorders at once - whichever is refused says why in whole words ("You were talking to Jarvis, so the card was not read out." / "Jarvis is listening for your answer to a card, so the microphone is busy. Try again in a moment.") - while Jarvis Live and "hey Jarvis" close their recorders for that window and reopen afterwards, and the talk button still shows "Listening for your confirm/deny". A PC without the feature answers 404 on all four routes and sends none of the three events, and every surface then says "Hands-free is not available on this PC yet." - no invented mode, no microphone. `jarvis-client`'s JVM suite is green at 3,475 tests (six new files: `HandsfreeFlowTest`, `HandsfreeClipTest`, `HandsfreeEventTest`, `HandsfreeNoticeTest`, `MicHandsfreeRulesTest`, `SmsSendTest`); four existing tests gained justified pins (the services-with-a-foregroundServiceType count 5 → 6, the wake word's pause condition, the jump list's last entry, and the two lists of Settings rows with no catalogue menu id).
