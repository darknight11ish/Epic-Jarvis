- **Each review, roundup or video can now show whether it was paid for, and the evidence for it (2026-11-04).** The owner's request was to filter out sponsored reviews and find quality across sources instead of trusting a star rating. This is the editorial half, built and tested but deliberately not wired to anything yet: `backend/jarvis_source_independence.py` (shipped whole, with its byte-identical `jarvis-backend/` twin) reads ONE supplied page and returns disclosure wording (sponsored, advertisement, #ad, gifted, free product, paid partnership, includes paid promotion, commission, affiliate) with the matched phrase, a snippet and where it was found; every link carrying an affiliate marker (`tag=` on a shop address, `aff_id`, `affiliate`, `associate`, `partner`, a known affiliate-network address), a tracking marker (UTM tags, `fbclid`, `gclid`, `ref=`), a shortener or a redirect wrapper, with the host and matched parameter and a few truncated examples; urgency wording beside the verdict, never part of it; and a plain "what could not be checked" list. The per-source answer is one of exactly five words - `disclosed_sponsored`, `likely_monetised_undisclosed`, `affiliate_only`, `no_monetisation_evidence`, `cannot_tell` - each with its own evidence. **There is no trust score, no probability and no percentage anywhere**, for the product engine's own measured reason: a single number hides which rule fired. `cannot_tell` is a real answer, not a guess: an empty page, a JavaScript shell or a page below 40 readable words is reported as unchecked, never as clean. `rank_sources()` puts the most independent sources first and says in plain words how many sources with no monetisation evidence made the same claim beside the monetised ones that made it, comparing claims word for word and printing no percentage. The link and urgency patterns are a Python-native port of dakidarts/dark-radar (MIT), credited in `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt`; no JavaScript is vendored. `backend/test_source_independence.py` has 150 checks over synthetic fixtures only, with no network, no browser and no real page; it tries every verdict path, both `cannot_tell` shapes, the no-score rule, the ranking and the agreement counts, and pins by AST that the module imports only `re` and `html.parser`. No route, chat tool, approval card, Settings row or app screen is added, and `docs/JARVIS-API.md` is untouched because there is nothing to call yet; the design and its honest limits are in `docs/SOURCE-INDEPENDENCE.md`.
