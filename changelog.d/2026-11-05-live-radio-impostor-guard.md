- **Jarvis Live stops answering the radio (2026-11-05).** The owner reported that `Jarvis Live` accepted a radio, a television or a call in the room as his own voice. The cause was the session latch added on 2026-11-01 (#1104 / DEF-028): one verified sentence covers the whole Live session, and in the 1.5-2.0 s band - and for every clip when the session's trigger needed no check - `jarvis_live.trust_decision` answered "trusted", so `jarvis_speech.hear` ran no speaker model at all. The latch is KEPT, because re-prompting the owner to re-enrol mid-session was the behaviour the 2026-11-01 ruling removed, and short confirmations ("yes", "do it", under 1.2 s) are still accepted while latched. What the latch may no longer skip is the cheap half of the owner check: a latched clip with `RADIO_GUARD_S` (1.2 s) of speech or more is now put to the in-RAM CAM++ embedding and compared with the enrolled print's centroid - about 15 ms, one embedding and one cosine, nothing written - and a clip under `RADIO_MIN_COSINE` (0.25 with a "hey Jarvis" prefix, 0.32 without) is dropped in silence: not transcribed, never sent to the model, and nothing said about it. The rule's own shape is `jarvis_live.IGNORED_BACKGROUND` (`{"ok": true, "ignored_unmatched_speaker": true, "reason": "background_media_rejected"}`); on the existing utterance wire the same clip answers `ok` **false** with `ignored_unmatched_speaker` true and that `reason`, because `Heard.as_dict()` defines `ok` as "a transcript was produced" and both apps read it that way - a client handed `ok: true` with no text would have nothing to do with it. A guard that cannot measure (no print, no model, an unusable clip) never swallows the owner's words. `backend/test_voice_check_rule.py` grew two test functions and 16 checks, including the regression itself: after one verified sentence a 1.7 s clip is still "trusted" by the latch and now must also pass the guard; 103 passed, 0 failed. The twins are byte-identical, `check_stale_twins.py` is clean, `test_base_matches_repo.py` 136/0 and `test_base_regeneration.py` 39/0. Not in this change: the "it's on your screen" answers and the slow first turn - separate fixes on the same branch, reported on their own.
