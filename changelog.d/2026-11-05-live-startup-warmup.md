- **Jarvis Live's first turn is warm now: it keeps the words you say the moment you press Live, says "Starting microphone & voice..." instead of pretending to listen, and cannot stay stuck on "Thinking..." (2026-11-05).** Three owner-reported problems on the same cold start had one honest answer each. The desktop's voice gate has always kept a little audio in front of the moment it hears you, but only 300 ms - the wake word's number - and a sentence that starts in the same instant Live is pressed loses its opening; Live now keeps 500 ms (`voice.rs` `LIVE_PREROLL`), in memory only, prefixed to that session's first clip. The status route grew two keys, `starting` and `starting_words` (`jarvis_live.py`): while STT, the VAD, CAM++ and TTS are still loading behind the already-open microphone, the bar and the badge show "Starting microphone & voice..." and "I'm listening." is said only when that warm reports - and never later than 8 seconds whatever the warm does, so the sign cannot stick. And the bar's "playing an answer" claim (Rust `SPEAKING`, read by `voice.rs` before it drops a sentence said over an answer) used to be a bare flag: if the player never cleared it, Live went deaf for the rest of the session. It now stores when it was set and is believed for at most 120 seconds, renewed every second the answer really plays. It is not a new pause and it adds no card, no route, no permission and no model-lane change; `docs/JARVIS-API.md` 63.1 and `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` 15 say the shape, `backend/test_live.py`, `jarvis-desktop/tests/jarvis-live.mjs` and the Rust tests prove it.
