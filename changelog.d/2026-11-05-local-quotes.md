Nothing is fetched and nothing is written down: the picker is `(day * 17 + 11) % 108`, and because 17 shares no factor with 108, day by day it sees every quotation once before any repeats, with the same day always giving the same quote on the PC and the phone. It adds no route, no model tool, no permission and no new card. `backend/test_quotes.py` (74 checks) proves the corpus's properties, the loader's refusals, the coprime full cycle, determinism with no file written, the four phrases and the six near-misses ("quote the last email" does not match), the kind's one card with three fire times, and the fired event's body; the schedule contract gained the kind so all three surfaces are compared on 32 cases each.
