- **Send a document, or a Notion export, from your phone into Jarvis (2026-11-05).** Settings, Folders Jarvis may look in, has two new buttons. Send a document puts a PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, text, Markdown or CSV file into a new "From my phone" folder inside one of the folders you already listed, after ONE card on the PC - nothing lands before you say yes. Bring in a Notion export carries the .zip you made yourself in Notion (Settings, Export all workspace content, as Markdown & CSV) into an Obsidian vault, with the PC's one card and its Undo. The phone keeps no copy and sends the file nowhere else; a PC that does not have this yet says so and nothing is sent. Up to 50 MB a document and 250 MB a Notion export.
