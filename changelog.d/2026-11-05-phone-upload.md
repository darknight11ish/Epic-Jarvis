- **A document sent from the phone now lands on the PC as if the owner had put it there himself, and a Notion export from the phone can be run through the existing vault import (2026-11-05).** The owner's ask - "upload any documents through the jarvis app and have it be fully processed as if I was uploading it to the jarvis desktop" - needed no new processing: the folder list, the text index, the PDF/Word/Excel/PowerPoint converter and the Notion->Obsidian importer all exist and are tested. What was missing was the transport, and it is a new no-patch drop-in, `plugins/ready/upload` + `backend/jarvis_upload.py`: `POST /api/upload/begin` (a name, a size, an optional sha256 and one of the folders `GET /api/folders` already returned, chosen by EQUALITY against `jarvis_documents.folders()` - the phone cannot create a grant), `POST /api/upload/chunk` (1 MiB of raw bytes per request, so every body stays about a third of the server's 4 MiB cap and a Notion export can arrive at all), and `POST /api/upload/finish` (the bytes that really arrived measured against the declared size and the sha256, then `jarvis_attached_file`'s own magic-byte, encrypted-PDF and zip-bomb guards, then ONE `os.replace`). A document lands in `<the listed folder>\From my phone\<name>` and from that moment is a desktop file - findable by name at once, indexed on the next search if it is a note or text file, convertible on request - because nothing in the new module indexes, converts or reads anything. A file already named there is never overwritten (`taxes (2).pdf`). **ONE card per file, before a byte is written:** `receive_uploaded_document`, tier `ask`, in `jarvis_asks_first.HARD_LIMITS` and `MUST_ASK` and on the "This PC and your phone" page, so no app and no settings line can turn it into a standing yes; a denied, timed-out or never-answered card writes nothing, and every chunk answers 409 while the card waits and 403 once it is denied. While it waits, the bytes sit in a dot-prefixed staging folder inside the destination, which `jarvis_documents.allowed_path` refuses - so a half-written file is invisible to find, read and search - and the staging is deleted on success, on refusal, on a denied card, on expiry (30 minutes) and by a sweep on the next `begin`. Caps: 50 MB a document (the number the desktop reader uses), 250 MB a Notion export, three uploads at once, 512 MB staged in all; executables, scripts, pictures and every kind outside the same eight the attachment reader takes are refused by NAME at begin and by BYTES at finish. **The second half reuses the Notion importer unchanged, behind two routes that take no path:** `GET /api/notion/vaults` offers only vaults this PC already validated (the one configured for notes, and the last one an import used), and `POST /api/notion/phone-import` takes an upload id and a vault id - no digest answers `plan_import` + `describe` and writes nothing, a digest calls the existing `request_import`, unchanged: one `import_notion_export` card, `run_import`, the receipt and Undo, and the staged zip deleted in a `finally`. The two path-taking PC-only Notion routes are untouched and still answer `403 pc_only` from any other device. `docs/JARVIS-API.md` section 196 and section 152 record the routes, `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` section 8 records that the phone's picker and progress chip are the next lane, and the reversal of the 2026-10-29 "no upload endpoint, now or later" line is recorded in `decisions.d/2026-11-05-phone-upload.md`. Proved by `backend/test_upload.py` (180 checks: the chunked happy path, the card raised before anything lands, a denied card and a timed-out card leaving nothing, the sha256 and size refusals, both caps, the 4 MiB body arithmetic, an unlisted folder, an empty and a damaged list, every refused extension, a renamed `.docx`, a zip bomb, an encrypted PDF, a text file that is really binary, a name with a path in it, the staging invisible before finish and gone after, expiry and the sweep, the three-in-flight cap and the staging quota, the Notion dry run writing nothing and the import raising ONE card, the two PC-only refusals still holding, the twins, the plugin catalogue and the gate tables), plus `backend/test_documents.py`, `backend/test_notion_obsidian.py`, `backend/test_asks_first.py`, `backend/test_card_words.py` and `backend/test_plugin_catalogue.py` unchanged and green.
