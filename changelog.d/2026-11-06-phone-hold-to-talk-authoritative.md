- **A held talk button is no longer ended by a pause (2026-11-06).** The owner reported that holding the mic button "keeps cutting me off and acting like I took my finger off of the mic button even when I'm still speaking sometimes". The button decided hold-versus-tap only when the finger LIFTED (`ui/parts/VoicePress.kt`, 400 ms), while `voice/VoiceSession.kt` ended the clip whenever the pause rule fired - the on-phone Smart Turn model saying "finished", or two seconds of quiet - which is right for a tap and wrong for a hold. The button now reports the hold about 400 ms into the press, while the finger is still down (`VoiceButton`'s new `onHoldStarted`, threaded through `HomeActions` to `VoiceSession.holdStarted()`), and the capture loop's new pure `voice/HoldEndRule.kt` suspends both the pause end and the model ask until the finger lifts; the release still sends, and the recorder's own 30-second cap still ends a held capture, so a finger left on the button cannot hold the microphone open past it. A tap never sets the flag, so tap-to-talk keeps its pause auto-send exactly as before, and Live and handsfree - which have no held button - are untouched. The five end cases are pinned by JVM tests (`HoldEndRuleTest`, plus the press predicate in `VoicePressTest`). Not proved here: the gesture itself, which needs a real phone; the field-test script is in the pull request.
