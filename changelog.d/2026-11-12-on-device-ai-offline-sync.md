- **On-Device AI companion and offline two-way sync:** The Android app runs local GGUF models via llama.cpp with optional Vulkan GPU acceleration, capturing notes, chat turns, and memories in an encrypted SQLite store while completely offline. When reconnecting to the PC over the private mesh (Tailscale/WireGuard), a two-way sync reconciles notes and memories to PC storage. Settings on phone include Vulkan toggle, CPU threads (2, 4, 6), context size (1024, 2048, 4096), auto-sync on reconnect, offline memories toggle, and battery saver guard. Jarvis answers chat inquiries about offline operation, mobile lanes, and sync deterministically.
