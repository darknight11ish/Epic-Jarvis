- **Jarvis answers where the chats are kept, from its own code - never with the model's "not stored anywhere"** — asked "where are the chat backups stored?", the live backend told the owner his conversations "live only in the app's memory until you close it or clear them" and offered to copy important chats to a file. That is false in the worst direction: `jarvis_chat_log.py` has kept chat history, voice included, on this PC, encrypted, by default since the owner's decision of 2026-09-24. The architect's ruling (Gemini, 2026-11-13 22:30 PT, section 5.6) is that a self-fact about storage, history, privacy or settings is answered deterministically from the code, or refused - so `jarvis_quick.py` has a new read-only `chat_history_storage` intent for "where are my chats stored", "are my chats saved", "do you keep a record of this", "where are the chat backups", "is my history encrypted", "what happens to my voice transcripts" and "when do the chat backups run". The words come from `jarvis_chat_log`'s own `settings()` and its own `chat-history.db` path, checked at the moment of answering (the file is opened before the reply says it is there), and never from `status()`, whose cipher mints the Credential Manager key on a brand-new history - this answer writes nothing, raises no card and never reaches a model. It states kept-on-this-PC-by-default, AES-256-GCM with the key in Windows Credential Manager, the file and folder, that voice transcripts are kept as ordinary encrypted turns and the recording is not, the retention window, and the switch as each app draws it ("Chat history on this PC" in the desktop's Settings, Security; "Keep chat history on this PC" in the phone's History screen). Three states are said out loud instead of the default sentence: the switch is off, the settings file cannot be read (the module's own full path is deliberately not repeated into a reply that reaches the phone), and the file cannot be opened. A backup timetable does not exist and is refused in words rather than invented, and the block is narrow enough that "where are my notes stored", "delete my chat history" and "how long do you keep my chat history" keep their own paths (`docs/JARVIS-API.md` section 189.8 hands the one storage shape it used to answer to the new intent and keeps every clearing, size and expiry shape). `backend/test_self_knowledge_honesty.py` holds all of it: the six question forms are deterministic and never reach the model, the answer carries the encrypted/by-default/switch facts and none of the old false phrases, the off state says so, an unreadable record is never claimed, nine unrelated questions are not captured, no write path is taken, and every one of the six checks fails if the rule is deleted.
