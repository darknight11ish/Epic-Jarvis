- **The two heaviest CI jobs get a 120-minute budget, so a slow run stops being reported as a red one.** The required `backend` and `backend-windows` jobs were capped at 90 minutes with no headroom left: on 2026-11-13 the `backend` job of PR #1240 was killed at 1h30m16s *while it was still running the suites*, so a change with no defect in it arrived as a failed required check and could not merge. Both ceilings are now 120 minutes, which unblocks that PR and leaves about half an hour over the slowest run measured that evening - `backend-windows` at 58m41s and 59m43s on adjacent pull requests, and `backend` at 1h13m56s on a tree that did finish. Splitting the `backend` suite into two parallel jobs is the durable answer and lands after this wave; this is the stop-gap, and no test is dropped or skipped to fit it.
