- **The phone's microphone now stops Jarvis when you press it, on by default (2026-11-13).** Before this, pressing the talk button while Jarvis was speaking did nothing: while a reply was still arriving both microphone buttons were greyed out, and once the reply had arrived but its last sentences were still playing `VoiceSession.begin` answered "Still finishing the last one." and returned without touching the speaker. Now a press during speech silences the reply at once - the same `VoiceSession.stopSpeaking()` the "stop" word already calls, which pauses the audio track and stops the phone's own TTS - then drops the old turn the way "hey Jarvis" does (`speakStreamed`'s job is cancelled and its `Turn.silenced` latch stops any further sentence), and starts listening exactly as an ordinary press does; the words still go to the PC's own voice route, so the phone transcribes nothing. This is the behaviour the desktop's talk button has always had (`jarvis-desktop/src/main.js` `startPushToTalk`: `noteCutOff(); stopSpeaking();`), so the phone was the missing half. The new switch is Settings -> Voice -> "Stop speaking when I press the mic", ON by default and this phone's own; OFF gives the old behaviour back exactly, including the "Jarvis is answering" line and "Still finishing the last one.". It is separate from the existing "Interrupting Jarvis" setting, which is about the phone listening by itself while Jarvis talks. The local JVM/contract suite is green (3,679 tests, 0 failures, 0 skipped); NOT tested on a real phone - in particular the "stops within a tenth of a second" claim rests on Android's `AudioTrack.pause()` (the stop word's existing path), and the first few milliseconds of the new clip could still catch the cut syllable when the speaker is on the voice-call route.
