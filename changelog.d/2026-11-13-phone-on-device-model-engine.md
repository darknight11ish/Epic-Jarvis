- **The phone can run a small AI model on itself, in its own Local AI screen (2026-11-13).** A `.gguf` model you put on the phone answers there with no PC and no internet, behind a switch that ships OFF. The screen says "On-device model: runs on this phone, not your PC", never downloads a model, never loads one until you tap Load, tells you before you load it whether the phone has room, and shows each reply's measured tokens per second or says "not measured". The field test on the owner's phone (2026-11-13) drove the real screen: Qwen2.5-0.5B, 1.5B and 3B Q4_K_M each answered `Hello, who are you?`, at 27.1, 13.8 and 7.7 tokens per second as shown on the screen and matched by the engine's own `generated N token(s) in M ms` log line. Two things the test found are fixed in the same change: the model now stays loaded for **5 minutes** with nothing asked instead of 5 seconds (the 5-second window unloaded it 16 seconds after Load, before a person could start typing), while the 180-second per-reply limit is unchanged and leaving the screen still frees the model at once; and the draft field and **Send** stay on screen with the keyboard up instead of being clipped out of the accessibility tree, with a small **Clear** action beside Send to discard a draft that survives leaving the screen. It adds no route to the PC, needs no permission, and leaves the ordinary Jarvis chat that uses your PC exactly as it was. Honest limit: the in-app **Import** (system file picker) was not exercised - the test models were copied over adb - so that path is NOT-TESTED, not a pass.
