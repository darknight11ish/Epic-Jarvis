- **The phone can run a small AI model on itself, in its own Local AI screen (2026-11-13).** A `.gguf` model you put on the phone answers there with no PC and no internet, behind a switch that ships OFF. The screen says "On-device model: runs on this phone, not your PC", never downloads a model, never loads one until you tap Load, tells you before you load it whether the phone has room, and shows each reply's measured tokens per second or says "not measured". It adds no route to the PC, needs no permission, and leaves the ordinary Jarvis chat that uses your PC exactly as it was. Honest limit: the native engine was proven on a real phone by the native lane's own standalone probe (about 19.9 tokens per second on a small Qwen model), but the Local AI screen itself has not yet been driven on a phone, so the app shows "not measured" until it is.
