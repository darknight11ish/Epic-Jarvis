- **The undo notice after an approved card times out after 15 seconds (2026-11-13):** the owner asked for the notification that says an action cannot be undone to disappear by itself. Before this, the one sentence `JarvisRuntime.decide` puts up after an approval (`"This cannot be undone."`, or a shorter sibling such as "Inbox tidy: 10 minutes to undo.") stayed on screen until something else replaced it. It now goes through `JarvisRuntime.showUndoNotice`, which keeps one 15-second timer (`UNDO_NOTICE_TIMEOUT_MS`), clears the notice only while it is still that same sentence (so an old timer can never wipe a newer, different notice), and restarts the 15 seconds when the same sentence is set again. Every other notice caller is unchanged: error and problem notices still persist until dismissed or replaced. Covered by the new `UndoNoticeTimeoutTest` in the phone's JVM suite - still up at 14.9 s, gone at 15.0 s, a newer notice survives an older timer, a repeated sentence restarts its own 15 s, and an error notice opens no timer - with the full suite green at 3,672 tests, 0 failures. A mutation that changes the timeout to 15.001 s and removes the identity check turns three of those tests red, so the assertions are live. No device was driven and the app on the owner's phone was not touched, so the visual behaviour on the handset stays unverified until the CI APK is installed.
