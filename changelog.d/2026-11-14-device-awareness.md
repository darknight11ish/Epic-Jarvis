- **Jarvis's device awareness is no longer phone-shaped (2026-11-14).** Asked "what devices are connected", it reads the paired-device store and says each device's name, its kind (phone, laptop or tablet), whether it is reachable now and whether it has its own approval key - read-only, no card, no change; an unreadable store says "I cannot read the device list on this PC just now" instead of inventing one. Asked how to add a laptop, tablet, iPad or browser, it now gives the real route instead of the model's invention: a `jdk1...` key made at this PC's own terminal with `py -3 jarvis_devices.py --add-device "<name>" --kind laptop` (or `tablet`), pasted into the pairing sheet of the page this PC serves at :4719 in that device's browser - and the key never goes in a chat, a note or a log. A phone keeps its two screens and QR card, and a second PC running the desktop app is told plainly that it is not supported yet (the thin client is designed, not built; a second PC's own copy would be its own Jarvis, with its own models). The model's own runtime note now says `devices: 2 (phone, tablet)` instead of `phone: connected`. Nothing here adds, removes, renames or retires a device: creating a credential stays at this PC's terminal, Remove stays on the Settings, Devices card, and that card can never be hidden. The feature row and `docs/JARVIS-API.md` section 90.7 say the same, and `backend/test_quick_wins.py` proves every shape, the unreadable store and the phrases that must remain no add/remove path.
