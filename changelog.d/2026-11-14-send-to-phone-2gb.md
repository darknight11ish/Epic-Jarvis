- **Send a video from your PC to your phone (2026-11-14).** The ceiling on a file sent from the desktop to the phone rises from **10 MiB to 2 GiB**, and a **"Send to my phone…"** button in Settings → folders starts one by opening a normal file dialog. You pick the file, approve ONE card that names it, and the phone finds it in Settings → "File from your PC" and saves it to Downloads. The file travels in 1 MB pieces, so a long video shows a progress line instead of one long wait, a dropped connection can retry the same piece instead of starting over, and a transfer only counts as done when the last piece arrives — the phone checks the whole file's SHA-256 before it saves anything. Nothing is downloadable until you approve the card, the card is still one per file and cannot be turned into a standing yes, and no file path ever reaches the phone. Files from the phone to the PC already had the 2 GB ceiling since 2026-11-13.
