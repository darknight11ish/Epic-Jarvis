- **Widgets wear their own colours (2026-11-14).** A widget you describe can now have its own look: an accent colour from eight named ones, three ways of being see-through, and a high-contrast version - said in words ("make it violet and see-through") or picked on the preview before you add it, and drawn the same way on your phone's home screen and in the desktop widget window. Every colour on the list is checked readable, so none of them can make a widget you cannot read, and nothing changes for the widgets you already have.
