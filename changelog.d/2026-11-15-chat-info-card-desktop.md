- **Each conversation in the Brain's History has a small "i" that opens one compact "About this conversation" card (2026-11-15, desktop).** The owner asked for an info menu on each conversation — *"if the chat was on which device (phone or laptop) and more"* — *"without this being overwhelming"*, and chose a small "i" with one card that closes itself, so the rows stay exactly as clean as they were. The card says the row's own facts, one line each: which app it was had in ("Had in the Jarvis bar on this PC.", "Had in the HUD window on this PC.", "Had on your phone." or "The PC did not say which app this was had in."), "Started: …", "Last used: …" (left out when it is the same minute as Started), the message count, and then only the marks the chat carries — "Some of it was said aloud.", the outside-text sentence, "Filed under: <name>" or "Not filed under a tag." while tag names may be read, "Pinned to the top." and "In Archived." A time the PC did not send says "The PC did not say when." rather than guessing, and a chat with no voice, no taint, no tag, no pin and no archive says only its device, its two dates and its count. Nothing is requested and nothing is written: no new route, permission, request or approval card, and nothing changes on the PC. It closes on Escape, on Close and on a click outside, and it reuses the one dialog the window already draws (`openTopicDialog`, whose generic part is now `openDialog`) rather than a second popup style. The phone half is a separate branch and uses these same words; the strings and the line-builder (`chatInfoCard`) live in `jarvis-desktop/src/history-view.js` so neither app can re-word the card alone. Proved by `tests/chat-info.mjs` (10 checks: the full card, the plain one with no marks, the "did not say when" case, each mark absent while false, and the tag line's three states) and `tests/chat-thread.mjs` (the real page: the button opens that row's card, Escape closes it, a click outside closes it), with `tests/history.mjs` and `tests/topics.mjs` still green for the row and the one dialog.
