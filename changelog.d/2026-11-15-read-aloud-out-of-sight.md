- **A read-aloud now carries on when you leave the app, PiP and the lock screen included, and long answers are read to the end (2026-11-15).** Pressing HOME, switching to another app, putting Atlas in picture-in-picture or locking the phone used to stop a read in progress. It no longer does: a media-playback foreground service keeps the voice going for the rest of the answer and puts one line in the shade with "Stop talking". A long note used to stop part-way as well - a 24-sentence cap ended the read, and a paragraph with no full stop was one slow request, which is what the gaps were. Long answers are now read to the end, in pieces the PC can synthesise quickly, with the next piece fetched while the current one is spoken. Nothing about what may be read changes - a locked phone, App lock and a stale link still refuse to start one.
