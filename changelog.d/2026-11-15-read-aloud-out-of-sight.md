- **A read-aloud now carries on when you leave the app, PiP and the lock screen included (2026-11-15).** Pressing HOME, switching to another app, putting Atlas in picture-in-picture or locking the phone used to stop a read in progress. It no longer does: a media-playback foreground service keeps the voice going for the rest of the answer and puts one line in the shade with "Stop talking". Nothing about what may be read changes - a locked phone, App lock and a stale link still refuse to start one.
