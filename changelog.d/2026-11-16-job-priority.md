- **A background job can be moved to the front, by one sentence (2026-11-16).** The owner asked to "prioritize it through chat". Each job row now carries a priority - low, normal (the default, and what every row written before this became) or urgent - and the queue takes the highest first, then the same `due` order it always used, so a database of ordinary jobs behaves exactly as it did before. "What jobs do I have" numbers the list in the order the queue runs it and names each job's priority; "make job 2 urgent", "do job 1 first", "make job 2 normal" or "lower job 2" moves one, and the answer echoes the job's label because the list can shift between the read and the write; "which job runs next" answers from the queue's own query. A bump grants nothing: it never runs a job before its time, never widens what the job may do, and a job parked for the owner's approval still waits for it. The owner may move a job and a model may not - no route and no model tool reaches the setter. Both apps still do not draw the priority; that is a later PR, and the field is already on `GET /api/jobs` for them.
