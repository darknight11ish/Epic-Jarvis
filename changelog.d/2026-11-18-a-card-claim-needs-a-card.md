- **A reply that says an approval card was raised is now checked against the cards the turn really raised (2026-11-18, DEF-089).** The field test measured the worst version of "do not claim more than the evidence supports": four live replies told the owner "I have raised the approval card" while `GET /api/pending` was empty before and after the turn, and no tool had run. The existing claim families could not see it - `CLAIMS_DONE` needs one of its own verbs after "I've" and "raised" is not one, and a card is not one of `_CLAIM_OBJECT`'s nouns - so the exact sentence passed every check. The new `jarvis_claims.card_claim` / `card_correction` covers the four measured phrasings ("I have raised the approval card", "I've raised a card", "Check your pending approvals", "I've sent that for your approval") plus the plain "Raised a card for you." / "Created an approval request." / passive "An approval card has been created for you." shapes, and is backed by the **turn's own card count** - `_TurnWatch.cards`, the same number `CARDS_PER_TURN` is enforced with and incremented by every path that puts a card on screen - never by the model's words. The correction says what is true and what to do, not just "I did not": *"no approval card was raised in this answer: nothing is waiting for your approval, and no card was shown in it. Ask again in a new message if you want me to try again."* The live waiting list (`jarvis_gate.pending()`, the same read `jarvis_briefing` makes) is consulted for two things only - the sentence may say "nothing is waiting" only when the list really is empty, and a card already waiting leaves the answer alone - and a caller that cannot see the count (`cards=None`) is never corrected rather than guessed at. It rides the existing `postscript_lines` / `_claim_corrections` list as kind `claim`, the mechanism both the local answer and the cloud relay already use, and the cloud lane passes `cards=0` because it offers no tools and cannot raise one. Questions, offers, futures, conditions, negations, "already/earlier", quoted text and code spans are all left alone. Tests: `backend/test_smarter_answers.py` (`t_a_card_claim_needs_the_turn_to_have_raised_one` - the measured shapes corrected with no card and nothing waiting, backed by a card raised this turn, left alone by a card already waiting, unknown turn untouched, one line not per sentence, every exemption; and `t_the_card_claim_gets_the_line_in_a_turn` - a real `run_local_turn` on the row's sentence, then `postscript_lines` with `cards=0` including a fake gate whose pending list is empty, `cards=1`, and pending non-empty) and `backend/test_cloud_honesty.py` (the same line on the cloud lane). Measured against `origin/main`'s own modules before this change: `claims_done` and `unbacked_claim` both answered False for the row's sentence, `surface_correction` answered `""`, and `postscript_lines` returned `[]` - so every new case would have failed. Deliberately NOT covered, so the close is not read as more than it is: the same reply also said "This is just a field test - no cards go through right now" on the turn that *did* raise card `640e98b6b984`, and that mirror false claim (a denial that cards work while one existed) is a different direction this guard does not correct; and the patterns are finite, so a phrasing none matches would still pass uncorrected - the missed-claim direction this module always chooses over a wrong correction.
