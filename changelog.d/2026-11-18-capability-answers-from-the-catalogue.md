Would you like me to show you how to do that?"* — while the catalogue carried the whole answer: the `document-from-phone` row's own `where` is the exact steps (*"On the phone: Settings, Folders Jarvis may look in, then Send a file from your phone..."*) and, since 2026-11-15, its aliases include "send a file from my phone to my pc". TWO causes, fixed separately. (1) **`POST /api/chat` never consulted the catalogue for this sentence.** `jarvis_quick.answer_turn` runs the fixed phrase grammar first and, when it has nothing, the local model answers — and `_CAP_LEAD`, the capability-question lead, deliberately did not take "can you ..." Only the ONE measured shape — a "can you" file transfer *to this PC*, the direction no tool of ours starts (the phone's own Settings picks the file) — now strips the lead, so `send_file_to_phone` (the other direction, a real tool) keeps its own path. (2) **The direction rule gave up instead of choosing the honest row.** With a lead that did match (*"Can I send a file from my phone to my PC?"*), `file-from-pc` scored highest because its own id says "file from pc"; `_cap_direction_ok` rejected it as the opposite direction, and `_cap_resolve` returned None rather than take `document-from-phone`, which is the row that names that door. It now drops direction-wrong rows and answers from the best direction-correct one; a question that says "to my PC" also rejects a row that sends "to your phone". The owner's measured words are a catalogue alias now (`features/features.json` — the catalogue was regenerated, never hand-edited) and an exact alias of a row's content words names that row. The same report said Jarvis told him "it's on your screen" about something it had not put on screen: `jarvis_claims.surface_correction` is a new claim family in the EXISTING postscript shape (`backend/jarvis_agent.postscript_lines`, the same list the nothing-done and scheduler-refusal lines use), checking "It's on your screen.", "I've opened it on your screen." and "It's been sent." against the turn's own tool steps — a real showing/opening tool (`retirement_whatif` writes its table on the owner's screen, `product_check` opens a page, `conversation_open`, the acting tools) or a real sending tool backs it, and a bare-pronoun claim with neither gets one plain correction line. Questions, offers, future sentences and anything "already"/"earlier" are left alone, because a wrong correction after a true sentence is worse than a missed one. No route, no card, no auto-approval and no state read: reading the catalogue is not a gated action, and both fixes answer only a turn nothing else owns.
