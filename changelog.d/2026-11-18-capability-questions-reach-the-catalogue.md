- **A question about an ability Jarvis really has now reaches its catalogue row, not the local model (2026-11-18).** An audit of the answer layer measured 74 of 183 catalogue rows reachable only by quoting an exact name, and 21 of the owner's 180 measured questions still missing their row - six of them falling straight to the local model, which is free to answer "I can't" about something that exists. Three measured causes are fixed in `backend/jarvis_quick.py`'s capability lane. First, `_explicit_feature_question` short-circuited on the word "and" before trying its own explicit shapes, so every feature question whose subject contains "and" went to the model - 49 catalogue rows, including all six measured fall-throughs ("typing", "chat-thread", "memory-people", "stale-link", "data-sandbox", "trust"). The short-circuit was redundant: the two-part matcher already ran earlier and this lane runs last in `match()`, so it could not be protecting an action path. The same lane also takes the speaker's frame off the subject ("a feature that lets you type..."), which was turning two of those six into a false "I do not have that one". Second, three measured false-denial questions now answer from their row's own words and its own rule: "Can you send a file from my PC to my phone?" (the `file-from-pc` row - the mirror of the one-way shape the file fix of 2026-11-18 already took), "Can you read my phone notifications?" (`phone-notifications`, including its off-by-default card rule) and "Can you add up my spending?" / "Can you check my bank export?" (`spending`, "Plain code on the PC adds it up"). Each is claimed only in the lane that runs after every action matcher, and each lead is only the words of its own question - a bare "can you ..." capability lead would claim "can you write a nice email for my boss?" as `email-send`, which `test_chat_honesty.py` pins to the writer's own path, so no generic "can you" lead was added. Measured with the real matcher (no model): all six measured fall-throughs now reach their rows, 7 of the 21 measured misses close (the sixth is "interrupt", which the "and" guard also hid), and 14 remain - each claimed by another deterministic lane or resolving through a neighbouring row, which this change does not touch. A before/after sweep of 4,488 feature questions shows only improvements: 80 sentences that reached no row now answer from the right one, two that answered `phone-notifications` now answer `phone-apps`, and no action path changed.
