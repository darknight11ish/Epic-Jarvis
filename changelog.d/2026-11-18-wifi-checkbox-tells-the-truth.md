(or "The auto Wi-Fi reconnect is already on. Nothing was changed." after the owner clicked it) over an unticked box, and the owner reported exactly that. The page now has ONE reader of the PC's yes-or-no - `enabled`, the field `jarvis_wifi.state()`, the Settings route and the chat/Live answer all use - and both the tick and the sentence come from it, so they can no longer name different states; an answer this app cannot read is said as unknown and left unclickable rather than drawn as off, and a PC whose saved setting is damaged says that instead of claiming a state. The row's own note now reads "On by default, as you asked" rather than "On to start", so it cannot be mistaken for the current setting.
