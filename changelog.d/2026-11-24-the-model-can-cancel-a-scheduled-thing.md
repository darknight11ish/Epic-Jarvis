- **The model can cancel a scheduled thing (2026-11-24).** The scheduling review's fifth fix: the model could set a timer, an alarm, a reminder and a to-do item and could never take one back - the owner had to open the app. There is one new tool, `cancel_scheduled`, and it adds no route and no approval card, because a delete only makes things quieter. It calls the scheduler's existing delete path with the `id` `coming_up` hands back, or - when the model has only the words - with the job's own words, matched case-insensitively against the same live list `coming_up` shows. Exactly ONE job is ever deleted: no match and more than one match both delete nothing and answer in plain words ("I could not find a scheduled thing called ...", "More than one matches ... - say which one"). It is in the timers group, on the Tools page, on the Reach page, and its gate row is `auto`. Proved by three new cases in `backend/test_schedule.py` (by id; by words with a second job left alone; an ambiguous match deletes nothing), plus `test_gate_tool_actions.py`, `test_asks_first.py` and the repair tool's own suite.
