- **Full note control: copy, merge, delete one section, and empty the vault's trash behind one card (2026-11-25).** Atlas now has the four note verbs it was missing. **Copy** duplicates one note (card-free when you write it yourself in the editor, exactly like create; one `edit_vault_note` card naming the source and the new title from the chat or the phone). **Merge** folds one to twenty notes into a survivor you name: one `delete_vault_notes` card says which note survives, names every note that goes and how many lines it gains, each absorbed body lands under an invisible marker, and the other notes move into the vault's `.trash` - never written over. **Delete a section** stays on the existing customize route and its card now says how many lines go and which line numbers, so it cannot be accepted blind. **Empty the vault's trash** is the one note action that really destroys bytes, so it has its own gate action, `empty_vault_trash` (`no`/`local`), whose card lists every file with its age in days, the count, the size and "this cannot be undone"; it refuses rather than half-emptying past twenty files, and an empty trash answers "already empty" with no card at all. A `.trash` worth mentioning gets one quiet line in the note-status and vault-location answers - only past 50 MB, 100 files or 90 days, never acting, never twice. Four measured routing bugs are fixed in the same change: "can you merge two notes" was answered in pull-request words (and a pull-request sentence can no longer reach the note lane), "find my notes about the garden" searched the literal "the garden" (one leading article or possessive is now dropped, and only one, so "the day after tomorrow" keeps its second "the"), the four ways of asking which notes are pinned reached no lane or the wrong one, and "add this to the top of my Shopping note" asked what to add to a note called "top of my Shopping".
