- **A "Simple" HUD layout is the first built-in preset (2026-11-25).** The owner asked on 2026-11-10: "also add a simple layout and have it at the top of layout presets for jarvis hud on the desktop. have this be simple with just the essential tools and not too much customization or options". The new built-in id `simple`, named "Simple", is the first row in every list that draws one: `jarvis-desktop/src/hud-presets.js`'s `LAYOUT_CHOICES`, `hud-layout.js`'s `PRESETS`, `contract/hud-presets.json` and `hud_layout.rs`'s `BUILT_IN_PRESETS`. The seven existing presets keep their ids, their words and their relative order; the desktop now has eight. Its composition is the essentials only: the four protected cards (Stop everything, the Atlas bar, approvals, voice controls) plus the conversation and its ask box. The navigation, lanes, Systems, Galaxy, Reminders, memory, calendar, waiting count, telemetry, tools, brain stats, budget and voice stats cards are NAMED and OFF, because Edit HUD puts an unnamed movable card back on the glass and would otherwise turn Simple into a full deck on the first visit. It reuses the page's own `single-stage-bento` shell at comfortable density, so both side rails are closed drawers and the stage keeps the page-owned AI Mode, Open Brain and Settings doors; no page HTML or CSS changed, and the three copies of `hud-layout.js` and `hud-presets.js` stay byte-identical. **It is not the new default:** `DEFAULT_PRESET_ID` is now explicitly `"cockpit"` instead of `PRESETS[0].id`, so a saved layout that names a preset, an old `jarvis.hud.layout` value and an unreadable or wrong-version document all mean exactly what they meant before. New `jarvis-desktop/tests/hud-simple-layout.mjs` proves the first-row order in all four lists, the six-card composition, every heavy surface named and off, Cockpit still the default, the page applying Simple with both rails off the glass, and (added 2026-11-25) an owner-style selection: a page that boots on Cockpit is switched by clicking Simple in the one Presets menu, the click saves the Simple document through the page's own store, and the next boot is Simple. `hud-layout.mjs`, `hud-presets-names.mjs`, `hud-shell.mjs` and `hud-window.mjs` are updated to the intended eight-preset state with assertions edited rather than deleted. README and `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` say eight built-in layouts. **Limit:** the page evidence is headless Chromium, not the installed WebView2 window; the desktop app was not rebuilt or deployed, so the real-window pixels stay a field-test row.
