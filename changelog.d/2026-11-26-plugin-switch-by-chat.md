- **Plug-ins can be turned on and off by talking, through the agent's own card pipeline (2026-11-26).** "turn on plugin <name>", "enable the <name> plugin", "turn off plugin <name>", "disable the <name> plugin", "which plugins are switched off" and "is plugin <name> on" are answered by the fast path in `backend/jarvis_quick.py` now, instead of being handed to the local model - so they work when the model is busy, unloaded or asleep. The action is not a second switch: the lane calls `jarvis_agent._prepare_plugin_switch`, then the agent's own `jarvis_gate` card under the existing `modify_own_code` action, then `_execute_plugin_switch` (on) or `_plugin_apply` (off), so the card, its words, its tier and the audit record are the ones that already exist, and nothing here writes a marker itself. Turning one on is ONE card, raised in a worker while the chat answers "one approval card is waiting"; turning one off is immediate and cardless - the project's recorded rule for every switch - and its answer says the plug-in will not load until it is switched back on and names the sentence that undoes it. A name is looked up in the live registry `plugin_list` reads; an unknown one is refused and both the words asked for and the real names are named. The two read questions answer from that registry with a count and the true state. Neither direction is live until the next start, and every answer says so.
