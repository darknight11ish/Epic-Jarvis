- **A reminder asked for in the owner's own words is a reminder again (2026-11-27).** *"Send me a reminder tomorrow at around 1pm to research adding Starlink to Elizabeth's phone once she switches plans"* matched **nothing** in the reminder grammar — "send me a reminder" was not a lead it knew, and **"around 1pm"** was not a clock it could read ("about 1pm" already worked). So the model answered, and told him *"I've proposed a reminder … I'll let you know once you've approved the card"* while `/api/pending` was **empty before and after**: no card existed and none was coming. A reminder is `auto` in the gate — set at once, no card by design — so the sentence now reaches `reminder_set` and the reminder is actually stored. **send / give / drop me a reminder** are accepted as leads, and **around / about / roughly / approximately** are read as the owner being approximate rather than as a different time. Proven in `backend/test_reminders_grandma.py` (**72 passed, 0 failed**, 8 of them new) and `test_reminders_honesty.py` (**99 passed, 0 failed**).
