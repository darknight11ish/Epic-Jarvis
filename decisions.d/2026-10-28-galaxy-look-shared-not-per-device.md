- **2026-10-28 — The Galaxy's look becomes a shared setting, reversing the written per-device rule, because the owner asked for his devices to look like one another:** the owner's words were *"including changing the jarvis face model and galaxy models"*, said one sentence after he asked that the voice be the same on the desktop and the phone - so this is the appearance half of "the same Jarvis wherever I look at it", not a new idea of mine. **What it replaces, in writing:** `galaxy-presets.js`'s own comment said the look was "a DEVICE display choice, in the same place and spirit as the theme ... never sent to the PC and never written to the backend", and `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` §8 (2026-10-27) recorded that the eight looks "write ONE `localStorage` key (`jarvis.galaxy.preset`), add no route, no command and no phone surface". That design was honest but it had a gap the owner could see: no device can read another device's `localStorage`, so a look chosen on the PC could never reach the phone, and the phone's Galaxy picture was always the same shape whatever the PC drew. **What it becomes:** `galaxy` is one more key in the `appearance` document the face already uses - same merge (a key that is present replaces, a key that is absent keeps), same server-stamped `updated`, same `appearance` event, no approval card - and the HUD's Look switch writes it through ONE new narrow Rust command (`hud_set_galaxy_look`), proved against `galaxy-presets.js`'s own list inside the existing `hud-faces` set. The server reads the look ids from that module rather than keeping a list of its own, and refuses an id no renderer knows instead of storing a choice that would be in effect nowhere. **The phone draws the look**, which is what makes the shared key mean anything: seven layouts ported from `galaxy-presets.js` into `GalaxyLayouts.kt` and held to the JavaScript's exact numbers by a regenerated contract fixture (`tools/gen_galaxy_picture_cases.py --check` joins CI) plus `GalaxyPictureTest`, and an Appearance-screen picker so a look chosen on the phone is the look the PC draws. **What is deliberately NOT reversed:** the desktop theme, the face's sharpness/frame rate/speed and the face's size on each screen stay per device - they are tastes about one screen and neither app can draw the other's - and the phone still cannot draw the desktop's default `constellation`, because that is the PC's own physics simulation. Rather than substitute silently - the failure `docs/APPEARANCE-SYNC-PROPOSAL.md` §2 calls the worst of the three outcomes, "a phone quietly showing one face while the desktop shows another" - the phone draws its zen ring for that one and the Galaxy plate says in words which look is on screen. **Honest limits of this decision as it lands:** the Brain window's own look switch still writes the device key, so it follows a change on its next load rather than live - a named follow-up, not a silent difference; and the two-way sync is proved by tests (the backend's, the Rust command's, the JVM's and the regenerated fixture), not by driving two real devices, which stays a field-test row.
