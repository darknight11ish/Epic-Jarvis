- **2026-10-28 — One voice, one setting, and it is the one that is heard:** The owner reported, in his own words: *"when i change the jarvis voice in the android app it still stays the same in jarvis live in the android app. it needs to be consistent, including across gemini on desktop and android. if i change voice on desktop jarvis it should change it on android jarvis and so forth."* The cause was a second, hidden decider: `jarvis_speech.tts_voice()` asked the chosen personality first (`jarvis_manner.voice_for_speech`, added with the personality presets on 2026-10-23) and returned the preset's fallback voice and pace **before** it ever read `jarvis_voices.speaker()` — so with a preset chosen (his PC has "Classic British Butler" with the voice lock at its default, ON) both apps' Voices screens, `voices/state.json` and the `voices` event all said one voice while every spoken sentence, Live's included, was made in another. Live was never a second path: the phone's Live reply asks the same `/api/voice/say`. Decided: the built-in voice and the speaking speed are ONE PC-wide setting (`voices/state.json`), every spoken sentence on this PC is made in it (`jarvis_speech.tts_voice()` reads `jarvis_voices` and nothing else), and a chosen personality **applies** its voice INTO that one setting (through `jarvis_voices.set_speaker_name`, the same function the two apps' pickers call) instead of overriding it at speech time — so the screen, the event and the sound are one value whichever app or person changed it last, and a voice changed in either app is heard on both. Turning "switch Jarvis's spoken voice" on now applies the preset's voice straight away rather than waiting for the next personality switch, and the answer names the voice, so nothing changes silently. A temporary chat's personality still changes words only: the voice is PC-wide, and a chat that is not saved must not change the machine. Two small losses, said plainly rather than hidden: a preset's own pace (`voice_speed`, 0.94–1.1) is no longer applied, because a pace on this PC is one of three choices (slower, normal, faster) and a preset's own lands on "normal"; and the phone's Live screen gains one line naming the voice it is speaking in (and saying plainly when the PC has no voice engine and Live is using the phone's own offline voice, which no PC setting can change) — the desktop's Live needs no such line, because Settings → Voice is on the same machine. No new route, no approval card (a voice is cosmetic, like the speed and the manner), no client-side speech-to-text, and nothing recorded.
