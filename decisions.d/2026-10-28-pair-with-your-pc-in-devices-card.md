- **2026-10-28 — "Pair with your PC" goes inside Settings, Devices, not as a Settings row of its own:** The owner reported a Pixel 9 that would not pair and a Settings screen with "nothing for pair/reconnect to the PC". The gap was real, and the smallest honest fix was not a new `item(key = ...)` row: it is a Primary action inside the existing Devices card, drawn in both of that card's branches. Three reasons. First, that card is where the phone already talks about pairing - its own sentence tells the owner to "press Pair a phone in Settings, Devices, on your PC" - and it is the card Settings search already surfaces for the word "pair", so the button lands where someone looking for it is already standing. Second, the card's only pairing-adjacent control today is "Unpair & clear the token", which destroys the very thing the owner was trying to establish; putting the safe action directly above it, in the same card, is the pairing of opposites that was missing, and it keeps the destructive one last. Third, a new row would have moved `SETTINGS_ITEM_INDEX`, the jump list, the search labels and `jarvis_settings_registry.py`'s section table - four hand-kept records that `SettingsJumpTest`, `SettingsSearchTest` and `backend/test_settings_registry.py` hold in step - to say something the Devices card was already the right place to say. Nothing is loosened: the button starts the same re-pair the Platform checks screen already starts (`repairing = true`), it opens the same pairing screen, pairing still needs the PC's own card approved with Windows Hello, the old token is put back if the new pair does not connect, and no token is sent anywhere new - the phone still only ever sends it to the PC it pairs with. The one wording this replaces is the card's claim that a PC it had not reached "does not have a device list yet": `Devices.notShown` now tells "cannot reach your PC right now" apart from "reached a PC too old to serve the list", because the first was being reported as the second on the one card that could have said what to do instead.
