- **2026-10-28 — The personality presets are one PC-wide list, chosen on both apps; only the editor stays on the desktop:** the owner asked "make sure the presets for jarvis personality and other presets are consistent too", after the voice fix that same day. The presets themselves were already one state (`jarvis_manner.py`, one `manner.json`, one `_merge_setting` writer), but the phone's "How Jarvis talks" card was still the older Warm/Plain pair, so it marked "Plain" as in use whenever the PC was on any preset other than Warm Collaborator (`manner` is derived from the active preset), and a tap on "Plain" sent `{"manner": "plain"}`, which the PC's legacy branch turns into `tactical_ops` and also resets the four dials - a desktop choice silently replaced from the phone. Now both apps draw the same `GET /api/manner` `personalities` array (ids, titles, descriptions, order), mark the PC's `personality` and nothing else, and switch with the same one field `{"personality": "<id>"}`; the PC also rings one `manner` event from its one writer, so an open card follows a change made elsewhere. **Still one-sided on purpose:** the four tuning dials, the "My personalities" save/delete form and Hear Sample are the desktop Settings window's, because writing a personality by hand is deep configuration and the dials are where a character is tuned rather than chosen; the phone names that in one line instead of drawing a second, weaker humour control. A client must never switch a preset with a legacy `{"manner": ...}` word - docs/JARVIS-API.md §27.7 and docs/ARCHITECTURE.md §8 now say so.
