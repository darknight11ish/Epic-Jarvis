- **2026-10-28 — A settings search answers with the sections that NAME what was typed, and a fixed overlay must reserve its own room:** Two rules came out of Gemini's `F4` on the phone (2026-10-28), both measured there. **Search:** the phone's Settings box filters in the screen's own draw order and never ranks, so a word found in a section's printed prose was as good as a word on a button - typing "pair" returned VOICE, APPEARANCE, DEVICES, because the first two print "Pair with your desktop first - these settings live on it." while Devices carries the button "Pair with your PC". The rule now: a section's heading, the names of the controls drawn on it and the search index's own synonyms are the words it NAMES; its printed sentences are not, and a mention-only match answers ONLY while no section names what was typed (so "pair" answers Devices alone, and "pair with your desktop" still finds the two sections that print it). This replaces "every field is searched as one bag of words, and the screen's order decides the order of the answers". It does not change the four match tiers, `rankPart`, the shared cross-app fixture, or what a section carries; it makes the phone's answer depend on the words the section uses for a thing rather than on where the card happens to sit. The desktop already sorts matches by rank (`compareMatches`) and the phone's own `FindAnything` already scores its rows; this is the Settings box catching up, by the one mechanism a fixed-order `LazyColumn` can offer. **Room for an overlay:** a control drawn over a scrolling screen takes no room from it, so the screen has to keep the room - and the room must be MEASURED, not typed in. The phone's "Needs your OK" line wraps the PC's own card title, so its height changes with the card; `MainActivity` measures the drawn line and `SettingsScreen` adds that height, the line's 12 dp edge gap and 10 dp of air to its own bottom padding (`BottomReserve`). The rule for the next screen that gains a control: reserve the drawn height plus the gap, never a guessed constant, and reserve nothing when the control is not drawn. The overlap it replaces, on the installed APK: the Unpair button at `[90,2058][990,2202]` and the line's top at `y=2064`.
