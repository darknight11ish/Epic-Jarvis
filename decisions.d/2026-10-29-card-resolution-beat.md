- **2026-10-29 — A waiting card is re-read on a 30-second beat on both apps, a failed read is recovered by the next one, and a phone decision says "phone" (narrows the 2026-10-27 "no second poller" rule):** The owner's report: *"when a decision/approval card has already been addressed (approved or denied) on either the Android app or the desktop program, make sure the Android widgets (and every other surface that shows a card/notification) update to reflect that - no stale card left showing, no dead Approve/Deny on a resolved card."* Decided: every surface still takes its queue from the ONE read (`GET /api/pending`), and that read is now also taken on a clock - once every 30 seconds, and **only** while a card is actually waiting on a CONNECTED link, on the phone (`QueueBeat.due` → `refreshPending`) and on the PC (`stream.rs`'s `queue_beat_due` → `refresh_pending`) - because the `approval` event every surface relied on is a poller diffing ids on the backend's own pump: it publishes only on change and catches its own exceptions, so a failed read, a backend restart or a pump that is not running rings nothing, and the phone had no fallback at all (a decided card stayed on the home screen with a Deny that could only answer "no longer waiting"). The rule is the SAME TWO conditions the phone already used - a card waiting, and a connected link - and `stale` is deliberately NOT one of them: a failed read is exactly what makes a link stale, and the next successful read is the recovery, so a `!stale` gate switches off the only retry that can clear it (the desktop's first version did that, and one transient `/api/pending` failure held Approve until the hourly reconnect while Deny still worked). A successful read on a connected link now clears `stale` from one shared rule (`queue_read_landed`), which is what the `approval` event already did. This NARROWS the 2026-10-27 decision's "no second poller" to what that decision was actually about: a *background* refresh that wakes a phone to fetch a queue nobody is showing, or a poll of an idle PC. This beat starts no service and wakes no widget, reads nothing when the queue is empty, and can neither approve nor deny - it is the same 30-second safety net the HUD page has carried since before that decision (`jarvis_hud.html`'s `setInterval(() => apprPoll(), 30000)`), now the same number on all three surfaces so "how stale can a card be" has one answer, and it is the documented exception to `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` §6's "from the pump and nowhere else" (two 30 s pending reads run on the PC while the HUD is visible and a card waits, deliberately: either can fail without silencing the other). A 2026-10-30 adversarial review hardened the two silent-failure halves and fixed the bound: the PC's beat cannot move the stream's 70-second silence deadline any more (the deadline is absolute, `last_chunk_at + SILENCE_TIMEOUT`, and only a real chunk moves it, so `"Jarvis went quiet"` still fires on a dead socket), and on the phone a queue response is applied only if no newer read has started (`PendingRefreshOrder`), so a slow pre-decision GET cannot re-add a card the owner just answered; and the honest bound is **30 seconds while the app is running and the link is live, up to 30 minutes for a killed phone's widget, and the next event or reconnect while the desktop's stream is down** (a stale-but-connected link recovers on the next beat). Two consequences decided with it: the approvals widget carries `updatePeriodMillis="1800000"` (the board widget's period since it was written), because a RemoteViews drawn by a killed process is otherwise held by the launcher for ever and a cold redraw must say "Not checked yet" rather than draw a card it cannot vouch for; and the phone sends `"by": "phone"` on BOTH `/api/approve` and `/api/deny` from one constant (`SignedApproval.BY`), closing the open disagreement in `docs/JARVIS-API.md` §7 - the route does read `by` and stores it as `decided_by`, so the phone's old `{"id"}`-only body inherited the `"hud"` default and every phone decision was recorded as the browser HUD's. Deliberately NOT done: no new route, no push channel, no service started from a widget draw, no client-side "clear expired" control, and nothing that decides anything by itself.
