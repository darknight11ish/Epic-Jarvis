- **2026-10-29 — A connect question is answered from this PC, never with a secret in chat:** The HUD field test of 2026-10-29 caught four answers that were wrong about this PC, and one of them was dangerous: asked "can you connect my calendar and email" and "what about connecting to a gmail account or google calendar?", Jarvis said connecting Google Calendar "requires a refresh token ... tokens are managed in your browser's signed-in account; I cannot retrieve them from here ... generate an OAuth token, then share the resulting code with me". The first sentence got the private-iCal card with the email half silently dropped; the second matched no fast path and reached the 8B model, which invented the rest. The rule now is the project's own rule 3, applied to these words: **a token, an auth code or a password is never asked for in a chat, and the two-way Google setup is not a secret exchange at all** - it is the one-time Google Cloud setup plus three commands on this PC (`py -3 jarvis_google_calendar.py client <client-id>`, `mode google`, `connect`), documented in `docs/GOOGLE-CALENDAR-SETUP.md`, with the sign-in returning to a loopback address on this PC. So a sentence that names a **Google calendar/account or email** is answered deterministically by `accounts_connect` (a read-only intent, no card, no connection) and names the four real paths: Google read/write (the CLI steps), the private iCal link on the secure connect card, GitHub on the same kind of card, and email as not built yet with its IMAP details typed in Settings, Accounts. It deliberately does not raise the iCal card for those sentences - one card cannot answer a question that names two accounts - while a plain "connect my calendar", "set up a calendar bridge for me" and "external calendars like google or outlook" keep the card they have had since 2026-10-28. Three more were fixed the same way, from the PC's own state: "change your voice to Ashby" says Ashby and Clara are the two voices made for Jarvis and not made on this PC, in the Voices screen's own `BLENDS_TO_MAKE` sentence, instead of dumping the built-in voice ids - the settings grammar's existing `settings_choice_bad` answer, not a second intent of its own (a blend the pack really offers still goes through the same cardless setter); "what voice are you using" reads `jarvis_voices.speaker_view()`, the same read the Voices screen makes; "how do I pair my phone" names Settings, Devices and "Pair a phone" on this PC and "Pair with your PC" on the phone, both buttons' own words. Two stale sentences went with them: the "Not connected yet" calendar answer no longer names a Google sign-in row that Settings does not have and keeps the `py -3 jarvis_google_calendar.py mode ical` step that chooses the read-only way, and the Appearance card no longer says the HUD always stays dark - the HUD follows the theme, Daylight included. Proved in `backend/test_hud_fieldtest_answers.py`, with `backend/test_calendar_answer.py`, `backend/test_connect_account.py` and `backend/test_google_calendar.py` updated for the sentences that changed hands. It touches no HUD file and no phone code: the HUD and the Kotlin app are another session's files.
