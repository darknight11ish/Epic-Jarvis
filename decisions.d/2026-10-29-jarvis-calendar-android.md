- **2026-10-29 — The phone's calendar draws only what the PC actually sent, and the widget keeps its place:** the owner's calendar decision of 2026-10-29 (a full Jarvis Calendar, local only, on by default and disableable; design brief `.dsh-scratch/JARVIS-CALENDAR-DESIGN-2026-10-29/REPORT.md`) reaches the phone as a `CALENDAR` screen, a Settings switch and a home-screen widget, and three things were decided on the Android side to keep it honest. **First, the read window is stated, not implied.** `GET /api/calendar?days=N` only ever answers from the PC's today forward (1-90 days), so a day before today, or a month past the end of what was sent, has not been read at all; the phone now asks for the full 90 days and says "Not read: your PC sent 3 November to 2 February, and nothing outside that is known" instead of drawing an ordinary empty day, and an unread day's number is drawn in the quiet colour. Saying "Nothing on this day" over a day nobody asked about would be inventing a fact, which is the one thing these screens must not do. **Second, the Repeat choices are the PC's own four** - none, daily, weekly, monthly - because `jarvis_local_calendar.RECURRENCES` refuses anything else with a 400: a fifth word ("Yearly" was in the first draft) is a control that looks like it worked and did not. **Third, the widget's own two rules.** It never disappears and never shows a blank square: switched off, it keeps its place and says "Jarvis Calendar is off · tap to turn it on", opening Settings at the calendar's row (the owner's decision D5); under App lock or "Hide memory lists and chat history" it draws one sentence and no title at all, not even a count, because the home screen is public; and on a stale link it says "Not up to date" rather than drawing yesterday's list as today's. Its date line comes from the PC's own window rather than the phone's clock, so just after midnight - before the launcher's half-hourly tick - the date and the events under it agree instead of disagreeing. The tap opens the calendar screen itself, by action, not the app's Home. Deliberately NOT in this slice, and said rather than implied: a week view (the brief's phone design is a month grid over a day list, and a second grid was not in it), reminders that ring (the owner's D3 stores `remind_minutes` and rings nothing, so the box says "Saved with the event. Nothing rings yet"), recurrence beyond the PC's four words, and any desktop or backend file - the HUD card, the four routes and the registry rows are the other slices'.
