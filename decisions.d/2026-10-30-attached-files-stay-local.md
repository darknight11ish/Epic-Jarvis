- **2026-10-30 — An attached file is read on this PC only, and that is enforced rather than promised:** an attachment's words are private local-only content, and four rules now hold it there. **The owner's own permission decides whether it is read:** the read is put through `jarvis_gate.check("read_files_readonly", …)` - the SAME row `file_read` and "Folders Jarvis may look in" use, the one the owner sees as "Ask me first" for reading files - BEFORE a byte is decoded; its card (only when that row says ask) shows the file's name and size and never its contents, `never` refuses with no card, and a gate that cannot answer refuses rather than reading (no second permission model, no new switch). **It never goes to a cloud lane:** `jarvis_router.choose(has_file=True)` answers gate "file" before the budget, the complexity check, the offer and the manual pick, and `_open()` strips the `attachment` field from every message on every relay hop, so even a request that ignored the gate cannot carry a byte of it out. **It is never stored in history or a backup in plaintext:** `jarvis_chat_log.strip_attachment_fields` takes the field off a copy of the request before the history module reads any message, so the encrypted row holds only the owner's own words - no byte, no base64, not even the file's name - and `jarvis_backup.py` leaves a settings file that somehow held a body out of the `.jbak` and names it in `excluded_settings` rather than carrying it silently. **The latch is the conversation's, not the PC's:** the turn marks the conversation that carried the file private through `jarvis_chat_log.note_outside` (the mark a picture's words set), NOT the process-wide `jarvis_gate.latch_taint`, so one attachment keeps that chat local without keeping every other conversation off the cloud lane. Proven by `backend/test_attached_file.py` (the gate is asked before the bytes; a file turn with a cloud lane enabled is local and the outbound payload holds no attachment and no earlier turn; the conversation is marked private), `backend/test_chat_log.py` (the stored row, decrypted, is the owner's words only) and `backend/test_backup.py` (the archived history row and every archive member are scanned) - docs/JARVIS-API.md section 163 is the contract.
