- **2026-10-30 — Put the timer on every card with a limited time:** The owner's ruling, verbatim: **"if there is a card with a limited time of any kind, be sure to put the timer on it."** It came from the same day twice over: his app-builder card ran out while he was reading it, and no screen had counted it down. Decided: a routine card whose REQUEST is still waiting carries the seconds it has left, and every screen counts them down - `backend/show-card-timer.patch`, applied after `no-deadline-cards.patch`, sends `expires_in` (whole seconds left until the row's own `waited_until`, never 0 and never negative, rounded up) and nothing at all for a ceremony card (pairing, an approval key, a restore) or for a request that has already ended; the 60-second grace is not counted down, because no request can act on the card in it, so a clock there would promise what the gate refuses. **What it supersedes, exactly:** only the DISPLAY half of the 2026-10-28 ruling ("if there doesn't need to be a time limit don't have one"), i.e. the sentence "no screen draws a countdown" and the removal of `expires_in` from the read. The CARD's life is unchanged and is still that ruling's: no card carries a deadline of its own, no card is refused because a countdown reached zero, nothing auto-approves, a Deny always works, and the read-time sweep, `still_waiting()`, `decide()`'s late-approval refusal and the 409 with its one plain sentence stay exactly as they were - the timer and the refusal now point at the same moment, which is the point. The desktop words it twice from one place (`countdownWords` in `card-words.js`, and the HUD page's `apprClockWords` held to it by `tests/expiry-parity.mjs`); the phone's own instrument readout ("EXPIRES IN 00:43" / "EXPIRING SOON" in the last minute, with the thin bar) lights up again and its last-minute rule is one pure function (`countdownReadout`) so a JVM test holds it. Not verified on a device or in a live deploy: the phone's Kotlin is compiled by CI only (`phone-unit-tests`), and the owner's gate takes the patch through `scripts/apply-patches.ps1` plus a backend restart, or through `tools/reconcile_live_backend.py` once it has a `show-card-timer.patch` delta rule - until then the live app draws the card with no timer, exactly as it does today.
