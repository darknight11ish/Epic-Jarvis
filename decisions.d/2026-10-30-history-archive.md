- **2026-10-30 — Chats can be archived from the HUD/Brain and the phone, and delete keeps its confirm:** The owner asked on 2026-10-29 that chats can be archived and deleted, with a confirm step. Delete already existed, so this adds archiving as a **reversible local flag**, not a deletion: the PC's own encrypted history file gains a plain `archived_at` column (NULL while the chat is in the list), `POST /api/history/archive {"id": "<conversation id>", "archived": true|false}` sets or clears it, `GET /api/history?archived=1` lists the archived chats while the ordinary list shows only the ones still out, and every conversation row carries `archived: true|false`. It deliberately raises **no approval card and needs no confirm step**, unlike Delete, because it reveals nothing and sends nothing: no chat word is opened, sealed or rewritten, nothing leaves the PC, and Unarchive puts the chat back exactly where it was. It is the same class of change as a pin or a chat tag, and both apps read the one flag back from the PC rather than keeping a list of their own, so archiving on the phone and archiving on the PC cannot disagree. A fork made from an archived chat is not itself archived: it is a new chat the owner just made.
