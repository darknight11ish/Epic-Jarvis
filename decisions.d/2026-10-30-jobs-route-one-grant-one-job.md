- **2026-10-30 — Jobs start with no permissions and a grant is answered one job at a time:** Long Fuse's two new routes (`POST /api/jobs`, `POST /api/jobs/answer`, jobs-routes.patch; JARVIS-API §169) follow the owner's existing 2026-10-15 "finish what I already started" rule rather than widening it. Creating a job freezes exactly the capabilities the request names - and the desktop's Work pane names **none**, so a job started there runs with an empty frozen set and parks on its first need. Answering a parked job decides ONE grant for ONE job: it is spent by its next use, a second tap is refused, and a No stops that job at once (it does not sit saying "waiting for you" until the next heartbeat) and writes no rule anywhere - the next job needing the same action parks and asks again. There is no list form and no "allow all". Both routes live beside `/api/jobs/cancel` behind the same origin and token checks, are granted to the desktop in the one `brain-act` set, and are unreachable through the Brain's read allow-list.
