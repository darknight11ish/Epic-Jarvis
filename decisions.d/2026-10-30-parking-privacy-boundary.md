- **2026-10-30 — A parking estimate may send a point, and one place name the owner said — never the calendar:** `jarvis_parking.py` talks to two public services, so the boundary is written down rather than assumed. Overpass and Seattle's ArcGIS layer receive a latitude, a longitude and a radius and nothing else; Nominatim (the one lookup that necessarily carries words) receives the place name the owner himself typed or said, or - only when he asked about that event - the calendar event's LOCATION line, never its title, never a chat message, never a note or a saved fact. The calendar bridge deliberately does **not** geocode by itself: it quotes the venue back locally with the padded window and offers the exact sentence to say, because a calendar entry is private even when the venue in it is public. This replaces an implicit "it is probably fine" with a rule the suite can check, and it is the reason there is no `find_parking` model tool: the six-slot Live budget on the 8 GB card is worth more than a tool that must stay switched off, and `jarvis_quick.py`'s grammar answers the same sentence for zero model tokens.
