- **2026-10-30 — A parking estimate may send a point, and one place name the owner said — never the calendar:** `jarvis_parking.py` talks to three public services, so the boundary is written down rather than assumed. Overpass and Seattle's ArcGIS layer receive a latitude, a longitude and a radius and nothing else; Nominatim (the one lookup that necessarily carries words) receives only the place name the owner himself typed or said - never the calendar event's own LOCATION line, never its title, never a chat message, never a note or a saved fact. The calendar bridge deliberately does **not** geocode by itself: it quotes the venue back locally with the padded window and offers the exact sentence to say, because a calendar entry is private even when the venue in it is public. Every request refuses a redirect rather than following one to a host nobody chose, and while Lockdown is on `jarvis_quick._run_parking` answers in one plain sentence and sends nothing at all. The 24-hour local cache is a plain SQLite file beside the module, deliberately not backed up - its geocode keys do hold the place names the owner looked up - because restoring yesterday's prices would only put an old answer in front of today's question. This replaces an implicit "it is probably fine" with a rule the suite can check, and it is the reason there is no `find_parking` model tool: the six-slot Live budget on the 8 GB card is worth more than a tool that must stay switched off, and `jarvis_quick.py`'s grammar answers the same sentence for zero model tokens.
