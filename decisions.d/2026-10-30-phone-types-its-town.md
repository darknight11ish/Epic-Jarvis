- **2026-10-30 — The phone may type its own town, and the "do it on the PC" limitation goes rather than being explained away:** the owner asked to be able to put his town in "through the android app ... and not have it just limited to the desktop", with a screenshot of Appearance telling him to type it on the PC. That sentence was not an accident: the 2026-09-28 decision said "a town the owner types once on the PC (the phone gets it from the PC)", and the backend enforced it with `jarvis_owner_check.from_this_pc`, answering 403 to any other device so that a stolen token could not move where Jarvis thinks the owner lives. **Widened:** `POST /api/sky {"place": ...}` now accepts a town from either app (`request_place()` no longer consults `from_this_pc`; the parameter stays in the route's signature because the words may one day differ), `view()` says `can_set_place: true` to both apps, and the PC's own words invite the phone (`PLACE_DETAIL`/`PLACE_PHONE`/`PLACE_NONE`) instead of sending it to Settings. **Why this is safe enough to widen:** typing a town sends words only to the owner's own PC over the pairing link; the PC answers from the town list it already carries (`jarvis_sky_places.py`, offline, no geocoding service), keeps only the position rounded to 0.1 degree, and the name is never logged, audited or offered to the model. It adds no route, no egress and no card: the risk the old 403 addressed is a stolen token, and a stolen token can already move the town by asking the PC through the same pairing link when the phone is the thing stolen. **What it replaces, and what it deliberately does not do:** the phone's field waits for a live link (typing a town ADDS something, rule 4), while "Forget my town" stays at once; moving the town while Open-Meteo is on still switches Open-Meteo off, because its ONE card named the old position, and that is kept rather than eased; the desktop's own field and words are unchanged; and no other "type it on the PC" limitation is silently turned on by this entry - each one earns its own decision. Gemini's later ruling that the phone should send `X-Jarvis-Client: phone` rather than `hud` is a separate change and is not part of this one.
