- **2026-10-30 — An unclassified action is approvable with maximum friction, and CI still demands its row:** Gemini's Q2 ruling, after the app builder's build card could not be approved on the owner's own PC even while he was pressing Approve. The rule, in the owner's own terms: **if Jarvis has not written down what an action can do, it is given the worst case the gate has - it cannot be undone and it can leave this PC - it always asks you first, its card says so in one plain line, and you can still say yes, with Windows Hello on the PC or your phone's own lock.** Nothing auto-approves, nothing approves in bulk, no swipe decides it, and no app or settings switch may loosen it; a yes recorded without that human check is refused by the gate. On the CI side the rule is the opposite direction and stays strict: an action the shipped settings file lets run with nobody asked, and that has no explicit risk row, is a failure that names the action. This replaces the old fail-closed behaviour at runtime, where a card for an unknown action was physically unapprovable ("Jarvis has no written words for this action yet"): that was safe on paper and bricked a real feature on the owner's desk. It does not replace the 2026-10-26 rule that a prompt may only ask about something Jarvis can name - a CLASSIFIED action with no written words is still refused - and it does not give rows to the nineteen ask-tier actions that still have none; their cards keep the warning line instead of a proper sentence until somebody does that work per action.
