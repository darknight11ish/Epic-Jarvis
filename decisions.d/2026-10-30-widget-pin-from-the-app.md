- **2026-10-30 — The phone asks the launcher to put a Jarvis widget on the home screen, and never claims it did:** The owner asked, verbatim: *"i want them customizable and buildable by just chatting to jarvis, including the custom widgets by chatting with jarvis"*, and in the field-test instruction: *"jarvos can build a custom wodget through the android app i can add immediately to my android screen"*. Until this slice the app only printed the touch-and-hold route in its hint text, so "immediately" was still three manual steps in a launcher picker. Decided: a home-screen slot that has a saved widget offers **Put it on my home screen**, and the phone calls Android's request-pin API (API 26+, `AppWidgetManager.requestPinAppWidget`) for that slot's own board receiver. The API answers whether the **request was sent**, not whether a widget was placed, so the app's words are three and the asked one never reads as done: that widget is already on the home screen; the home screen was **asked** (a launcher may still show its own confirm, or refuse); or this launcher cannot add widgets on request, in which case the touch-and-hold route is given in words. It adds no route, sends nothing to the PC, raises no card, and changes nothing about which saved widget a slot shows - that stays the slot picker. The three slots, the picker's count and the three receiver names are held equal in one list (`JarvisWidgets.PIN_RECEIVERS`) by `JarvisWidgetsTest`, so a slot can never be offered without a receiver again. Not verified on a device: no phone or emulator at this desk - what is proven is that the app compiles, `assembleDebug` succeeds, and the JVM tests pin every sentence and every receiver; the pin is driven in the field test after deploy and stays NOT-TESTABLE until then.
