- **2026-10-31 — A real notes editor for the whole Obsidian vault, in both apps:** The owner was asked whether to build a read-only notes view and answered *"how about option 1 with actual editing?"*, then chose **"The whole vault, both apps"**. Decided: both apps get a Notes surface in the Brain (search the vault, open a note, edit, save), limited to `.md` and `.txt` files inside the configured Obsidian vault. **Every save raises ONE approval card** — the existing gate card, Approve or Deny, never an app-side approval; the File Guardian copies the old bytes before any write; a save whose starting sha256 no longer matches is refused with `conflict` and the current hash rather than overwriting someone else's edit; and **create, delete and rename are deliberately not in this slice**. The phone reaches the same four PC routes and never approves anything itself; its plate sits behind the existing lists' Show gate, with no new bypass. The backend half is a drop-in module (`backend/jarvis_notes_edit.py`, `edit_vault_note` at tier `ask`, four `/api/notes/*` routes) and the two app halves call it; the work is implemented and proved locally on branches and is **not deployed to the live PC** — the live apps do not show it until a deploy pass runs.
