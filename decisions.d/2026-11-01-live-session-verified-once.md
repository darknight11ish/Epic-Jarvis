- **2026-11-01 — A Jarvis Live session is verified once, and an answer it can say is said:** The owner's request, "once I actively verify my voice in a single jarvis live session, jarvis shouldn't need to re verify my voice", replaces the 2026-10-18 refinement in which one successful check bought a 120-second window or 16 short clips and then asked again. **What is cached, and for how long:** in the `Live` engine's own memory, three things and no audio - the clock time of the one successful strict check, the strictness it passed at ("very_strict" or "balanced"), and the identity of the enrolled print it passed against (its label, file size and write time - the print itself is never read). It lasts from that check until the session ends, and it is reset by `_trust_start` on every start, by `_trust_end`, by a failed or erroring check (`abort_trust`) and by a changed print (`_trust_print_changed`). Nothing is written to disk, no new secret is created, and a page reload or backend restart starts unverified. **Why that is not a weakening of the check:** the check was done, once, in this session, by the owner's own voice, at the owner's own setting - this caches a result rather than skipping the check itself. Nothing else moves: the first clip must still be a full sentence, the >= 2.0 s spot check still runs silently on every full sentence (which is what refuses a voice that is not the owner's and what a private answer needs before it may be read aloud), a failed check still voids the session at once, no approval, no lock and no permission rule is touched, and no answer becomes readable that a setting keeps on screen. The one trade is said plainly and is the same 1.5 s trade the 2026-10-18 decision took: a 1.5-2.0 s clip in a verified session runs without a speaker model, so a voice in the room that is not the owner's is treated as the owner for those clips, and their answers now keep the read-aloud flags the session's check earned (before this change those flags were lost, which is why Live said "It's on your screen" about an answer that used what Jarvis remembers, read aloud by default). **The alternative that was NOT taken:** skipping the spot check too, so that no speaker model ran for the rest of the session. That would have let any voice command Jarvis for up to two hours, and it would have lost the real check a private or remembered-fact answer needs before it may be spoken - it is the owner's call, not this change's, and it is not in this pull request.
