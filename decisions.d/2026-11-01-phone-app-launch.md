- **2026-11-01 — Jarvis may open the owner's own listed apps on the phone and start a media search in them, and the list is the permission:** The owner asked for Jarvis Live to open certain apps on their Android phone when asked, and to do things like "open YouTube Music and play The Weeknd". Decided: this is execution, not a new surface - the existing `control_phone` tool and `jarvis_android_control.py` gain `open_app`, `play` and the read-only `list_apps`, and the phone stays the hands while the PC stays the brain and the gate. The permission is a `[phone_apps]` table in `jarvis-framework.toml` (friendly name to package) that ships EMPTY; an app that is not in it cannot be opened, played or searched for, an empty list refuses everything with one plain sentence, and anything under `com.jarvis.` is refused so no tap can reach Jarvis's own Approve button. Chosen over a phone-side AccessibilityService allow-list (the separate app-control slice) because this needs no new Android permission, no new route and no second authority: `list_apps` answers from the same settings file, so the phone can never widen what the PC allows. `play` means "open the app on that app's own search address" and is offered only for apps whose address is documented (YouTube Music today); everything else can be opened and not searched, and the refusal says so. `open_app` and `play` are also exempt from the Jarvis-in-front guard, because both name the package in the command itself and a `com.jarvis.` package is refused before a command exists - the guard protects against a tap inside Jarvis's own app, and it would otherwise refuse this request every time it is asked FROM the phone, which is where Live is. One card per plan at the unchanged `ask` tier, every literal command printed, and the tool stays opt-in (`control_phone` is not in the shipped `[tools].enabled`); a list-only call or a refusal raises no card because there is nothing to approve. Recorded with the honest limit that opening a media app backgrounds a Live session on that phone, and that the intent's behaviour on the real device is verified on hardware, not in this repository.
