- **2026-11-01 — A small read-only approval is approved by the card alone, and the fingerprint is a setting:** The owner's own request: *"for small approvals like asking jarvis to search the web (for things like weather or the news or anything small) i would like to only have to approve the card, not necessarily use my fingerprint on top of approving the card. make this setting adjustable in settings or through chatting to jarvis."* Decided: the approval check gets a third value, `small` (`small` / `risky` / `every`), and **it ships as the default** because that is what the owner asked for. A card is small only when its action is on an explicit read-only list AND the gate's own risk row for it says it changes nothing, is classified, and was not rushed - so nothing unclassified, irreversible, rushed, or that writes, spends, sends, deletes or changes an account, a credential, a device, the network, an app or a permission ever qualifies, and the list is named action by action in `jarvis_owner_check.SMALL_ACTIONS` and `SecurityRules.SMALL_ACTIONS`. What it changes: on the PC the backend no longer shows its Windows Hello prompt for such a card approved from this PC (it was asking because `is_risky` calls anything outbound risky, and a web search is outbound); on the phone such a card is approved by the card alone and is not signed (`SecurityRules.approveTakesSignedPath`), which is safe only because the same rule exempts it from the device signature at `approve_check`; and "Every approval" still asks every time, on every card the rule does not exempt. **How to turn it back:** on the phone, Settings, Lock and fingerprint settings, "Fingerprint for approvals" - choose **Risky only** (today's rule, the small ones included) or **Every approval**; on the PC, say or type "check the small approvals too" (the `small_approvals` setting, through the existing `change_setting` tool) to put the check back on the small cards, and "leave the small approvals alone" for the default again - the loosening direction is this PC only, never a phone, because it is this PC's own check. Chosen over a per-card exception list or a guess from the card's words because the gate already carries a risk row for every action and the project's rule is that a security rule is read from the metadata, not inferred from prose. **What it costs, said plainly:** the phone's copy and the PC's copy are two settings with the same name and the same default, as every security setting in this project already is (the phone's lock is the phone's); changing one does not change the other, and the desktop's Settings card says which is which. Held by `tools/gen_risky_approval_cases.py` (the shared rows, now with `small`), `SmallApprovalContractTest`, `SecurityRulesTest`, and the new checks in `backend/test_owner_check.py` and `backend/test_settings_registry.py`.
