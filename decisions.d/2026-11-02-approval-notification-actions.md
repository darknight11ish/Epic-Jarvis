- **2026-11-02 — Approve, Deny and Review in Jarvis on an Android approval notification:** The owner asked, 2026-11-02: *"make sure i can approve jarvis cards through an android notification with a deny or approve button or tap too see more button if the summary that's short enough to fit into an android notification isn't long enough."* Decided, under Gemini's binding five-point envelope, that the phone's approval notification carries two actions and one redacted public version. **(1)** Deny may run from the lock screen - refusing without reading is the safe direction and was already allowed. **(2)** Every approve action is built with `setAuthenticationRequired(true)`, so Android withholds its `PendingIntent` until the device is unlocked; no Kotlin check is the wall. **(3)** Only a card that `SecurityRules.isSmall` exempts (the owner's 2026-11-01 request) and that the owner's setting does not ask about may submit directly; that route goes through the app's non-exported `EventService`, which waits for a live link, re-reads the live card, re-checks `isSmall` and the phone's privacy state, and then calls the existing `JarvisRuntime.decide`. Every write, config, send-file, phone-app or otherwise non-small card's Approve opens Jarvis on the focused card and runs the same `approveItem`/`BiometricPrompt` the card's own button runs, forcing the check even where the card alone would not have asked. **(4)** When the text the notification shows (title and body together) is over 240 characters, Approve is replaced by **Review in Jarvis** and Deny stays; the same applies to a PC-only card and to a proposal that needs one of several plans chosen, neither of which the phone may approve. **(5)** The notification stays `VISIBILITY_PRIVATE`, its public version says exactly **"Jarvis: 1 approval waiting"** with no title, summary or action name, its `sync()` cancels a notification when `/api/pending` no longer carries that id, and no secret is added. A redacted notification - App lock on (the default), hidden memory lists, or a phone with no secure lock - offers Review in Jarvis and Deny, never Approve: approving words the notification did not show is the same blind approval point 4 forbids, and the redaction itself is the owner's existing 2026-09-30 privacy rule (`SecurityRules.notificationsLocked`), not weakened here. App lock off is what shows the card's safe `notice` words in the shade and therefore what makes Approve appear. The policy lives in `service/ApprovalNotificationPolicy.kt` so the JVM tests hold it without a phone; the two routes both end at the existing `/api/approve`, so there is no backend change, no new route and no second permission path, and `notice.approve_ok` is still not read. This supersedes the 2026-09-26-era rule recorded in `ApprovalNotifier.kt` that an approval was never approvable from a notification. It deliberately does not build: a desktop/Windows notification for approvals, any bulk approve, any approve on a locked phone, any approve of a card the owner cannot see, or an approve that skips the fingerprint for a write. Evidence: `ApprovalNotificationPolicyTest` (19 checks) and the updated `AppProjectsTest`, `NotificationStyleTest`, `MainActivityLaunchTest` and `ApprovalDecisionTest` in the required `phone-unit-tests` job; the phone itself is NOT-TESTABLE at this desk.
