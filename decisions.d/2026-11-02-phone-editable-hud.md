- **2026-11-02 — The phone gets its own editable HUD, and the desktop's seven widescreen presets stay desktop-only:** The owner's instruction of 2026-11-01, "add building the real editor to the queue", is acted on by building the Android `A1`-`A4` slice into shipped code rather than leaving it a model nothing drew. **What decided it:** `HudLayout.kt` and `MenuPrefs` already existed on `main` with four ordered sections (`top_strip` / `hero` / `body` / `composer`), five read-only built-in arrangements, one `apply` reducer, a `normalize` for untrusted documents and a versioned JSON document - and a `git grep` for `hudLayout|applyLayout` under `ui/` returned nothing, so the owner could not reach any of it. **What the phone gets:** Home draws the saved layout (with the existing hide rules applied on top, so a hidden menu still empties its card), and an "Edit HUD" screen moves cards by drag, by click-only buttons and by TalkBack custom actions that all call the one reducer; the five built-ins stay read-only and a copy is what the owner edits; a pasted layout goes through the same safe reader a stored one does; `Done` is the single write. It is a display preference - the same class as the theme - so it raises no approval card, opens no route, sends nothing to the PC and needs no pairing. **Why the desktop is deliberately untouched:** the desktop's HUD is a 1280x820 widescreen cockpit whose named regions (`header`, `left_rail`, `stage_top`, `stage_main`, `right_rail`) and card ids exist only there, and its seven built-in presets are desktop data mirrored in `contract/hud-presets.json` for its own Rust and tests. The phone's four sections are not those five regions and its card ids are the phone's own `MenuCatalog` menus, so the two layouts are different questions rather than two halves of one choice - the phone never reads `contract/hud-presets.json`, and `tools/check_parity.py` has nothing to classify. **What this does not do:** it adds no shared route (the PC has no `/api/home-cards`, and the phone's choice is not readable from the PC - `docs/HUD-MAIN-SCREEN-CARDS-2026-10-29.md` section 7 is still the design for that), no adding or removing of the *cards the catalogue offers* beyond putting a layout-missing card back, and no real-device drag: the phone's own touch and pen drag is NOT-TESTABLE at this desk and CI's Gradle build is the compiler.
