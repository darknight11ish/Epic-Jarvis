- **2026-11-02 — Resetting the phone app must never change the PC's chosen voice, and the PC must always be able to say which voice the owner chose:** The owner's words, 2026-11-02: *"when I reset the jarvis Android app it changes the voice to the default. it doesn't seem to remember what I changed it to. fix it"*. Decided: the phone's one app-level reset ("Unpair & clear the token", `POST /api/devices/remove`) may clear what the phone owns - its pairing, its Keystore token, its saved address and resume point - and must never change a setting on the PC; the voice Jarvis speaks in, its speed and any animal's voice are the owner's PC-wide settings in `voices/state.json` (`jarvis_voices`), and the reset route writes the device registry only, which is now pinned by a test that compares the file byte for byte around a real reset. A re-paired or reinstalled phone gets the stored choice back the moment it connects - the PC reports `speaker.choice` (the voice that SPEAKS) plus `speaker.saved` (the owner's own choice), because with only `choice` a fallback or a fresh install made the pack default look like the choice the owner made, which is what "it doesn't remember what I changed it to" was. And a `voices/state.json` that exists but cannot be read is REFUSED rather than merged onto defaults: one changed key must never rewrite the owner's whole voice, the same rule `jarvis_manner` already follows for its own file (data-integrity audit F3, 2026-10-28).
