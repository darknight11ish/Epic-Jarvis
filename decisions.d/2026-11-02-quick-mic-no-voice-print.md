- **2026-11-02 — The phone's mic press does not check the voice print:** The owner asked: *"also, when i do the mic press to make a short verbal message to jarvis, dont have it confirm my voice through the voice id"*. A `source=push_to_talk` clip from `mic=phone` - the phone's own talk button, and nothing else - now skips `jarvis_voice.verify()`, and the 2-second "say a little more" refusal goes with it, because that minimum exists only to give the speaker model enough audio; a short message is transcribed and answered. The press on his own paired phone is the act, the way typing a message is, so whose voice it is no longer decides that one surface - stated plainly because it is the honest cost. What the check also bought is NOT granted: a private, memory or sensitive answer is not read aloud (the reply says `strictness: ""` and `mode: "quick_message"`, so `_really_checked()` is false), automatic learning refuses the turn (GUARDS L8 needs `very_strict` in mode `owner`), and `screen_aloud` is unchanged because that rule never rested on the check. It cannot open Jarvis Live either: "let's talk" from that press is an ordinary message, so the ways into a session stay a passed check (the desktop's button, "Hey Jarvis, let's talk") or the app's own Start control - "NO LIVE WITHOUT A REAL VOICE CHECK" is not loosened. Every other surface still checks every clip: Jarvis Live (its own start gate and its per-clip check are untouched), the phone's "hey Jarvis", the desktop's talk button (`mic=desktop`), and any clip that names no microphone. The button's readiness gate (`listening.push_to_talk`: a trained print, the voice-ID model and speech-to-text) is unchanged too - this removes the per-clip confirmation, not the enrolment. The desktop's own quickbar microphone was deliberately NOT changed, because the request names the phone's press. No route, setting or permission was added. Proved in `backend/test_quick_mic.py`, with the unchanged halves in `backend/test_voice_mic.py`, `backend/test_live.py` and `backend/test_voice_strict.py`; the reasoning in `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` §8 ("One-sided on purpose") and `docs/JARVIS-API.md` §5.
