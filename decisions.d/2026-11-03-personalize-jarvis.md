- **2026-11-03 — "Personalize Jarvis" is the setup quiz's own name, upgraded in place:** The setup questionnaire is now 25 questions in seven sections, in the SAME Settings card (`setup-questionnaire`) rather than a second window: a section tab row and a step-through view are less plumbing than a new Tauri window, and the card id stays what OpenPlace and both apps reach. The noun is "Personalize Jarvis" (aliases "customization quiz", "setup quiz", "retake setup quiz") so it cannot be confused with `jarvis_quiz.py`'s Quiz Decks. `setup.json` gains a schema version, `completed_version`, `runs`, `dismissed_at` and `in_progress`, and `apply()` now MERGES its answers into the saved ones: a partial retake used to replace them, so every question it did not ask lost its answer. A partial retake still marks the question set complete (`completed_version = QUIZ_VERSION`) because the owner has seen the new questions by then. Applying remains the desktop's own step (`POST /api/setup/apply` is PC-only and card-driven); `POST /api/setup/progress` is cardless and desktop-only, records where the owner is and stages draft answers, calls no setter and never applies, and the chat `setup_quiz` tool can explain and stage but has no way to apply.
