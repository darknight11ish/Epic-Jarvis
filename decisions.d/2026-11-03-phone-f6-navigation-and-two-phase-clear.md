- **2026-11-03 — The phone's F6 slice reuses the closed paths rather than adding a second navigation or clearing mechanism:** Two rules, from the F6 research and the wave lead's course correction, and what they replaced. **Navigation** rides the answer's `X-Jarvis-Route` field and the existing `ChatSession.openSettings` one-time request into `OpenPlace`, whose table is extended to name whole screens ("chat", "live", "history", "approvals", "inbox"), the Brain's plates ("memory", "models", "goals", ...), the shared menu ids from `backend/jarvis_menus.py`, and - in words - the real places this build has no screen for yet (Habits, Mood, Plugins are backend-only today). It deliberately does NOT add an SSE `ui.navigate` event kind (a new kind is a two-client, allowlist and `tools/check_event_names.py` change; an empty-room doorbell is a known defect here), Navigation Compose, a deep link, or a new `Screen` entry - the back stack is saved by enum name, so new screens may only be appended, and none was needed. The field's spelling is read in the four reasonable forms (`open_settings`, `open_place`, `open_area`, `open_screen`) because the PC side is being built in the same wave and its name is not pinned yet; a PC that keeps sending `open_settings` needs no change. **Clearing** a conversation is two-phase and per item: one request carries one exact conversation id, the PC raises ONE approval card naming that conversation, saying it cannot be undone and that the facts it taught are KEPT (the same `DELETE_STAYS` sentence the desktop and the phone's confirm use), and nothing is deleted until the owner answers. A waiting answer (202 / `waiting` / `asking`) is never read as "deleted"; the row stays, the queue is re-read so the card can be answered on the phone, and the list re-reads when that card leaves the queue. There is no bulk clear, no card-in-one-card, no cardless delete, and the model cannot clear anything without the owner's card. The desktop's own clear keeps its existing per-item confirm; the two apps still cannot drift on the kept-facts sentence because it is one constant.
