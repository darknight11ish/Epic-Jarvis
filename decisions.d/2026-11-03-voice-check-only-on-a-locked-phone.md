- **2026-11-03 — Jarvis checks your voice only when it should, and the exemption is one setting:** The owner's ask, in their own words: *"my voice should only need to be confirmed if i say hi jarvis when the phone is locked … when i start jarvis live on my unlocked phone i dont want it to need to confirm my voice. make this a setting i can change"*. **This SUPERSEDES the 2026-11-01 decision "a Jarvis Live session is verified once, and an answer it can say is said" (`decisions.d/2026-11-01-live-session-verified-once.md`) and the 2026-11-02 decision that followed it (`2026-11-02-live-voice-start-verifies-the-session.md`), but only for the PHONE and only for the trigger:** under those two decisions the first full sentence of every Live session was checked and the result then covered the session, so a session the owner began by pressing Live on a phone he had just unlocked still ran the speaker model on its first clip - exactly what he asked to stop. That check is not cached differently now, it is **removed for that trigger**: the unlock of the phone is the act that proves who is holding it, in the same way the 2026-11-02 decision already treated the phone's mic press as the act. Everything the old decisions protected still holds where it mattered: the **desktop is unchanged** (a PC has no "locked phone", so it keeps the 2026-11-01 rule, its spot check and its read-aloud flags); a **voice-started** session still carries the check in, because "Hey Jarvis, let's talk" is only answered after that clip passed the owner check (2026-11-02, untouched); a phone session that does not say whether it was locked - every app older than this change - keeps the check, so nothing about an old phone changes; approvals are still taps and never voice; and a session that was not checked carries no strictness, so **nothing that needs a real check is read aloud on it** - the rule grants nothing, it only stops asking. **One rule, one place:** `backend/jarvis_live.py`'s `voice_check_needed(trigger, device, locked, setting_on)` answers in one order - a non-phone device keeps its own rule; (a) the mic press never checks; then the setting; with it OFF every remaining trigger checks (the OLD, full checks, `locked` ignored); with it ON (b) "hi Jarvis" checks unless the phone explicitly said it was unlocked, and (c) a Live start does not check when the phone explicitly said it was unlocked; `locked=None` is "did not say" and always keeps the check. The phone's copy is `jarvis-client`'s `voice/VoiceCheckRule.kt`, held to the Python table row for row by its JVM test, and the phone's only power is to SAY whether its own screen was locked - one new `"locked"` field on the existing `POST /api/voice/live` start body, never a new route and never a client-side skip. **The setting is the EXEMPTION, and that is why OFF points the safe way:** `voice_check_locked` ("Check my voice on my locked phone"), ON by default = the owner's rule; turning it OFF withdraws the exemption and gives back the old, full checks, where the phone's "I am unlocked" is a claim and not a permission. So the looser direction (ON) raises ONE `change_own_config` approval card and the tightening direction (OFF) is at once - the standing rule, and a setting file that cannot be read reads as ON so only the owner can move the gate. It is read and written through the SAME existing Live route (`{"do":"voice_check"}`, no `enabled` meaning read), so no route was added for it, and its `section` in `backend/jarvis_settings_registry.py` is deliberately `None` - the third switch with that reason - because the "voice" Settings card belongs to the "Say Hey Jarvis" feature and `jarvis_feature_guide.switches_for()` would otherwise teach "turn on Say Hey Jarvis" to change this switch (`tools/check_feature_controls.py` caught exactly that). A skipped Live start is written down once in the audit trail (`voice.live` / `voice_check_skipped`, plain words, no clip detail); the mic press is a rule rather than a skip, so it writes nothing. **Chosen over two alternatives:** keeping the check and caching it per session (the 2026-11-01 rule, which is the asking the owner reported), and making the setting mean "no checks" (withdrawing an exemption must never quietly weaken a gate, and the full checks are the honest OFF). **The honest gaps, recorded rather than hidden:** (1) the desktop has no switch for it yet - the phone's Settings, Voice card draws it, the spoken phrases work on both apps, and `contract/feature-controls.json` carries the desktop row as `planned` / `setup-ui`; (2) the wake word's `locked` INPUT is not wired yet, because `/api/voice/utterance` lives in `backend/voice-flow.patch` and `voice-mic.patch`, so passing the phone's lock word into `jarvis_speech.hear()` needs a patch edit plus a `build_patch_history.py` rebuild - until then a wake clip keeps today's check, which is MORE than the rule asks for an unlocked phone and never less.
