- **2026-11-04 — Four `dsh-superagent` patterns are adopted for watchers, and a watcher must run in `observe` before it may interrupt:** the owner forwarded the 2026-11-04 spec (Part II sections 2.2-2.4 and 2.6B) after reviewing `deepseek-ai/dsh-superagent-prototype` (MIT), and chose to port its four mechanisms natively rather than embed any of its code: **Pattern 1**, an `accept_mode` with three states - `off`, `observe` and `active` - where `observe` is the default for a NEW proactive rule and runs the whole pipeline while writing a structured would-accept decision row instead of interrupting; **Pattern 2**, per-thread trigger batching so one pull request at one commit is one thread and the seven checks finishing minutes apart produce ONE message, with terminal fail-fast and terminal all-green settle and a receipt keyed `(pr_number, head_sha, terminal_verdict)`; **Pattern 3**, a pure deterministic `gate()` followed by an optional `assess()` that a filtered or informational event can never reach; and **Pattern 5B**, effects and outbox receipts with a deterministic `(kind, key)` written to disk before delivery and re-hydrated on startup. The decision, in the owner's terms: a watcher earns its voice rather than being given one, and a restart must never lose a question it was holding. Two boundaries are recorded with the decision because they are the parts a later reader would otherwise widen: `audit` and `python-advisories` are INFORMATIONAL - they are red on `main` while merges land - so they can never be the reason for a notification, and `observe` may only be promoted to `active` by the owner's own edit of `[watch] pr_checks_mode` in his settings file, never by a route and never automatically (an unreadable value reads as `observe`, never as `active`). The outbox is at-least-once by design and the limit is stated in the module: a crash between the send and the settle repeats one notice, and it can never do the opposite. Rehydration re-announces a parked card; it approves nothing, and `decide_grant()` remains the only thing that decides a grant. Nothing in this slice adds an approval path, a push, a merge or a new standing permission, and the watcher is off until a job names a pull request.
