- **2026-11-04 — A phone call can be recorded by saying "record call", and what happens after it is all local:** the owner's request, in their own words: *"add the ability of jarvis to record a call if I tell it a keyword 'record call' where it will record all the audio, transcribe it, put a date and identify the speakers in the transcription, and store all of it in a new obsidian note labeled with the date and time"*, the call being taken through the Android Jarvis app. **Six decisions in one, each with its reason:** (1) **the keyword is the whole message** - the eight sentences ("record call", "record this call", "record the call", "start recording the call", "start call recording", "stop recording", "stop the call recording", "stop call recording") are matched whole-message only, after the repo's own tidier, and are answered by `jarvis_quick.py` with the additive `X-Jarvis-Route` field `call_record: {"action": "arm"|"stop"}` that the phone acts on; a longer sentence ("record call now") is the model's. (2) **Android cannot capture the call stream, so the phone records on the speaker path** - `VOICE_CALL` is privileged (his primary phone is a Google Pixel 9), so the phone records through its microphone with the call on speaker and uploads 16 kHz mono s16le PCM in order; the far end is heard through the speaker and the words are less clear than a system recorder would give, which the card, the docs and the note all say, and importing a file from the phone's own call recorder is a later addition that is not built. (3) **transcription and speaker labelling are local, and reuse what is already here** - `jarvis_media_read.read_media()` (the existing sherpa-onnx model, English only, in 9-minute parts because its own ceiling is 10 minutes) and `jarvis_voice`'s enrolled print + the embedder it was made with (`EcapaEmbedder`, or `StrongEmbedder` when the print says `strong`) + `cosine`, then clustering at 0.55 up to six; with no speaker model or no trained print every line says "Speaker" and the note records why, because a guessed name on other people's words is worse than no name. (4) **a NEW Obsidian note named for the date and time** - `Calls/YYYY-MM-DD HHMM - Call.md`, created through `jarvis_notes_edit.handle_create()` (its own `edit_vault_note` card, File Guardian copy and undo receipt; the transcript is `after_outside_text` provenance because a call's words are other people's), with `date`, `started_at`, `duration`, `source` ("phone call, speaker recording"), `speakers`, `audio` and `[HH:MM:SS] You: ...` lines in the time of day they were said; if the vault is missing or invalid the transcript is still written beside the audio and that path is returned, so a recording is never lost. (5) **a consent reminder, three times, and one gate** - the phone's own recording notification, the card, and the note's frontmatter plus a visible line in its body: "Recorded locally; tell the other person"; `finish` refuses unless the phone sends `consent_notice: "on"`. (6) **off by default until the owner turns it on** - `[call_recording] enabled = false` in the shipped settings file, a hand edit with no in-app switch (the same shape `github_search` and `shell_exec` use), and starting is ONE `record_call` approval card shipped at tier `ask` because it starts capturing other people's speech - the owner-initiated note actions ship `auto`, and this one deliberately does not. **Chosen over three alternatives:** starting the recording with no card at all (it would capture other people's speech with nothing asked), a PC-only start (impossible - the phone holds the microphone), and a cloud or on-phone transcription (against the local-only rule, and the phone must not do speech-to-text). **Recorded rather than hidden:** `jarvis_gate.py` has no `_RISK` row for `record_call`, so the card prints the generic "unclassified action - treated as permanent and able to leave this PC" warning whose second half is wrong here; the row that fixes the wording must be written in the gate by a patch and this lane adds none. The phone's recorder is its own pull request written against JARVIS-API §186, and neither lane drove a real call on the owner's Pixel 9 - the speaker path and the quality of its words are NOT-TESTABLE until his field test. There is no delete route and no retention policy (recordings stay until the owner removes them; only a 400-session cap refuses a new start). English only, ten-second segments, and no import of a phone-recorder file. No real phone recording, transcription, speaker model, vault write or approval card was driven by this lane - those rows are NOT-TESTABLE, never passed. The whole story is `docs/CALL-RECORDING.md`, the wire contract `docs/JARVIS-API.md` §186, and the architecture `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` §14.
