- **2026-11-04 — Jarvis discusses any of its tools from a code-verified dossier, deterministic answer plus bounded model context, and no new tool or route:** The owner asked on 2026-11-04 to be able to hold full detailed discussions about any of Jarvis's tools and how they change what it can really do. The record is the two-way answer, and what it deliberately is not. A new shipped module, `jarvis_tool_profiles.py`, holds one hand-written page per name in the real `jarvis_agent.TOOLS` dict (51) and per capability family (11), each carrying what the thing is in beginner words, what it lets the owner do, what it refuses, what it pairs with, how to switch it on, and the repository files that prove every claim; its own suite re-derives the load-bearing claims from the modules rather than trusting the prose. Chat reaches it two ways. A deterministic fast path answers a sentence that names a tool **as a tool** - the word "tool", or the tool's own code name typed with its underscore - and sits after the plug-in block and before the feature guide, registered in `READ_ONLY_INTENTS` so a private chat hands it to the model instead. The model gets the same page as a system note of at most 1,200 characters plus one fixed line, subtracted from the room, present **only when a tool is actually named** so every ordinary prompt keeps the bytes it had, and kept alive across a follow-up by looking back at most two earlier user messages. The vocabulary split is the part that had to be proved rather than assumed: 42 of the catalogue's 175 feature titles open with words that also name a tool, and one feature alias is the exact phrase "plugin list", so the tool layer is addressed by its own marker and the feature layer keeps every one of its answers - `test_feature_answers.py` and `test_feature_guide.py` are the proof, not a preference.
