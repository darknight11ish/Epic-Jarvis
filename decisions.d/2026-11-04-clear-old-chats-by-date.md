- **2026-11-04 — Clearing old chats by date: keep the pinned ones, erase the facts they taught, ask twice and check a fingerprint:** The owner's decision, in his own words after Jarvis wrongly said it had no such action: *"it should be able to clear conversations it does NOT have pinned when asked to clear ones of a certain date, INCLUDING FACTS ... it must give a card with all the details, with a DOUBLE CONFIRMATION of yes, and require a Windows PIN or fingerprint (phone: fingerprint; Windows desktop: Windows Hello PIN/biometric) before deleting."* What that settles, and what it deliberately differs from: (1) **pinned chats are the boundary** - a chat the owner pinned is never deleted by a date purge unless the preview was explicitly asked to include pinned chats, which is never the default; a chat pinned after the card is skipped at execution time and counted; (2) **facts go with their chats, by provenance** - the facts cleared are exactly those whose own record names one of the deleted conversations, and their words are ERASED, where 2026-09-28's "Forget a time frame" retires them and keeps their words; a fact that records no chat, or another chat, is untouched; (3) **two separate yeses, plus a platform check** - the first yes only restates the exact counts, the second needs an HMAC-SHA256 ticket valid for 90 seconds and usable once, signed with `~/.openjarvis/purge-auth.key` by the desktop app after Windows Hello, or with the existing pairing token by the phone after its fingerprint; (4) **it cannot be undone**, and the card says so plainly; the ten-minute Undo belongs to Forget a time frame, which this does not replace. The trust model is local-only and is written down rather than implied: the backend proves the caller holds an owner-level secret and both yeses, it cannot prove a human was at the prompt, because it cannot see Windows Hello or a fingerprint reader - the app asks, and then signs. That is the same trust level the pairing token already has.
