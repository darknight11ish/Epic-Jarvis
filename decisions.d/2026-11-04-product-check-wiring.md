The engine (`jarvis_product_research.py`), the read half (`jarvis_product_read.py`) and the capture seam (`jarvis_product_capture.py`) had shipped on 2026-10-25/26 with no route, no chat tool, no approval card, no app surface, no browser driver and no network call, so the feature the owner could actually use did not exist.
