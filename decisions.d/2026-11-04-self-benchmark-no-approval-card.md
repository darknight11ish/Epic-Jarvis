- **2026-11-04 — The self-benchmark's routes raise no approval card, and that is a decision rather than a shortcut:** `POST /api/bench/run` and `POST /api/bench/cancel` act with no card for the same reason `POST /api/hardware/measure` already does - they measure this PC and change no setting the owner keeps - and the run is deliberately backend-only until a screen exists (the desktop Benchmark panel is phase 2, the phone's `BenchScreen` phase 5). Every route in `backend/bench.patch` is either a read of `bench.jsonl` or one measurement: starting a run loads a model that is already installed, times a fixed prompt, and asks Ollama to unload it again, and it writes only the benchmark's own rows. **What a card would have bought is bought explicitly instead:** the request body's `confirm: {model, lane, may_evict: true}` is mandatory, so a POST without it measures nothing, and the run is refused rather than faked when the PC cannot measure (answering, standby, a task running, a benchmark already running, a lane down, a model missing), each with its own sentence. **What a card could not have bought is any less eviction:** a run may push the owner's loaded model out of video memory, which is why the controls read carries that note and a screen repeats it before the press - the consent is a sentence in front of the button, not a card behind it. The routes stay off both apps on this tree, listed one by one in `tools/check_parity.py`'s backend-only table, so a screen that starts calling one is a failure rather than a silent port. **This record exists because the reasoning was not in it:** `jarvis_bench.py`, `bench.patch` and `tools/check_parity.py` all cited "the recorded DESIGN section 7 decision", and DESIGN.md lives only under `.dsh-scratch/` - outside the project record - so the citation pointed at nothing a later reader could check; it points here now. The same pass wrote the one-sidedness down in `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` §8 ("the self-benchmark has no screen on either app yet") and says plainly that `jarvis_bench.advice()` has no route at all on this tree. Nothing here decides a future delete: deleting a benchmark row is not built, and it will be decided with its own record.
