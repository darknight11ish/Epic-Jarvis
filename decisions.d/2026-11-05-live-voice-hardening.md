- **2026-11-05 — A Live session's latch may skip the re-check, never the cheap half of the owner check:** The owner reported that `Jarvis Live` took a radio, a television or a call in the room for his own voice, a regression of the 2026-11-01 session rule (#1104 / DEF-028). The 2026-11-01 rule was right about the thing it was answering - being asked to "say a full sentence so I can re-check your voice" two minutes after a successful check was the complaint - but it was implemented as a blanket exemption: once `trust_active` was set, `jarvis_live.trust_decision` answered "trusted" for the 1.5-2.0 s band and `jarvis_speech._SessionTrusted` ran no speaker model, so nothing in the room had to sound like the owner. The ruling, recorded here so the next change does not undo it: **KEEP the latch** (one verified sentence still covers the session; the owner is never asked to re-enrol mid-session), and **never let the latch skip the cheap half of the check**. Every latched clip with `RADIO_GUARD_S` (1.2 s) of speech or more is compared with the enrolled owner print by one in-RAM CAM++ embedding and one cosine (~15 ms), and a clip under the bar - `RADIO_MIN_COSINE` 0.25 when the clip carried "hey Jarvis" or arrived inside the follow-up window, `RADIO_MIN_COSINE_NO_WAKE` 0.32 when nothing in it was addressed to Jarvis - is BACKGROUND AUDIO: dropped in silence, never transcribed, never sent to the model, and nothing said about it. **Short confirmations are deliberately exempt**: "yes", "do it" and "stop" are under 1.2 s, a speaker model has almost nothing to go on in one second, and asking the owner to repeat himself to answer a card is exactly what the 2026-11-01 ruling removed. A guard that cannot measure (no enrolled print, no model, a clip the embedder refuses) does NOT reject: swallowing the owner's words on an unmeasured clip is the worse failure of the two, and the existing strict check still owns the first sentence and every >= 2.0 s clip. Two alternatives were rejected: dropping the latch altogether (it re-introduces the 2026-11-01 complaint), and raising `TRUST_STEADY_S` to 2.0 s so every clip gets the full strict check (it costs an owner waiting on a card the 2.0 s check's latency, and it still leaves the 1.2-1.5 s band unguarded). The two other Live bugs the owner reported the same day - answers that say "it's on your screen" instead of speaking, and the slow first turn - are separate rulings and are not part of this record.
