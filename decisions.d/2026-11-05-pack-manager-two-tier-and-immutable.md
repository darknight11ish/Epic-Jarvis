- **2026-11-05 — Packs: prompt-only, immutable, and replaced rather than updated.** The owner's principal-architect rulings Q2 and Q3 bind this slice, and both are narrower than "install a plugin" sounds. **Q2, the two-tier boundary:** phase 1 ships prompt-only skills only, and a folder or `.zip` holding any executable or script - `.py`, `.pyc`, `.ps1`, `.bat`, `.cmd`, `.sh`, `.exe`, `.dll`, `.so`, `.pyd`, `.js`, `.ts`, `.wasm` or any binary - or a symlink/junction that leaves its own folder, is HARD-REJECTED before a card, with no partial install. Code plug-ins stay blocked until an out-of-process runner exists: third-party Python is never imported in-process, whatever a scan says. The full pre-install scan (prompt-injection patterns, hidden and bidirectional Unicode, schema compatibility, effectiveness rubric, licence, SHA-256 manifest pin) is shown on ONE approval card and the receipt's summary is stored in `jarvis-packs.json`; installing approves the files and enables nothing. **Q3, immutability and no update command:** there is no `pack_update`, no auto-update and no in-place re-scan-free path. Installing a name the ledger already holds is a replacement - the new source is scanned from scratch and a unified diff against the installed files is shown with the new scan report on a NEW card, and the swap happens only after that card is answered (the installed copy is saved first and put back if the copy fails; after a yes the ledger keeps the replaced digest, the diff's sha256 and the previous receipt in `history`). Once installed a pack is IMMUTABLE: every file's SHA-256 is pinned in `jarvis-packs.json` and re-verified on every load - `pack_list`, `load_pack`, the skill hub's visible list and manual reads, the Skills menu, and `jarvis_skills.load()` itself through `backend/packs-tamper-on-load.patch` - and a file modified behind Jarvis's back is refused with a plain sentence and the pack marked `tampered`, never silently loaded and never moved or deleted. Restoring the approved bytes clears the mark; a tampered pack can still be removed, and removal deletes only files whose hash still matches. Two things are deliberately not built, and are not silent: **code packs** (phase 2, blocked on the out-of-process runner) and **PC-only with Windows Hello** on these cards (recorded in the first-slice decision fragment, with the two ways to get it and what each would cost).
