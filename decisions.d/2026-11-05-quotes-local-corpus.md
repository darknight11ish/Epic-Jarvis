- **2026-11-05 — Quotes come from a public-domain corpus on the PC, never from a model or a website, and the picker writes nothing down:** The owner asked for "real insightful quotes" on demand and on a schedule. A model-written quotation fails in the one way that is invisible - a confident sentence attributed to somebody who never said it - and a quote API fails differently: it needs the internet at the moment the quote is due, it cannot be cached under its own terms (ZenQuotes is all rights reserved; quotable's data licence could not be verified), and it would add a gate action and a reach row for a fortune cookie. So the words are a JSON file beside the module (`backend/quotes_corpus.json`), and the only sources are ones that ask nothing of a reader: Standard Ebooks (CC0), Project Gutenberg bodies with every Gutenberg header, licence paragraph and trademark stripped, Wikisource pages that state the work is public domain, and a nineteenth-century Bartlett's. Wikiquote is a finding aid only - its selection is CC BY-SA and much of its text is fair use - which is why it is refused as a corpus source on the record. Every entry must carry its words, author, work, year, source and licence, and a missing one is a load-time error rather than a quote that says "Unknown". The picker is `(day * 17 + 11) % 108` with `math.gcd(17, 108) == 1` asserted at import, so a full cycle sees every quotation once before any repeats, with no "last quote" file to drift between the PC and the phone. The scheduled half is a `quote` kind on the one scheduler, registered the briefing's way: a repeat raises the briefing's own ONE `schedule_repeat` card (next three fire times), a one-off raises none, and the firing's words ride the ordinary `state:"fired"` event as an additive `text` key via the new `Kind.say`, because a job's own text is written once at set-up and a quote changes every day. Both apps read that key after the App lock / hidden-lists check, so a locked phone shows only that a quote is ready. No route, no model tool, no gate action, no reach row and no permission were added.
