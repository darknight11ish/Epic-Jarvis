- **2026-11-05 — Waking restores the chosen pooled Coding lane:** When standby really stopped the Coding preset's pooled both-cards lane (port 11450), waking to Active now starts that lane again in the background through `jarvis_model_lanes.start_pooled_lane(by="waking from standby")`, and the waking answer says so. It raises no new card: the owner already chose the preset and answered its `switch_model` card when it was applied, and standby is what stopped the lane, so the wake is a restore rather than a new choice. It does nothing when the preset in `choice()` is not the pooled one, or when the lane was already down before standby (the lane's own `stopped` flag is the proof). It never restores the two single-card lane models itself - the pooled start pauses what it needs, exactly as a preset apply does. This replaces the old behaviour, where a still-chosen Coding preset pointed at a stopped port until the owner re-applied it.
