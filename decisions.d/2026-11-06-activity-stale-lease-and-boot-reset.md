- **2026-11-06 — A busy activity mark has a five-minute lease, and a restart resets it explicitly:** The owner's ruling of this day, after the benchmark refused for four hours. The HUD's in-memory activity mark is what tells both apps, `jarvis_power.in_flight()` and `jarvis_bench.answer_streaming()` what Jarvis is doing, and on 2026-11-06 it was left saying "thinking" by a chat turn answered by the `jarvis_quick` fast path - that return happens before `/api/chat`'s own idle write - so `POST /api/bench/run` refused for over four hours and only a restart cleared it. Three changes, one rule each: (1) **every exit from a chat turn resets the mark**, through one wrapper on the HUD's `do_POST` (fast path, tool loop, a raise while the lane is chosen, a client that disconnects - all of them pass through it), and the boot sequence clears the mark explicitly rather than relying on the in-memory dict being new; (2) **a busy mark with no progress for `jarvis_events.ACTIVITY_STALE_SECONDS` (300 s) is a leftover, not a live turn** - every streamed chunk and every tool step stamps `updated_at_mono`, so a real turn never ages out, and the first guard to look (bench's `answer_streaming()`, `jarvis_power.in_flight()`, or a `/api/status` read) auto-clears it through `jarvis_events.set_activity("idle")` with ONE structured warning naming the state and the age - it is never silent, because a leftover is a defect in whatever wrote it; (3) **the refusal and the status are readable**: the 409 body carries `busy_reason` and `activity_age_s`, and `GET /api/status` carries `activity`, `activity_age_s` and `busy_reason`, all additive. What this deliberately does not do: it does not trust the age alone - a genuinely running turn is protected by the exact in-process answer count the wrapper sets and clears, and by the heartbeats, so a five-minute lease cannot unblock a real answer; it does not clear any other hold (a Live conversation, another conversation, a waiting approval card and a running task are untouched); and it touches no client, no route contract and no setting. What it costs: a turn that reaches five minutes with _zero_ progress (no chunk, no tool step - an approval card waiting does count elsewhere) is treated as gone; the alternative measured that day was a benchmark that could not run again until the app restarted. Proved by `backend/test_activity_turn_guard.py` and by the before/after measurement recorded in the pull request.
