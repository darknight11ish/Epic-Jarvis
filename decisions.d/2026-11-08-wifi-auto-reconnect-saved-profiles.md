- **2026-11-08 — The auto Wi-Fi reconnect is approved as a bounded, default-ON, PC-only maintenance feature (owner ruling Q4):** the owner asked for a feature that "defaults on but can be changed in settings or chat with jarvis", and that every 2 hours while the desktop has no internet asks Windows to reconnect to a password-protected Wi-Fi network it has already saved. It runs on the 2-hourly tick with NO per-attempt card (an offline PC cannot be reached to answer one), but the switch is ordinary: turning it ON raises exactly ONE `change_own_config` card and nothing changes until that card is answered; turning it OFF is instant and cardless. Six guardrails bind it, all enforced in `backend/jarvis_wifi.py` and proved in `backend/test_wifi_reconnect.py`: (1) only saved profiles whose authentication is `WPA2PSK`, `WPA3SAE`, `WPA2` or `WPA3` and which carry a stored key — Open/WEP/802.1X are refused and `key=clear` is never passed; (2) it acts only while the switch is ON, Lockdown is readable and OFF, this Wi-Fi has no working internet, and the radio/adapter was not turned off by the owner (an unreadable radio or Lockdown fails safe); (3) at most the top 3 such profiles that are actually broadcasting in range, each verified for 10 seconds, stopping at the first success; (4) the switch appears in Desktop Settings, Chat and Jarvis Live through that one carded path, and no model answer can toggle it; (5) every attempt writes a Change Journal receipt (`jarvis_framework.audit_log`) with `timestamp`, `profile_name`, `auth_type` and `result: connected | still_offline`, never a key, so "did you reconnect my wifi?" is answered from that record; (6) this decision record. The feature is PC-only: the phone neither shows nor changes it (ARCHITECTURE section 8, JARVIS-API section 204; `/api/wifi-reconnect` is `deliberate` in `tools/check_parity.py`). On a PC where Location services is off, the in-range scan and the radio state cannot be read, so the check reports that and makes no attempt — it never guesses.
