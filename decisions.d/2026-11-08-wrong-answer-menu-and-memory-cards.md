- **2026-11-08 — A wrong answer gets one reason, one correction, and one memory card, and both apps read it back (owner ruling Q2):** Right/Wrong on the desktop and the phone now opens one sheet with five plain reasons (wrong fact, out of date, misunderstood, too long, too sensitive) and one optional correction of at most 500 characters; `POST /api/feedback/mark` accepts the optional `reason` and `correction`, and `GET /api/feedback/mark?turn_id` returns both, so a re-shown answer says what was chosen and shows the owner's own words rather than a machine tag. A wrong-fact or out-of-date mark raises ONE existing memory-review card for the first remembered fact the answer used: the owner's correction becomes an update card (`feedback_correction`, labelled "Update the fact" / "Keep it as it is") and no correction becomes a forget card (`feedback_retire`, labelled "Forget that fact" / "Keep using it"), asked for with `retire_cards=1` by every desktop read and by the phone. A misunderstood or too-long answer leaves a manner note and a too-sensitive one a privacy note; nothing is auto-approved, nothing is decided in bulk, and the card is the only decision. The single-decision design was kept over a multi-button queue (the owner's ruling Q2) because replacement words have to be typed for an update to mean anything: a correction present is an update, a blank correction is a forget. The migration smoke test opens a COPY of the live `feedback.db` and proves the old rows and marks still read with the two new columns — never the live file.
