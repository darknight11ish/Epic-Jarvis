- **2026-11-13 — A notification click is answered on both launch paths, and a toast's own sentence must name a control that can be pressed:** the owner reported that clicking the body of an approval toast did nothing, and expected the ordinary Windows behaviour (open the app, and for an approval open that card). Measured on this PC: the toast is in Windows' store with `activationType="foreground" launch="jarvis-open"`, 34 s later Windows logged "Some toast notifications have been cleared", the app's log records no launch at all, and the installed Start Menu shortcuts carry the AUMID but no `ToastActivatorCLSID` - which Microsoft's table for unpackaged Win32 apps is what makes in-process toast activation impossible. Decided, and this is the rule a later edit should not quietly drop: (1) the app answers its toast's launch marker on **both** paths a click can produce - the single-instance callback while Jarvis is running and a cold start - because answering only one is the exact half-fix the Deny button already had to learn, and the two look identical from the owner's chair; (2) a click that names no card goes through `windows::show_hud` and an approval's goes through `commands::open_approval_in_quickbar` with target `hud`, so a notification reuses the app's existing doors instead of adding a window, a tray icon or a second focus path; (3) an approval toast carries **Open** beside **Deny**, on the same action activation Deny rides and with the same marker as its body, because a sentence that says "open Jarvis to approve" must not point only at a gesture Windows will not deliver; (4) the install-time `ToastActivatorCLSID` (a stub CLSID on the shortcut, or a real COM activator plus its registry entry) is deliberately **not** added by this change: it is installer plumbing that cannot be watched firing from here, and a wrong or stale CLSID outliving the app is the failure mode this module already refuses for Deny. What that leaves, said plainly: a body click still cannot be delivered on this build, so Open is the press that works today, and the body marker needs no further change when the activator lands. Deny's action XML is unchanged, and the owner's other toast buttons' readers are untouched.
