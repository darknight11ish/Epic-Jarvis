- **2026-11-13 — The phone's microphone press interrupts Jarvis, ON by default:** the owner asked, verbatim: *"when I use the top mic button in the android app and jarvis is still speaking out from a previous response, I want it to interrupt its response and process the new thing I said. also add this to the settings as a changeable Default"*. So a press of the microphone while Jarvis is speaking now silences the reply at once (the stop word's own `VoiceSession.stopSpeaking()`), drops the old turn the way "hey Jarvis" does (`interruptForWake`'s half: the old job is cancelled, and its `Turn.silenced` latch means `SpeechAhead` asks for and plays no further sentence, so a reply still arriving from the PC never starts again on this phone), and starts listening exactly as an ordinary press does. The phone still does no speech-to-text: the new clip goes to the PC's own voice route like every other press. It is a changeable default as asked: ON, with its own switch in Settings -> Voice, "Stop speaking when I press the mic", this phone's alone (the words are in `voice/MicBargeIn.kt`, the value in `data/ClientSettings.kt`, the row in `VoiceSwitchesPlate.kt`, and Settings search finds it by its printed words). OFF restores the behaviour measured before this change: the microphone greyed while the answer streams, and "Still finishing the last one." once the reply has arrived but its sentences are still playing. This is not a new idea - the desktop's own talk button has always done it (`jarvis-desktop/src/main.js` `startPushToTalk`: `noteCutOff(); stopSpeaking();`, with the comment "Barge-in: holding the mic to talk again is as clear a signal as this app gets that whatever Jarvis was saying is done mattering right now"), so the phone was the half that did not have it. It deliberately does not touch the existing "Interrupting Jarvis" choice (that one is about the phone listening by itself while Jarvis talks), the stop word, the wake word, hands-free, or the `MicOwner` arbitration: the press is the ordinary `begin`, which opens the ordinary `Recorder` and asks `MicNow` first.
