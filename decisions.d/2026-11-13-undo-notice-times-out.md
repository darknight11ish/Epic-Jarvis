- **2026-11-13 — An approved card's undo notice clears itself after 15 seconds, but error notices do not:** the owner asked on 2026-11-13 for the notification that says an action cannot be undone to time out after 15 seconds. The sentence `JarvisRuntime.decide` puts up after an approval (`"This cannot be undone."`, or a sibling such as "Inbox tidy: 10 minutes to undo.") now goes through one reusable clear: `UNDO_NOTICE_TIMEOUT_MS = 15_000L`, one `Job`, and a clear that runs only while the notice is still that same sentence (`compareAndSet`), so an older timer can never wipe a newer, different notice; setting the same sentence again restarts its 15 seconds. This deliberately does NOT apply to error and problem notices, including every other `setNotice` caller: those keep the behaviour they had before and stay until the owner dismisses them or something replaces them, because a failure nobody has read must not vanish. No card semantics, routes, permissions or approval gates changed, and no device was driven.
