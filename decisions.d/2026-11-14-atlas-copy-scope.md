The copy the PC and the phone own is **not** renamed in this pass, and the reason is measured rather than assumed: renaming whole-word Jarvis inside quoted Rust literals moved 765 words across 109 files, compiled clean, and failed **30 of 1,275** tests - every one a mirror reading a shared fixture (`the Python side builds deepseek's target differently`, `refused must say jarvis_not_running's words`).
