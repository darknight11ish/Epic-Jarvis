- **2026-11-14 — Answer budgets are caps, not costs:** the benchmark's one-answer token budget is a ceiling, not a price: Ollama stops at `done_reason: "stop"` when the answer finishes early, so a larger `num_predict` cannot flatter a model or slow a suite that already has its answer - it only stops the harness cutting an answer off before it starts. The old budgets (64 general, 64 tool-calling, 48 vision, 400 coding) were smaller than a thinking model's hidden reasoning alone: measured on this PC, `qwen3.5:9b` asked "How many days are in a leap year? Reply with only the number." produced 266 characters of thinking, `done_reason: "length"` and an empty response, and every model scored 0/6 general, 0/5 tool-calling and 0/1 vision while every scorecard read `answered ''`. The general, tool-calling and vision suites therefore share `ANSWER_TOKENS = 1024`, coding keeps its larger headroom at `CODE_TOKENS = 1200`, and every suite result and stored scorecard carries `answer_tokens` so the terms are visible beside the score. A cut-off answer is still a failed answer and never a skipped one - what changed is that the scorecard now blames the budget, not the model.
