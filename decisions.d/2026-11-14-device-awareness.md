- **2026-11-14 — Device awareness stops being phone-shaped, and the owner will use "Jarvis" and "Atlas" interchangeably:** two decisions in one change, both the owner's. **First, devices.** The chat and voice answers about devices only knew a phone, so "how do I add my laptop" fell through to the model (or to the reminder how-to) and "how do I pair my iPad" was invented, while the device list question named one phone and never said a laptop or a browser could be paired at all. From now on the pairing answer is chosen by the thing the owner named: a **phone** keeps the two screens and the QR card unchanged in substance (this PC's Settings, Devices, "Pair a phone"; the phone's Settings, Devices, "Pair with your PC"; the PC's card approves it; both devices on Tailscale or NordVPN Meshnet); a **laptop, tablet, iPad or browser** gets the route that really exists and used to be documented only in `docs/JARVIS-API.md` section 90.5 - a key minted at this PC's own terminal with `py -3 jarvis_devices.py --add-device "<name>" --kind laptop` (or `tablet`), printed `jdk1...` **once**, pasted into the pairing sheet of the page this PC serves at `http://<this PC's mesh address>:4719/` in that device's browser, and never put in a chat, a note or a log; a **second PC** running the Jarvis desktop app is told plainly it is **not supported yet** - the thin client is designed, not built, and a second PC's own copy would be its own Jarvis, with its own models and settings - and the older "how do I add a second pc" answer that already said so is left in place. A new read-only `devices_list` answer serves "what devices are connected", "which devices are paired", "list my devices", "what devices can reach you" and the phone's own "is my phone connected" from `jarvis_devices.paired_status()` alone: each device's name, kind, whether it is reachable now (heard from inside five minutes, else "not heard from since"), and whether it has its own approval key (the apps' "Signed approvals: on / waiting / off" line). `paired_status()` gained `approval_key` for that one read and still returns no token, key or hash; an unreadable store answers "I cannot read the device list on this PC just now" and changes nothing. The model's runtime note now carries `devices: 2 (phone, tablet)` instead of `phone: connected`, and the field is renamed from `phone` to `devices` with every reference. **The owner's one limit, kept exactly:** nothing in chat or voice adds or removes a device - creating a credential stays terminal-only, removing stays on the Settings, Devices card, and that card can never be hidden. **Second, the name.** The product is called **Atlas**, and the owner will use "Jarvis" and "Atlas" interchangeably: this extends the 2026-11-12 visible-name decision rather than replacing it - neither name is wrong in prose, in-chat wording and the spoken wake word may keep saying Jarvis until their own pass, and no sentence has to be force-renamed either way. Code identifiers keep their names: `jarvis-desktop/`, `jarvis-client/`, every `jarvis_*.py` module, `JARVIS_*` environment variables, the `X-Jarvis-Client` header and the release assets are unchanged, so existing installs keep updating with no migration. Where it lives: `backend/jarvis_quick.py` (the answer shapes and the read-only roster), `backend/jarvis_devices.py` (the one extra field), `backend/jarvis_agent.py` (the runtime line), the `devices` row in `features/features.json` with `backend/jarvis_feature_catalogue.py` regenerated from it, and `docs/JARVIS-API.md` section 90.7; proven by `backend/test_quick_wins.py`, `backend/test_chat_honesty.py`, `backend/test_hud_fieldtest_answers.py` and `backend/test_runtime_identity.py`.
