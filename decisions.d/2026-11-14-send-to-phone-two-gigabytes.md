A dropped link therefore no longer kills a large transfer, a lost final reply is retried inside the window, and "once" now means one completed transfer. No filesystem path ever appears on a phone route or in a phone answer, and the phone checks the whole file's SHA-256 before anything lands in Downloads. The phone → desktop direction already accepted any file up to 2 GiB since 2026-11-13 and is unchanged.
