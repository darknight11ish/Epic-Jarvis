- **2026-11-15 — "About this conversation" is a small "i" and one compact card, not a wider row:** the owner asked whether chat history, in both the desktop and the phone, has an info menu on each conversation that names *"which device (phone or laptop) and more"* *"without this being overwhelming"*, and chose a small "i" that opens one card which closes itself. So the row keeps exactly the three actions it has today (Open, Archive, Delete) plus one small quiet ghost "i" drawn before them, named "About <title>" the way the others are "Open <title>" / "Archive <title>" / "Delete <title>". The card is not a new popup style: it is the one dialog the Brain window already draws, `openTopicDialog`, whose generic half became `openDialog` and now also closes on a click outside and keeps one dialog open at a time; Topics' own dialogs are unchanged in behaviour. The card shows only facts the row already carries, one line each, and nothing else — a chat with no voice, no taint, no tag, no pin and no archive says its device, its two dates and its message count. Two consequences were decided with it: the tag line is left out entirely while tag names may not be read (App lock / "Hide memory lists and chat history"), because "Not filed under a tag." would be a claim about a filing the window cannot see, and no line names the chat's kind, because the row already carries its kind mark and the card is for what the row does not say. The words and the line-builder live in `history-view.js` (`chatInfoCard`) rather than in `brain.js`, so the phone half can mirror them exactly and a Node suite can pin every line; it reuses `deviceTag`, `messagesWords` and `whenLine` instead of copying any of them, and reads the row's own `pinned` flag, which `/api/history` has sent since 2026-10-27 and this window had been dropping. There is no route, request, write, permission or approval card: the card only reads the row, and Escape, Close and a click outside all close it. Deliberately not done: no per-chat info on a row itself (the owner asked for the rows to stay as clean as they are), and no new backend field or payload — everything shown was already returned.
