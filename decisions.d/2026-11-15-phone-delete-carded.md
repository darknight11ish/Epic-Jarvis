- **2026-11-15 — The phone's History Delete uses the carded clear route, and an older PC's delete fails closed:** DEF-047 (Class A, `docs/defect-register.tsv`) found that the phone's Delete posted `POST /api/history/delete`, which raises no approval card (`docs/JARVIS-API.md` 184.6, `jarvis_self_portrait.clear_request`), while the phone's own record said the delete was two-phase with one PC card and nothing deleted until the owner answered - so an irreversible action was presented as gated when it was not. The fix points `JarvisRuntime.deleteHistory` at `POST /api/conversations/clear`, the one id body the route takes, and reuses the `ChatLog.Delete.waiting` handling F6 (2026-11-03) had already built: a 202 `waiting: true` says a card is waiting, the row stays, and the list is read again when the card leaves the queue. **The one new rule this decides:** a 404 with no `ok` body from that route means an older PC that does not have the carded route yet, and that is NOT "already gone" - the phone deletes nothing, says so, and never falls back to the uncarded route, because a fallback would delete with no card and reopen the defect. It deliberately does not change the desktop's own History Delete, which still posts the older uncarded route after its own on-screen confirm (recorded as `desktop-todo` in `tools/check_parity.py`, not claimed as done), and it does not carry the "delete the facts this chat taught" ticks through the card: the PC's card says the facts are kept, so the phone tells the owner the ticked facts were NOT forgotten and points at Memory instead of silently forgetting them after an approval the card said would keep them.
