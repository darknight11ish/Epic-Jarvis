The generator is the only writer: the file was regenerated by it and never hand-edited, and `contract/feature-controls.json` was not touched. The same gap existed one file over - the desktop's `tools/gen_feature_directory.py --check` was gated only indirectly, inside `jarvis-desktop/tests/feature-directory.mjs` in the required `frontend` job, and the PC's feature catalogue by `backend/test_feature_guide.py` in the required `backend` job - so all three writers now have a direct step in the one job whose contract is "reads only the repository".
