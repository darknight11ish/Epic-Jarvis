The checker holds the rule instead of restating it: `tools/check_feature_controls.py` imports `PLANNED_NOTE` and `_resolves` from the generator it is checking, so there is one copy of the rule and two readers, and a row whose note and sentence disagree fails at the desk rather than on the owner's screen.
