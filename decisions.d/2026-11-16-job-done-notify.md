- **2026-11-16 — A finished job may say that it ended:** The rule that "nobody is looking, so the result must not speak" is narrowed, not repealed. A background job the owner started and is waiting on may now announce that it ENDED — one phone notification and one desktop toast, carrying the job's own label and its state and nothing else — because those are the owner's own words today: "can jarvis give me a progress update and send me notifications when I ask on task that takes a while". The job's words still wait to be collected: neither notice shows a result, a param or an error text, and tapping either opens the Work pane or Work tab where the result already is. Progress was already carried on the event bus and is unchanged, and the PC's rule that a `cancelled` job gets no notice is kept. Quiet hours, a foreground-only rule, and any switch for this kind are the owner's call and were deliberately not invented here.
