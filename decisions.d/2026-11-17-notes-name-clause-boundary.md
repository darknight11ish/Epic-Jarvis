- **2026-11-17 — A note's name is cut at ONE list of instruction boundaries, shared by the create and append readers:** The owner's live sentences handed Jarvis a name made of the sentence's tail: "make a note for me called Mood Log and have this be one of my favorite notes" created `Mood Log and have this be one of my favorite notes.md` with no favourite, and "add to the mood log and make a note for today and put today's date and say that ..." answered that no note was called "mood log and make a note for today and put today's date and say". The boundary list lives in ONE place, `_NOTE_NAME_BOUNDARIES` in `backend/jarvis_quick.py`, and BOTH readers use it - the create reader (`_note_title_and_rest`) and the append reader's name cut (`_note_append_name`) - so the next sentence that swallows a clause is fixed by adding one rule, never by writing a second extractor. The rules added for the owner's sentences are "and make/create ..." (an embedded create instruction) and "and have this ..." (a favourite said as a description); a bare "and" stays inside a title ("Salt and Pepper"). The description form sets the SAME separate `favorite: true` front-matter property that "favourite it" already writes, because the notes store has no other favourite concept and none was invented. The append's words after "that" stay the entry verbatim, with its commas, and the date heading comes from the turn's clock, never from a date word in the sentence. A name that is genuinely absent is still refused with "I could not find a note called ... Nothing was written" and creates nothing - the fix never turns a failed lookup into a create. Proved by `backend/test_notes_create_find_append.py` on a temporary vault.
