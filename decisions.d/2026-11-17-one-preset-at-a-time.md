- **2026-11-17 — One preset's model at a time, and any preset may use all three cards:** Only ONE preset's model may be loaded at a time. Applying a different preset ends the previous one first - the pooled lane is stopped and proved unloaded, or the outgoing model is replaced on the same lane - and nothing is left resident "just in case". Because no preset has to keep its own card's memory free for another preset, any preset may use all three cards where its model warrants it; that is the consequence that makes the rule useful rather than restrictive. This replaces the older design, which reserved one model per card so several could run at once and which parts of `docs/MODEL-TOPOLOGY.md` still describe in their dated sections. The rule lives in the rulebook as `CLAUDE.md`'s "One preset's model at a time", the technical statement is at the end of `docs/MODEL-TOPOLOGY.md`, and `backend/test_preset_switch_lane_guard.py` is what gives it teeth: it proves the app-click apply path will not answer `ok` when a lane holds another preset's model. The model decision this rule follows is in `decisions.d/2026-11-18-everyday-26b-three-cards.md`: General is `gemma4:26b-a4b-it-qat` at a 32,768-token window across all three cards, and Documents and Browser keep `qwen3.5:9b` at 196,608 because those are the rows that need the long window.
