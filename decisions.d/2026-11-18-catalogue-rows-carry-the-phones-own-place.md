- **2026-11-18 — A catalogue row that names a PC-only place must name the phone's own place:** The 2026-11-18 phone awareness audit measured 32 `both` feature rows whose `where` named only a PC Brain tab (`Brain, Work`, `Brain, Memory`, `Brain, Now`, `Brain, Model`, `Brain, Projects`, `Brain, History`) and six more that sent the owner to a Settings card his phone does not draw. The phone's Brain is one scroll with seven headings ("Waiting for you", "What Jarvis knows about you", "What Jarvis is doing", "Money and time", "Your projects and goals", "Your learning", "Jarvis on your PC") and no tabs, so those sentences were honest on the desktop and a dead end in his hand. The catalogue's words now carry both apps' real places in one sentence (`PC: Brain, Work, Today. Phone: Brain, Money and time, Today.`) and say plainly when the phone has no screen for something rather than inventing one. `surface` is `both` only where the phone genuinely draws the feature: `reminder-actions` and `email-drafts` (the phone shows the note and answers the card; the PC writes the file) and `chatbot-money` (the phone reads the spend and the limit; the keys and limits are the PC's, so its label stays PC-Only Setting). A row whose full page is the PC's - `memory-known`, whose by-topic list with the filter box is the desktop's Memory page per `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` section 8 - stays `desktop` with its reason, and its `where` now names the phone's same-named heading so the two are not confused. The rule is machine-checked, not a convention: `tools/check_feature_list.py` rule 10 reads the phone's real places from `BrainSections.kt`, `ui/OpenPlace.kt` and `SettingsScreen.kt`, and `backend/test_feature_list.py` holds the five rows the audit ranked most likely to them. No route, screen or behaviour changed; the sources are `features/features.json` (three identical copies) and `contract/feature-controls.json`, and the generated consumers are regenerated by their generators, never hand-edited. This replaces writing `where` from the desktop's own screens alone.
