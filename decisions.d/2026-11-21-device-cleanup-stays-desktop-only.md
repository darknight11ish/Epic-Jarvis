- **2026-11-21 — The device clean-up stays desktop-only, by decision:** Gemini's ruling 5 (2026-11-21) makes the duplicate/inactive-device clean-up a permanently desktop-only action rather than a slice: concurrent Pixel 9 + OnePlus polling is expected, so a phone that polls for months leaves a trail of pairing rows, and pruning paired devices is an administrative, destructive-adjacent action backed up on the host filesystem (the whole `registry.json` is copied beside itself as `registry.json.before-cleanup-<stamp>` before anything is removed) - so it stays an owner-card-approved, dry-run-first button on the desktop's Settings -> Devices, and the phone keeps no clean-up button at all. The phone's Settings -> Devices keeps the list and the single Remove it already has; `tools/check_parity.py` keeps `/api/devices/cleanup` `deliberate` permanently (the desktop is the only caller, with no `PENDING_CLIENTS` entry), and the desktop Settings surface is the only door. The docs are corrected where they implied a future phone half: `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` section 8's bullets said "for now", "gains no clean-up button in this slice" and "the phone half is its own follow-up against the same contract, not a decision that the phone never gets it", and they now say it stays that way for the ruling's own reasons; `docs/JARVIS-API.md` section 231 said "the phone has no call site in this slice", and it now says the phone has no call site and never will, with the same reasons. No route, permission, card, tier or behaviour changed - the feature that shipped on 2026-11-21 (#1446) is exactly as it was, and its field test - the owner pressing the dry run and answering the card from a screen - is still owed rather than implied.
