- **2026-11-21 — The phone's Projects screen is a concise ready list, not a shrunken Kanban:** For PM4 the phone gets the surface the owner approved rather than a multi-column drag-and-drop board: a clean Projects list, the **Next 3 ready tasks** (each naming its project), a tap that marks one Done with its normal receipt/Undo, and a Quick Add that saves the owner's typed words only. Two boundaries are the decision, not a style choice. First, **the phone may summarize the plan in substance but may never weaken a safety rule and never speaks for the PC's permissions**: it shows the store's own `ready_next()` order and caps the READOUT at 3 (the same `jarvis_quick._MAX_NEXT_ITEMS`/`BRIEF_READY_MAX` cap the deterministic answer, the morning brief and the spoken answer keep), the writable actions are only the owner's own taps on their own rows, and it never sends a date, opens no new permission and adds no card class. Second, **those two taps are the plan store's existing doors, shared with the desktop's PM4 page, not a phone-only shortcut**: `POST /api/projects/<id>/work_items` calls the store's `add_work_item`, and `POST /api/projects/<id>/work_items/<wid>/status` calls its `set_work_item_status` - one event and one `jarvis_undo` receipt per change, a real status only, a second tap changing nothing, and the PC's own refusals in its own words; both are cardless because they are the owner writing down and ticking their own things, the same `todo_add` owner-direct tier the typed tools use, and both are held on a stale link. The one read they share, `GET /api/projects/<id>/plan`, is additive (the frozen §88.2 project shapes are untouched), returns only what the store holds (a missing date is null, an empty plan is the honest empty, the read writes nothing and does not create `projects.db`), and is classified `planned` in `tools/check_parity.py` with a written reason because the desktop half of PM4 lands in the same wave and calls it next - not `PHONE_ONLY`, since both apps are meant to use it. Deliberately not built: a Kanban editor on the phone, the Run Step dispatch (still deferred, §88.9), the 1-click Obsidian export (that page's own PM4 desktop card), and any date invention. `docs/JARVIS-API.md` section 88.10, `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` section 8 and `docs/PROJECTS-DESIGN.md`'s PM4 build note record it.
