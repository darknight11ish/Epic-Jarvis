- **2026-11-22 — The field battery's spend expectation is a range, and the $0.05 hard ceiling is the only failure line:** the external review's ruling 5.4, applied to `tools/field_battery.py`'s EFFICIENCY section. The battery printed its expected spend as a hard "Announced maximum" - measured recently as `4 turn(s) x $0.004050 = $0.0162` - and then FAILED a run when the four escalated turns really spent `$0.0177` or `$0.0218`. That is an estimate wearing a ceiling's clothes, and it made a correct measurement read as a product failure. What changed, minimally and without restructuring the section: the expectation is now printed as `Estimated spend: ~$0.0081-$0.0324`, derived from the same per-turn figure `_eff_estimate` already measures (the ledger's price applied to a generous token budget, or its measured average per request, whichever is larger) times the number of escalated hard turns, with the range set at half to twice that figure; the text says plainly that it is an ESTIMATE and a RANGE, not a ceiling; and the run now FAILs only when the dollars the ledger measured are above the HARD `$0.05` safety ceiling (`EFF_CEILING_DOLLARS`), whose printed line calls itself the failure line. Being above the range - including both measured overruns, at 1.09x and 1.35x of the old announced maximum - is not a failure. Unchanged on purpose: `EFF_STOP_DOLLARS` (`$0.04`) still stops sending once that much has really been spent, the pre-flight SKIP when the estimate itself is above the ceiling and the remaining-cap check still send nothing, `_eff_estimate` still prices the same token budget, and the section's rows still measure cost and seconds per escalated turn the same way. The two range factors are a stated judgement, not a measured confidence interval: they are wide enough to cover the two measured overruns and are labelled as an estimate so a future run outside them reports the dollars instead of pretending the prediction was a promise. `backend/test_field_battery.py` proves the range comes from `_eff_estimate`'s own per-turn figure, that a spend inside the range passes, that `$0.0177`/`$0.0218`/`$0.04`/`$0.05` pass, and that `$0.0501`, `$0.06` and `$0.10` FAIL.
