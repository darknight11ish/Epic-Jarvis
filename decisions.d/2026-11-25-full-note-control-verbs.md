- **2026-11-25 — The four note verbs the owner asked for: copy and merge and a section are built, and the trash gets its own card:** The owner asked for *"full control and full ability to manipulate and change any of my notes, with deletions requiring a card of approval"*. Create, append, insert, tag, property, alias, rename, move and delete already existed, each already behind its own card (only create is card-free, because a brand-new file cannot overwrite or delete anything), so **no existing card was removed or loosened** and only the four missing verbs were added. Copy keeps create's rule: card-free only when the owner writes it directly in the editor, one `edit_vault_note` card naming the source and the new title from the chat or the phone. A merge is a write AND a delete, so it is asked under `delete_vault_notes` (whose row already says every byte is kept in `.trash` and neither app can put the note back - both true), and its card names the survivor, every note that goes and the line counts, because "which one survives" must be on the card in the owner's own words rather than implied by field order. Emptying the vault's `.trash` folder is the ONE note action that really destroys bytes: no Undo receipt, no File Guardian copy (the trash IS the copy), nothing for Explorer to restore - so `edit_vault_note` ("the old text is copied first") and `delete_vault_notes` ("every byte is kept in `.trash`") would both be false on that card, and it got its own action, `empty_vault_trash`, with a `("no", "local")` risk row in the published base's end-of-file update block (never a new patch, the ruling of 2026-11-14) reaching a live install through `tools/repair_live_backend_gaps.py`. Its card lists every file with its age in days, the count, the total size and the words "this cannot be undone"; it refuses rather than half-emptying past `MAX_DELETE` and an empty trash answers "already empty" with no card at all, because there is nothing to approve. The `.trash` nudge is passive by ruling: one quiet line in the note-status and vault-location answers, only past 50 MB, 100 files or a file older than 90 days, silent below every one of them, never acting and never twice in one reply. `untitled*` notes are deliberately OUT of this change - they belong to the vault organizer's own deferred feature - and no route here deletes a note for good; only the trash can be emptied, and only behind that card. Two smaller rulings fell out of the measured bugs: a note-merge sentence must never be answered in pull-request words and a pull-request sentence must never reach the note lane (pinned both ways), and a search needle drops ONE leading article or possessive and only one, so "find my notes about the garden" searches "garden" while "the day after tomorrow" keeps its second "the".
