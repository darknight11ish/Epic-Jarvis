- **2026-11-26 — Plug-ins are switched on and off by talking, through the agent's own card pipeline:** The owner's ask was "full manipulation of every tool and plug in"; listing a plug-in (`plugin_list`) and adding one (`plugin_install`) were already answered by the fast path in `backend/jarvis_quick.py`, but turning one on or off had no sentence there at all - the switch guard inside `_plugins_question` handed every "turn on/off ... plugin" sentence to the local model, and "which plugins are switched off" came back as the whole list. Six whole-sentence shapes now reach four new intents: `plugin_on` ("turn on plugin <name>", "turn on the <name> plugin", "enable the <name> plugin"), `plugin_off` ("turn off plugin <name>", "turn off the <name> plugin", "disable the <name> plugin"), and the two reads `plugin_off_list` and `plugin_on_state`. THE ACTION IS THE AGENT'S OWN, not a second mechanism: `_run_plugin_switch` calls `jarvis_agent._prepare_plugin_switch`, then the agent's own `_gate_check` under the SAME `modify_own_code` action the model's `plugin_switch` tool uses, then `_execute_plugin_switch` (on) or `_plugin_apply` (off). So the card, its wording, its tier and the gate's audit row are exactly the ones that already exist (2026-11-02; `docs/JARVIS-API.md` section 181), and the quick lane writes no marker itself - pinned structurally by `backend/test_plugin_chat.py`, which parses the module and finds no `open`/`unlink`/`rmtree` in any of its plugin functions. THE OFF HALF IS DELIBERATELY NOT CARDED, and this is the one place the build brief and the recorded design disagreed: the brief asked for a card on disabling, but the owner's recorded rule for every switch is that turning one ON raises the card and turning one OFF is immediate (the 2026-11-02 entry in `docs/DECISIONS.md`: "TURNING ONE OFF IS IMMEDIATE AND CARDLESS"), the agent's own `plugin_switch` tool has answered "off" that way since it shipped, and `backend/test_plugin_switch.py` pins "off: NO card was raised". A second, carded off path in the chat lane would have been a second switch mechanism with two permission models for one act, so the brief's card was not built; instead the answer says plainly that the plug-in will not load the next time Jarvis starts and names the sentence that undoes it ("Say \"turn the news plugin on\" to undo this"). A DENIAL CHANGES NOTHING, and neither does a timeout or a loosened tier: the test drives a denied and a timed-out verdict and reads the marker back from the registry, and drives `modify_own_code` at tier "auto" to prove the agent's `NEEDS_A_PERSON` belt still refuses with nobody asked. ON WAITS IN A WORKER, NOT IN THE REQUEST: the gate blocks on a person, so the card is raised in a thread and the lane answers "one approval card is waiting" after 1.5 s (the same shape `jarvis_note_capture.capture(wait_s=...)` uses), then applies the SAME prepared plan when the card is approved; a chat request is never held open for minutes, and a card answered later still lands. THE NAME IS THE LIVE REGISTRY'S: `_plugin_lookup` looks the owner's words up in `jarvis_agent._plugin_rows` (the same `jarvis_plugins` folders `plugin_list` shows) - exact name first, then the one alias the catalogue answers already use (spaces for hyphens, "notes edit" -> "notes-edit"); a miss is refused and names both the words it looked for and the real names, never a nearest-match guess. THE TWO READS ARE READ-ONLY: `plugin_off_list` is the complement with its count and the sentence that switches one back on, and `plugin_on_state` is the true state with the loader's own reason when it is off; both are in `READ_ONLY_INTENTS` (so neither clears what "cancel that" would undo) and in `_PRIVATE_DETERMINISTIC_READS` (a private chat gets the real state instead of a model's guess), while the two changes are in neither. NOTHING WAS PREVIOUSLY REMOVED OR REWORDED: every existing intent keeps its own sentence in both directions (`settings_tool` still takes "turn on the timer tool", `plugin_install` still takes "how do I install a plugin", `plugin_list` still takes "what plugins do you have" and "list my plugins", `plugin_state` still takes "is docker plugin installed?"), and the tests pin each of those by intent. NO NEW ROUTE, SCREEN OR GATE ROW: the card is the existing `modify_own_code` action, so no tier line, risk row, card wording or `jarvis_gate.py` patch is invented. ONE COLLISION WAS FOUND BY CI AND FIXED IN THE SAME PULL REQUEST, and it is recorded rather than hidden: the first commit's grammar read `turn on the plugin list tool` as a plug-in called `list tool`, which is the Tools page's own `plugin_list` switch - the audit job's claim C101 (`backend/test_live_settings_parity.py`, 10 checks) and the self-awareness rows C104/C105 (`backend/test_self_awareness.py`, 1 check) both went red on it. Nothing in the live registry is called one, so no real plug-in loses its sentence.
