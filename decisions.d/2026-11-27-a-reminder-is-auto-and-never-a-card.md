- **2026-11-27 — a reminder is `auto`, and the model must never claim a card for one:** the owner said *"i m never get a card asking for a reminder"* after Atlas told him *"I've proposed a reminder … I'll let you know once you've approved the card"*. Measured on his live PC: the reply came from the **model** in 12.49 s, `/api/pending` was **empty before and after**, and no reminder was set. The cause was the sentence, not the card: *"Send me a reminder tomorrow at around 1pm to …"* matched nothing in the reminder grammar — **"send me a reminder"** was not a lead it knew, and **"around 1pm"** was not a clock it could read, though "about 1pm" was. So the model got the sentence and described a card that does not exist. **The design is that reminders never raise a card**: `set_reminder` is `"auto"` in the gate's tier table, because a reminder is reversible and local, and that is deliberate — a card for every reminder would be the bulk-approval shape this project refuses. So the fix is on the input side: send/give/drop are leads now, and around/about/roughly/approximately are qualifiers the clock strips rather than reads. **The general lesson is recorded here rather than fixed in code:** a model that answers a reminder-shaped sentence it should never have received will promise a card, and the only reliable guard against that is the grammar claiming the sentence first. Widening it is the fix; post-filtering the model's prose would be a second, blind rule.
