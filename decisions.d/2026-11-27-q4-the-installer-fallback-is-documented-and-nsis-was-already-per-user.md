- **2026-11-27 — Q4 — the installer fallback is documented, and NSIS was already per-user:** the owner's Q4 ruling of 2026-11-27, and what the tree already said. The ruling asked for two things: keep the `msiexec /a` extract documented as the official non-elevated recovery, and move the Tauri installer config to strictly per-user so future updates ignore stale `HKLM` MSI keys. **The second half was already true and was not changed:** `jarvis-desktop/src-tauri/tauri.conf.json:102` has carried `"nsis": {"installMode": "currentUser"}` since the NSIS target was added, which is why this PC's installer writes under `%LOCALAPPDATA%` and why it says **Already Installed** rather than demanding elevation. The MSI target is the other half of the pair and cannot be per-user this way — WiX writes `HKLM\...\Uninstall\{GUID}` by design, which is where the two orphaned 0.2.294/0.2.297 keys come from — so nothing in the config could have removed them, and pretending otherwise would have been the wrong fix to ship. What was genuinely missing is the page a person reads while standing at a PC that will not install: step 6 now carries the extract-and-copy recovery, the measured refusal (`/UPDATE /S` exits **0** installing nothing), and the two honest limits of an extracted copy. The measured evidence is from 2026-11-26 on this PC, where 0.2.519 went in that way with four files copied and every hash matching the MSI payload.
