- **2026-11-27 — Turning the price radar on is ONE approval card, and off is instant:** the owner's ruling, chosen explicitly: stop making `[price_radar] enabled` a hand edit of `jarvis-framework.toml` and give the radar the switch every other feature has. ON raises exactly one card under its own action, `price_radar_enable`, at tier `ask`; nothing is written or read until the owner approves it. OFF is immediate, cardless and withdraws a waiting card, and a denied, timed-out, refused or withdrawn card changes nothing. The action has its own `_RISK` row (`"yes"`, `"local"`) in the published base's end-of-file update block and its own `_NO_RULE_FROM_DENIAL` entry, so a local, reversible config write is not warned as permanent and outbound, and a "no" answers that one card instead of becoming a standing rule that would stop the radar ever being offered again; the row reaches the owner's live install through `tools/repair_live_backend_gaps.py`, never a new patch. The module's `request_enabled` is the one writer the settings registry's `BoolSetting` and any future route call, so no second writer can disagree with the card rule. What this deliberately does not do: it builds no Settings card on either app (so the `BoolSetting`'s `section` is None and the row's Switchable/Adjustable reclassification belongs to the lane that owns the derived status), and it leaves `jarvis_quick.py`'s older answers about the hand edit to that same lane. The radar itself is unchanged: read-only, prices only, never a booking, and its public page stays unofficial and breakable.
