# Android UI audit — 18 September 2026

**Brief from the owner:** "as cutting edge as possible, while still being easy
to use and understand."

**Scope:** every screen and shared component in `jarvis-client` on
`claude/android-apk-build-q435fi` at `543efbd`, read in full: 17 UI files
(5,000 lines), `MainActivity`, `FaceView`, the manifest, the resources, and the
two prior design documents (`UI-AUDIT-2026-09-14.md`, `SHARED-LOOK.md`).

This is a **reading** audit. No local build exists on this branch, and the
emulator smoke test does not take screenshots, so nothing here was seen
running. Every finding cites the file and line it comes from, so it can be
checked. Where a finding is a judgment rather than a defect, it says so.

---

## 0. The verdict, in one paragraph

The foundations are unusually strong — stronger than most shipped apps. The
theme system is measured to WCAG figures, the motion system is one easing and
three durations, the type scale is a deliberate ramp, and the face is a real
piece of live art nothing on Play matches. **The problem is that the chrome
around the face has not caught up with it.** Three screens use the new design
system; three still use the old shim and stock Material widgets, so the app
looks like two apps. Navigation is four small text buttons in a top bar, with
the most important screen hidden behind a tap on the face that only a 11sp
caption hints at. Half the "cutting edge" that is safe to add is
*consistency*, and the other half is *restraint* — the reactor should be the
one thing moving, and everything else should get out of its way.

## 1. Findings, ranked by how much they cost the person using the app

### 1.1 Three screens are on the old design system — the app looks like two apps

**Severity: high. Not a style nit; it is visible on the first minute of use.**

`Parts.kt` defines the new vocabulary: `Plate`, `Kicker`, `Pill`, `Quiet`,
`Affirm`/`Refuse`, `pressable`, `Section`, `Rule`, `Gap`. `Theme.kt` says of
the old `T` shim: *"New code should read `LocalChrome.current` directly."*
`BrainScreen.kt`'s `TopBar` is the app's one shared header.

Home, Brain and Appearance use all of that. **Pairing, Checks, Inbox and FAQ
use none of it:**

| Screen | Header | Cards | Buttons | Tokens |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Home | `LinkBar` (own) | `Plate` | `Quiet`, `Affirm/Refuse` | `LocalChrome` |
| Brain | `TopBar` | `Plate` | `Quiet` | `LocalChrome` |
| Appearance | `TopBar` | `Plate` | `Quiet` | `LocalChrome` |
| **Pairing** | none — a `titleLarge` and a caption | — | **Material `Button`** ×2, **`OutlinedTextField`** ×2 | `T` shim |
| **Checks** | own `Row` with a text "Back" | own `Column` + `RoundedCornerShape(12.dp)` | **Material `Button`** ×5 | `T` shim |
| **Inbox** | own `Row` with a text "Back" | own private `Card` (12dp) | own private `Action` | `T` shim |
| **FAQ** | own `Row` with a text "Back" | own `Column` (12dp) | — | `T` shim |

Concrete consequences a user sees:

- **Corner radius is 14dp on some cards and 12dp on others**, on adjacent
  screens (`Radii.card = 14.dp` vs `RoundedCornerShape(12.dp)` in
  `InboxScreen.kt:295`, `ReadinessScreen.kt:637/732/767`, `FaqScreen.kt:203`).
- **Three different "Back" controls.** Brain/Appearance: `← Back` in the
  accent, left-aligned, in a `TopBar`. Inbox/Checks/FAQ: `Back` with no arrow,
  **right-aligned**, using the ripple `clickable` instead of `pressable`
  (`InboxScreen.kt:84`, `ReadinessScreen.kt:546`, `FaqScreen.kt:170`). Back
  moves from one side of the screen to the other depending on which screen you
  are on.
- **Material ripples on some taps, spring-press on others.** `pressable` was
  built specifically to replace the ripple (*"a circle of someone else's brand
  expanding across a surface that is meant to look machined"*), and eight
  controls still ripple: every `clickable(role = Role.Button)` in Inbox, FAQ,
  Checks, and `ApprovalCard.kt:216, 315`.
- **Stock Material buttons on Pairing and Checks** with `ButtonDefaults`
  colours, a 40dp minimum height, and Material's own press ripple — next to
  `Affirm`, which is 48dp and hairline-flat. `PairingScreen.kt:151, 176`;
  `ReadinessScreen.kt:579, 588, 595, 690, 701`.
- **Material `OutlinedTextField` on Pairing** — notched outline, floating
  label, 56dp minimum — while Home's composer is a flat `BasicTextField` in a
  `surface2` box and the comment there says exactly why (*"none of which
  belong in a design made of flat plates and hairlines"*). The first screen a
  new user sees is the one that breaks the design.

**Fix:** port the four screens onto `Parts.kt` + `TopBar`. Mostly mechanical.
Pairing needs one new part (a flat text field matching the composer). Checks
needs `Affirm`-shaped primary buttons. This is the single highest-value change
in this document and it needs no design decision from the owner.

### 1.2 The most important screen is hidden behind a gesture with no affordance

**Severity: high for a beginner user; medium once learned.**

The brain screen ("State of mind") — rush latch, freshness, route, model,
attention budget, jobs, memory review queue, capabilities — is reached only by
**tapping the face**. The hint is an 11sp `labelSmall` in `textLo` (the faintest
tier, 4.57:1) reading *"State of mind →"* at `HomeScreen.kt:265`.

Everything else is four `Quiet` text buttons in the top bar: `Retry` (when
offline), `Inbox · N`, `Look`, `Help` (`HomeScreen.kt:335-345`). They are
`labelMedium`, 13sp, in `textMid`. There is no icon anywhere in the app.

Consequences:

- A new user does not know the brain screen exists.
- "Look" is not a word anyone would guess means "appearance settings".
- The bar mixes a **status** (the dot and label, tappable → Checks) with
  **navigation** (Inbox, Look, Help) with an **action** (Retry) in one row of
  same-weight text. Nothing signals which is which.
- The row overflows on a narrow phone in a large font: five weighted text
  items plus a status label with no ellipsis.

The previous audit's constraint stands: no bottom tabs for five screens
(*"navigation for its own sake"*). But the current answer under-serves the
opposite failure. See §3 for the options — this one is the owner's call.

### 1.3 Colour-only signals, where the app's own rules forbid them

**Severity: medium. Accessibility, and consistency with `SHARED-LOOK.md` §9.**

The design's own rule: *"ok and bad are never distinguished by colour alone."*
`Affirm`/`Refuse` honour it (filled vs outlined). These do not:

- **Inbox `Action("Undo", T.Ok)` and `Action("Cancel", T.Dim)`** — the same
  text pill, tinted (`InboxScreen.kt:246, 271`).
- **Brain `Quiet("Keep", okInk)` / `Quiet("Discard", badInk)`** — the same
  quiet text, tinted (`BrainScreen.kt:565-576`).
- **Sleep offer `Enable` / `Not now` / `Stop asking`** — three same-shape text
  buttons in three tints (`BrainScreen.kt:521-535`).
- **The theme-row selected mark is a "●" glyph** in the accent
  (`AppearanceScreen.kt:400`) alongside a 1.5dp vs 1dp border — a 0.5dp
  difference is below what a phone shows reliably.
- **`Freshness` and `LinkBar`** pair a dot with a word — good. **`Pill`**,
  `ReachBadge` and the wake-word state do the same. Good.

**Fix:** give Keep/Discard and Undo/Cancel the `Affirm`/`Refuse` shape
treatment at `Quiet` scale — a filled affirmative, an outlined destructive —
or a leading glyph. Make the selected theme row's check a real check mark.

### 1.4 Touch targets under 48dp

**Severity: medium. Material and WCAG 2.5.8 both want 48dp/24px minimum.**

- `FaceChip` and `CycleStatesChip`: `labelMedium` + 9dp vertical padding ≈
  **38dp** (`AppearanceScreen.kt:441, 469`). Twenty of them in a grid.
- The `LinkBar` status button: 10dp padding + 13sp text ≈ **40dp**
  (`HomeScreen.kt:322`).
- `Pill` is a label and correctly not a target.
- `Quiet` is `heightIn(min = 44.dp)` — 4dp short of the Android minimum, and
  it is the app's most-used button.
- `ReachBadge` and the "Show detail" text: `minimumInteractiveComponentSize()`
  handles the second; the first is not tappable. Fine.

**Fix:** `Quiet` to 48dp; chips to 44dp minimum with a 4dp touch slop, or
48dp.

### 1.5 The Home screen has no empty state — it has an empty *space*

**Severity: medium. This is the screen the user lives on.**

When nothing is pending and no reply is showing, the Home list is: face block,
then nothing, then the composer. On a tall phone that is ~40% of the screen
blank. When approvals are off it shows a warning plate, which is correct, but
in the normal case the app looks like it is waiting to load.

The desktop equivalent is a tray icon; the phone has the whole screen. What
belongs there is small and already known to the app: the last reply (kept
after streaming, which it is), the attention line ("3 things waiting to be
told"), and the lane chip — the last of which currently sits *inside* the face
block at 1/4 the size it deserves.

**Fix:** a quiet "now" strip under the face: lane · model · power · attention
count, as `Field` rows or pills in one `Plate`. No new data. See §3 option B.

### 1.6 Text is doing the work of icons, and it is a little too much text

**Severity: medium. Legibility and the "understand at a glance" half of the brief.**

There are zero icons in the app — every control is a word. That is a
defensible choice (no icon font to bundle, no ambiguity) and it is honestly
carried through. But it produces a wall of same-weight text in places:

- Every `ReadinessCard` on Checks is a title + a 2-4 line paragraph. Eleven of
  them. The screen is a document.
- `WakeWordCard` alone carries **four** paragraphs of body text.
- The FAQ answers are 60-90 words each in `bodySmall` (13sp) in `textMid`.
  13sp for 80-word paragraphs on a phone is small.
- The `ApprovalCard` footer *"Nothing runs until you decide."* is repeated on
  every card in the list. On five cards it is five copies.

The brief's other half — "easy to understand" — argues for keeping the words.
The fix is hierarchy, not deletion: leading `Kicker`s, `Rule`s between fields,
and body text at `bodyMedium` (15sp) for anything longer than a line.

### 1.7 Smaller, all verified against the source

- **`Notice` cannot be dismissed with a swipe** and stays until tapped. Fine on
  Home, but it is inserted at list position 1 and shifts the whole list when
  it appears (`HomeScreen.kt:190`). Animate its entry with `animateItem()` or
  hold a fixed slot.
- **`LinkBar` has no `Modifier.statusBarsPadding()` gap** — `root` applies
  `windowInsetsPadding(systemBars)` so it is safe, but the bar is a flat
  `surface1` strip under the status bar with no separation from it on Void
  and Contrast, where `surface0 == surface1` (`#000000` both). On those two
  themes the status bar, the link bar and the page are one black field.
- **Contrast theme's `surface1 == surface0`** (`Themes.kt:693-694`) means
  every `Plate` without an outline is invisible on Contrast. The theme doc
  says *"depth comes from borders rather than tone"* — but `Plate` only draws
  a border when `outline != null`, which most call sites leave null. On High
  Contrast most cards have no edge.
- **The composer's Send button** (`HomeScreen.kt:483-514`) uses
  `accent` fill with `surface0` text when `canSend`, `surface2` with `textLo`
  otherwise. `textLo` on `surface2` is the tightest pair in the system
  (4.57:1) — it passes, barely, and it is the disabled state, so fine. But
  "Stop" uses `badInk` on `badInk @ 16% over surface1`, which is not measured
  anywhere.
- **`ExpiryCountdown` says "Expires in 0m 43s."** Minutes-and-seconds for
  under a minute reads as a timer, not a deadline. "43 s left" / "4 min left".
- **`ReachBadge` labels are shouting**: `LEAVES THIS MACHINE`, `NO UNDO`,
  `UNCLASSIFIED` in `labelSmall` uppercase, which is the tracked kicker style
  — correct — but `RoundedCornerShape(6.dp)` instead of the `chipShape`
  every other label uses (`ApprovalCard.kt:296`).
- **The approval swipe has no visual affordance while dragging.** The card
  translates; nothing behind it says "approve" or "deny". A user who swipes
  half-way sees a card moving and no consequence. Draw the destination word
  under the card, in `okMark`/`badMark`, revealed by the offset.
- **The "Show detail" toggle and "Show surrounding text" toggle** are text in
  the accent with the Material ripple (`ApprovalCard.kt:216, 315`) — the only
  two ripples on Home.
- **`FaqCard` uses `+`/`−` glyphs** for open/closed (`FaqScreen.kt:214`) —
  the only disclosure control in the app; the chevron the theme row and
  `Section` do not have.
- **Voice `VoiceStrip` phases stack vertically** (`HomeScreen.kt:439-457`):
  phase strip + transcript strip + notice strip can all be visible at once,
  each with its own 8dp gap, pushing the composer down by up to ~130dp.
- **`CrashScreen` is deliberately off-system** and says so. Correct. Leave it.
- **No predictive back.** `android:enableOnBackInvokedCallback` is unset in
  the manifest, so on Android 14+ the system's back-swipe preview animation
  is disabled and back is a hard cut. `NavBackHandler` uses Compose
  `BackHandler`, which supports predictive back once the flag is on. One line.
- **The launcher theme sets `windowBackground` to `@android:color/black`**
  (`themes.xml:4`), so the Android 12+ system splash is a black field with
  the icon. That is fine and matches Reactor/Void, but on Daylight the app
  hard-cuts from black to `#EEF1F6` at first frame. Set the splash background
  once from the persisted theme via `SplashScreen` API, or accept it.
- **IBM Plex Sans is still not bundled** (`JarvisTheme.kt:338`). The
  environment cannot fetch it. Dropping the four OFL TTFs into `res/font/` is
  the single largest *visual* upgrade available and it is a file copy.

## 2. What is already right, and must not be "improved" into something worse

Listing these because a cutting-edge pass that touches them would be a
regression, and because a future reviewer will be tempted.

- **The face is the only animated surface, bounded to 260dp.** The previous
  audit priced everything else and refused it with numbers (blur 8-20ms,
  full-screen shader 4-12ms continuous, shadows 2-8ms). Those numbers have not
  changed. Do not add a second animated surface, an ambient background, or
  glass.
- **Hairlines, not shadows.** Flat plates with 1dp borders. This is the look,
  and it is cheaper.
- **Spring-press instead of ripple** (`pressable`). Physical and cheap.
- **`Affirm`/`Refuse` shape channel.** Filled vs outlined. Keep.
- **The accent is derived from the idle colour and there is no picker.** Keep.
  It is the load-bearing idea of the theme system.
- **The theme picker uses static swatches, not live faces.** Keep — a grid of
  live reactors is the recorded photosensitivity bug.
- **`Freshness` says in words whether the screen can be trusted.** Keep on
  every screen that shows server data.
- **The approval card refuses swipe for anything outbound or irreversible and
  says why in words.** Keep.
- **Reduced motion is honoured** by `Motion.reduced`. Keep.
- **The text sizes are deliberately large** (body 17sp). Keep; the brief's
  "easy to understand" needs them.
- **The type ramp, the radii scale, the motion tokens** are all correct and
  should be *used more*, not changed.

## 3. Choices that are the owner's — with the trade-offs

Each of these changes what a user sees every day, so it is a decision rather
than a fix.

### Choice A — Navigation. How do the five screens get reached?

The screens: Home, State of mind (brain), Inbox, Look (appearance), Checks,
Help. Today: four text buttons in a top strip + tap-the-face.

**A1 — Keep the top strip, fix it.** Icons + words, evenly spaced, status
separated from navigation by a `Rule`. Brain gets a real button. "Look"
becomes "Appearance". Smallest change; keeps the face at the top where the eye
lands. Costs a small icon set (six 24dp vector paths, drawn in-app like the
microphone glyph is — no icon font). *Still leaves navigation at the top of a
tall phone, where a thumb does not reach.*

**A2 — Bottom bar, three items: Home · Inbox · Mind.** Look, Checks and Help
move to an overflow on the Home link bar (or into a "More" sheet). Thumb-
reachable. This is what every mainstream app does, which is both its strength
(zero learning) and its weakness (it looks like every app). The previous audit
called five-tab navigation "for its own sake"; three is not five. *Costs ~56dp
of vertical space on the screen the face lives on; the face would shrink or
the composer would tighten.*

**A3 — The face is the navigation.** Tap for Mind (as now), but make it
obvious: a pressed state that lifts the face, a proper `Quiet("State of mind")`
button under it at 48dp instead of an 11sp caption, and swipe left/right on
the face for Inbox / Look. Most "cutting edge"; the reactor becomes a control
surface. *Gestures are invisible until learned, which is the exact problem
1.2 describes — this fixes discoverability of the tap and adds two new
undiscoverable gestures.*

**Recommended: A1 now, and revisit A2 if the owner finds themselves reaching
for the top of the phone.** A1 is a day of work and no regression risk. A2 is
a layout change that needs a real device to judge the face's size against.

### Choice B — What fills the Home screen when nothing is pending?

**B1 — Nothing.** The face, then space, then the composer. Calm. The desktop
equivalent is a tray icon, so this is the honest port: when there is nothing to
say, say nothing. *Looks unfinished to a new user.*

**B2 — A "now" plate under the face.** Lane · model · power · attention
count, as `Field` rows. Static, no new data, no new requests. Answers "what is
Jarvis doing" without opening Mind. *One more plate on the busiest screen; it
must stay small (4 rows, no paragraph).*

**B3 — The last reply stays, and a one-line status sits in the composer's
placeholder** ("Ask Jarvis · local · llama-3"). Least screen used; the
information is where the eye goes to type. *A placeholder is the least-read
text on any screen.*

**Recommended: B2**, with the lane chip moved out of the face block into it.

### Choice C — How much text?

**C1 — Keep every sentence**, add hierarchy (kickers, rules, `bodyMedium`
instead of `bodySmall` for anything over one line). The words are the product's
honesty. *Checks and FAQ stay long.*

**C2 — Progressive disclosure.** Each `ReadinessCard` shows title + one line
+ state; the paragraph opens on tap, like `FaqCard`. Approval footer shown
once, above the list, not on each card. *Two taps to read a full explanation.*

**Recommended: C2 for Checks and the approval footer; C1 for FAQ**, which is
a document by design.

### Choice D — Bundle the font?

**D1 — Yes.** IBM Plex Sans, four weights, OFL. ~600KB in the APK. The type
scale was designed for it and every desktop surface uses it. The largest
single visual step available. *Someone has to copy four files from the
desktop repo into `res/font/` — the build environment cannot fetch them.*

**D2 — No.** Platform font. Zero bytes, zero work, and Roboto/Inter at the
right weights is already good. *The phone and desktop will never quite match.*

**Recommended: D1, by the owner**, since only the owner can get the files in.

### Choice E — The Contrast theme's flat cards

**E1 — `Plate` draws a `hairlineStrong` border by default when
`surface1 == surface0`.** One condition in `Plate`. Cards regain edges on
Contrast and Void without any other theme changing.

**E2 — Give Contrast a `surface1` one step off black** (`#0A0A0A`) like Void
does. Cards become visible by tone. *Contradicts the theme's own doc ("depth
comes from borders rather than tone").*

**Recommended: E1.** It is what the theme's comment already promises.

## 4. Proposed order of work, if all of the above is approved

1. **Port Pairing, Checks, Inbox, FAQ onto `Parts.kt` + `TopBar`** (§1.1).
   Add a flat `Input` part for Pairing. Kill every `clickable` ripple and every
   Material `Button`/`OutlinedTextField` outside `CrashScreen`. One commit.
2. **Touch targets to 48dp** (§1.4). `Quiet`, chips, link bar. Same commit as 1.
3. **`Plate` border on flat themes** (§1.7, choice E1). One line.
4. **Predictive back flag** (§1.7). One manifest line.
5. **Navigation per choice A.** Six in-app vector glyphs, drawn like the mic.
6. **Home "now" plate per choice B.**
7. **Shape channel for Keep/Discard, Undo/Cancel** (§1.3). Small `Affirm`/
   `Refuse` variants at `Quiet` scale.
8. **Swipe affordance on approval cards** (§1.7).
9. **Text hierarchy per choice C.**
10. **Font, when the owner has copied it in** (choice D).

Items 1-4 need no decision and can start now. Items 5-10 wait on §3.

Each commit is a ~15-minute CI round trip with no local compile, so they
should be small and land one at a time — the audit-fix batches earlier today
were the right size.

## 5. What this audit did not do

- **See the app running.** No screenshot pipeline exists. Adding one — a
  `captureToImage()` in `FaceRenderTest` uploaded as a CI artifact — would let
  the next audit look rather than read, and it is a modest addition to the
  existing smoke job. Recommended as its own small piece of work.
- **Measure the "Stop" button and the `Notice` tint contrasts.** Both are
  alpha composites and the theme doc measures only opaque pairs. They are
  likely fine and are noted so they get measured when a screenshot exists.
- **Audit the notification, the QS tile or the lock-screen text.** Those are
  system-drawn surfaces; the strings are in `strings.xml` and read well.
