## The three worst problems

1. **The "Jarvis is watching" Stop button can fail without telling the owner.**
   - Home's PC-watching plate says "The link to Jarvis is catching up - Stop still works". Tapping "Stop watching" calls `JarvisRuntime.stopScreenWatch()` (JarvisRuntime.kt:1593). That calls `api.stopScreenWatch()` and throws away the result, then re-reads status.
   - If the PC is unreachable, nothing happens. The sign stays on, no error appears, and the button is re-enabled.
   - This is the one control the owner must be able to trust, and the plate has promised it works. Fairly sure (read the code, did not run it).
2. **What-asks-first has no row for "Look at this" or "Watch with me".**
   - The page promises every action, but the screen-looking groups have none. Checked in /tmp/pr35/backend/jarvis_asks_first.py:GROUPS and in /tmp/pr35/jarvis-client/app/src/test/resources/contract/asks-first-cases.json.
   - Rows exist for picture mode ("Let it look at pictures of your screen, slowly, on the processor"), Obscura ("Use a headless browser (Obscura) for plain web reading") and phone notifications ("Start reading notifications from apps you choose on your phone").
   - Missing: reading the screen's words, the phone's screen-share watch, and the fingerprint gate on the phone's "Let Jarvis read this phone's screen" switch. Sure, verified in both files.
3. **"Hear it" gives feedback in the wrong place, and there is no Stop.**
   - VoicesScreen.kt:576-620. Each voice row has "Hear it". The status line ("Asking the PC for the sound…", "Playing …") is drawn once, below the whole list (VoicesScreen.kt:617-620).
   - On a phone with about 20+ voices, the owner taps a row and the words appear off-screen.
   - While one sample plays, every "Hear it" is disabled (`enabled = hearing == null`, line 602). There is no Stop. The only ways to cut it are to leave the screen (VoicesScreen.kt:132) or start a question.
   - Fairly sure.

  - The owner can start watching and then have no way to ask. Fairly sure.
