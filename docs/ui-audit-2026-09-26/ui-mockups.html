Audit ideas 1 and 7 · both apps

The face moves with real voices, and shows it heard you

Watch the two bars under each face. Loudness heard is how loud the talking is. Face size is how much the face swells. Today on the PC they do not match: the face moves to a timer. Proposed, the face follows the loudness. Only the loudness number is passed along, never the sound itself.

When you stop talking, the face does a short "inhale" so you know it caught what you said. On the phone you also feel one light buzz. Today, pressing and cancelling buzz on the phone, but letting go does nothing.

Audit ideas 2 and 10 · mainly the PC

One moving thing on the Jarvis bar, and words for what it is doing

While Jarvis works, the bar today runs seven animations at once: two spinning rings, a breathing glow, a pulsing centre dot, a light sliding along the top edge, a pulsing status dot and a blinking cursor. Proposed: only the small logo spins. The status says what Jarvis is doing, in words.

The small logo also gets one look per state, so "waiting on you" and "problem" can be told apart without seeing colour.

Audit idea 3 · mainly the PC

One rule for "fewer animations"

Windows and Android both have a setting that asks apps to move less. Today each desktop window handles it its own way. Proposed: one rule everywhere. Nothing shakes, slides or spins, and short fades stay.

Audit idea 4 · the PC

Teach the right colour for "thinking"

The first-run tour shows "thinking" in amber and "waiting on you" in red. But in the real app, amber means waiting on you and thinking is violet. So a new user learns the wrong meaning on day one. Proposed: the tour uses the real state colours.

Audit idea 5 · the phone

The phone shows bold, lists and links properly

Today the phone prints answers as plain text, so the model's formatting marks show: ** around bold words, - in front of list items, and links as raw brackets. Proposed: simple formatting, like the PC. Every link also shows its real web address, because after Jarvis reads an email or web page, a link's words may have been written by someone else.

Audit idea 6 · the phone

Phone lists glide instead of jumping

When a new approval card or reply arrives, everything below it jumps down at once. Proposed: the new item slides in once, over a fifth of a second, and the rest glides down. It only moves when something changes, and it is instant with "fewer animations" on. The card itself gets no glow and no pulse, and a card on its way out has its buttons switched off.

Audit idea 8 · both apps

A short, fixed list of text sizes

A type scale is a short list of allowed text sizes, used everywhere. Today the PC uses about 20 loose sizes, and some important warnings are tiny. The phone already has big sizes it never uses. Proposed: 4 or 5 sizes on the PC with nothing important under 12 px, and big numbers on the phone. Section titles get stronger through size and weight, not the accent colour, because the accent means "selected".

Audit idea 9 · the phone

"Mind" becomes "Brain", in four groups

You decided on 2026-09-26 that the screen is called "Brain" in both apps. The phone still says "Mind" (BrainScreen.kt:242, HomeScreen.kt:1269). It is also one long scroll of about 30 sections. Proposed: rename it, and put the sections under four headings: Now, Memory, Model and PC, Settings. Nothing is removed. Another agent may already be renaming it, so check first.

What these pictures cannot tell you

  • Nobody has run the phone app for this audit, and there are no phone screenshots in the repo. The phone panels are drawn from reading the code, so spacing and some shapes are approximate.
