      Wiki

      Put .md or .txt files in your vault's Jarvis Wiki/Sources folder, and the model on the second graphics card (or the big model, if you have switched it on for the wiki) turns each one into linked pages in Jarvis Wiki/Pages. Each one asks you first, on one approval card. Nothing leaves this PC.

      Deep questions

      Ask one question you want a careful answer to. The big model answers it in the background on this PC, with no tools, no internet and no memory of your other conversations. It is slow: expect minutes, not seconds. Nothing leaves this PC, and the answer is kept on it for you to read here.

      Conversations

      Newest first. Opening one only reads it. Deleting one removes it from this PC and cannot be undone. There is no "delete everything": delete them one at a time, or choose above how long they are kept.

      Coming up

      Timers, alarms and reminders, kept on your PC. They go off on both apps while they are connected. Anything that repeats waits for your yes on an approval card.

      Just went off

      In the last hour. Snooze sets it to go off again in 10 minutes - a repeating one keeps its usual times.

      Tell me when

      Say or type "tell me when an email from Alex arrives" or "tell me when the washing machine finishes" - add "urgently" to make it ring until you look. Setting one up asks once with an approval card; when it happens, Jarvis only tells you.

      To-do list

      To start another list, say or type "add milk to the shopping list".

      Standby schedule

      Jarvis goes on standby at night and wakes in the morning - but it wakes only if the schedule put it on standby: if you chose Standby yourself, it stays on until you choose Active. Standby unloads its models and frees the graphics card; waking loads the chat model again, so the first answer is quick. Timers and reminders still go off. Setting it up asks once with an approval card.

      Your standby schedule is in the list above. Pause skips it and Delete turns it off. Neither wakes Jarvis - choose Active for that.

      Morning briefing

      A short list of your day, put together on your PC without the AI model. It only reads: it changes nothing and approves nothing.

      When it arrives each morning is in Settings, under Morning briefing.

      Long Fuse jobs

      Each job's capability set was frozen when it was created and cannot grow. A finished job never makes a sound — it adds a notch to the digest.

      Undo shelf

      What each entry was, never the stored bytes — the before-images stay on this machine. Irreversible actions are listed too, with the reason.

      Content risk

      Read-only, deliberately. Nothing here approves a tool description or clears a rush latch: approving means a person read the full current text, and that happens where the scanner runs.

      Ledger

      Chain status only — entry count, the last verified point, the anchors. Never the payloads.

      GitHub watchlist

      Pull, not push: nothing here is ever spoken. Licence is a column, and “none stated” means no permission — not “probably fine”.
