Most faces are hand-written Canvas 2D; Nucleus, Membrane, Tokamak, the four animals and the robot are drawn by the graphics card through WebGL 2, and fall back to Canvas 2D when WebGL is missing or the card cannot keep up. No three.js, nothing fetched at runtime. Speed ranges differ per face because nine of these integrate their state every frame and cannot simply be handed a faster clock; those are sub-stepped instead.
