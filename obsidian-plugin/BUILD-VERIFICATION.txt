HOW TO INSTALL INTO A TEST VAULT
--------------------------------
A "test vault" here means a throwaway folder that is NOT the owner's live vault.
Make one, point $vault at it, and unpack the zip into its plugins folder. This
writes nothing outside that folder:

  $vault="C:\path\to\a\throwaway\test\vault"; New-Item -ItemType Directory -Force (Join-Path $vault ".obsidian\plugins") | Out-Null; Expand-Archive -LiteralPath "C:\Users\pcadmin\Documents\Jarvis github\Epic-Jarvis-main\.dsh-scratch\OBSIDIAN-PLUGIN-LANE-2026-10-31\jarvis-obsidian-plugin-0.1.1.zip" -DestinationPath (Join-Path $vault ".obsidian\plugins") -Force; "Files landed in: " + (Join-Path $vault ".obsidian\plugins\jarvis-notes-bridge")

The files land in <$vault>\.obsidian\plugins\jarvis-notes-bridge\ (manifest.json,
main.js and styles.css at its root, plus the source, tests and docs). Launching
Obsidian and enabling the plugin is the next step and was NOT done here.

Do NOT do this to the live vault without the Lead's word. This lane was told not
to touch it, and it did not.
