Jarvis: an AI assistant that lives on your own PC, and asks before it acts. Every time.

On your phone, a risky yes needs your fingerprint. Android checks the fingerprint; Jarvis never sees it.
Ready for a second graphics card: a browser you can watch, doing only the steps you approved, on only the site you approved.
It asks before it remembers anything about you, and it keeps a history of what changed.
Say "stop" and it stops.
Your email, your files and what it knows about you stay on your PC. No subscription.

Will my PC run it? Today it needs an NVIDIA graphics card (the part that runs games) with 8 GB of memory. To check, press Ctrl+Shift+Esc, open Performance, then click GPU: the card's name is at the top right, and "Dedicated GPU memory" is its memory.
Is it always listening? No. "Hey Jarvis" is off until you switch it on, and the phone shows when it is listening.
One tap on Standby frees the graphics card when you want it back.
